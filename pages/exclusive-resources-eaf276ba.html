JTEST4YOU’S GRAMMAR EBOOKS

What’s special about JTest4You’s grammar ebooks:

  • Easy to carry around. Forget about heavy textbooks, you can put JTest4You’s grammar ebooks on your smartphone, tablet or computer to learn easily anywhere and everywhere, at your own convenience!
  • Easy to read on all devices. The text is designed big enough so that you can read the ebooks on any device, including small smartphones.
  • Clickable table of contents. There’s no need to search for the pages manually. Just click on the items on the table of contents and you’ll be brought straight to the grammar points.
  • Tons of examples. Each grammar point has about 10 to 60 example sentences, so there are a total of over 10.000 example sentences for you to learn!
  • Different versions for advanced learners. For JLPT N1 and JLPT N2 grammar ebooks, I made two different versions: version 1 without furigana and version 2 with furigana. If you’re an advanced learner who prefers not to read furigana to enhance your reading skills, these different versions will be perfect for you!
  • Printable. All our grammar ebooks are printable, so you can print any page or all the pages out to learn without electric devices.
  • Audio companion available. To help you improve your listening skills and pronunciation, I’ll also provide audio companion for the ebooks. Audio companion for JLPT N2 and JLPT N5 Grammar Ebooks are available so far.

JTEST4YOU’S VOCABULARY AUDIO LESSONS

What’s special about JTest4You’s vocabulary audio lessons:

  • Learn 25 words a day. Less is more! There’s no need to force yourself to learn by heart hundreds or thousands of words in a short period of time. You can learn 25 words a day fast and easily with these 35-minute audio lessons.
  • Learn two languages at once. Unlike the audio companion, which is entirely Japanese, these audio lessons contain example sentences read by native Japanese and English speakers. You can learn Japanese and English at the same time!
  • Lots of examples. Five to six example sentences are provided for each word, which should be more than enough for you to remember the usage of the words by the end of each lesson!
  • Transcript available. Transcript is available for every audio lesson! I strongly recommend that you look at the transcript only after you’ve finished listening to the audio.
