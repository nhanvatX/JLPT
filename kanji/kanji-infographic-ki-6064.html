Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 期 (ki)

September 5, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 期 (ki)]

MEANING:

period, time, date, term

HOW TO READ:

On: ki, go

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

期 (ki): period, time, age
時期 (jiki): time, season, period
期間 (kikan): period, term, interval
期待 (kitai): expectation, anticipation, hope
期限 (kigen): term, period, time limit, deadline
定期 (teiki): fixed term
任期 (ninki): term of office
早期 (souki): early stage
短期 (tanki): short term
雨期 (uki): rainy season
予期 (yoki): expectation, forecast
学期 (gakki): school term, semester
乾期 (kanki): dry season
周期 (shuuki): cycle, period
延期 (enki): postponement, adjournment
同期 (douki): same period, same class
後期 (kouki): latter period, final
期日 (kijitsu): fixed date, settlement date
今期 (konki): the present term
期末 (kimatsu): end of term
初期 (shoki): early (days), initial stage
末期 (makki): last stage, end stage
前期 (zenki): first term, preceding period
長期 (chouki): long time period, long term
次期 (jiki): next term, next period
中期 (chuuki): middle period
会期 (kaiki): session (of a legislature)
長期的 (choukiteki): long term
四半期 (shihanki): quarter (of a year)
上半期 (jouhanki): the first half-year
定期的 (teikiteki): periodic
短期間 (tankikan): short term, short time
一時期 (ichijiki): a period (of time)
新学期 (shingakki): new school term
適齢期 (tekireiki): marriageable age
周期的 (shuukiteki): periodic, cyclic
形成期 (keiseiki): formative period, formative year
無期限 (mukigen): indefinite
思春期 (shishunki): puberty
定期券 (teikiken): season ticket

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

わたしたちは、毎年同じ時期に畑に種をまく。
watashitachi wa, maitoshi onaji jiki ni hatake ni tane o maku.
We sow in the field at the same time every year.

ゲリラは、その後も定期的に襲撃訓練を繰り返した。
gerira wa, sonogo mo teikiteki ni shuugeki kunren o kurikaeshita.
Periodically the guerrillas would repeat the exercise.

株式相場のバブルには、ほかにも三種類の周期的な特徴がある。
kabushiki souba no baburu ni wa, hoka ni mo san shurui no shuukiteki na tokuchou ga aru.
Stock market bubbles have three other recurrent features.

私の母は、二歳から四歳にかけての形成期を、このような雰囲気のなかですごした。
watashi no haha wa, nisai kara yonsai ni kakete no keiseiki o, kono you na fun’iki no naka de sugoshita.
This was the atmosphere in which my mother lived the formative years from two to four.

戦争の末期でも、これほど大量の金を動かすことは疑いなく危険な行為だった。
sensou no makki demo, kore hodo tairyou no kin o ugokasu koto wa utagai naku kiken na koui datta.
Mobilizing such vast amounts of gold even at the tail end of a war was risky, no doubt.

それどころか、かなりの割合が変動金利型住宅ローンで、要するに、短期貸出金利の変動に応じて利率が代わりえるものだった。
sore dokoro ka, kanari no wariai ga hendou kinrigata juutaku roon de, you suru ni, tanki kashidashi kinri no hendou ni oujite riritsu ga kawarieru mono datta.
On the contrary, a high proportion were adjustable-rate mortgages – in other words, the interest rate could vary according to changes in short-term lending rates.
