Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 形 (kei)

August 30, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 形 (kei)]

MEANING:

shape, form, style

HOW TO READ:

On: kei, gyou
Kun: kata, -gata, katachi, nari

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

形 (katachi): form, shape, figure
新形 (shingata): new type, new model
形態 (keitai): form, shape, figure
人形 (ningyou): doll, puppet, figure
小形 (kogata): small size, tiny
形式 (keishiki): form, method, style
形成 (keisei): formation, molding
扇形 (senkei): fan shape
図形 (zukei): figure, shape
造形 (zoukei): molding, modeling
地形 (chikei): terrain, topography
円形 (enkei): round shape, circle
形状 (keijou): shape, form
花形 (hanagata): floral pattern, flourish
変形 (henkei): transformation, variety
形跡 (keiseki): traces, evidence
固形 (kokei): solid (body)
体形 (taikei): form, figure
奇形 (kikei): birth defect
原形 (genkei): original form
成型 (seikei): cast, mould
無形 (mukei): abstract, immaterial
形勢 (keisei): condition, situation
定形 (teikei): fixed form, regular shape
卵形 (rankei): oval, egg-shaped
形容 (keiyou): personal appearance, looks
大形 (oogata): large, large scale, big
髪形 (kamigata): hair style, hairdo
仕形 (shikata): way, method, means
手形 (tegata): draft, draught
形作る (katachizukuru): to form, to shape
自由形 (jiyuugata): freestyle
形式的 (keishikiteki): formal
三角形 (sankakukei): triangle
多角形 (takakkei): polygon
長方形 (chouhoukei): rectangle, oblong
形容詞 (keiyoushi): adjective
正方形 (seihoukei): square
整形外科 (seikeigeka): plastic surgery

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

今、そうしたもやもやが、明白な疑惑へと形を取り始めた。
ima, sou shita moyamoya ga, meihaku na giwaku e to katachi o torihajimeta.
Now those doubts were gelling into outright disbelief.

我々はセレモニー会場を後にして、長い長方形の部屋に集まった。
wareware wa seremonii kaijou o ato ni shite, nagai chouhoukei no heya ni atsumatta.
We left the ceremonial hall and crowded into a long rectangular room.

彼が成人を迎えた時代がその人格形成に一役買ったのは間違いない。
kare ga seijin o mukaeta jidai ga sono jinkaku keisei ni hitoyaku katta no wa machigai nai.
Certainly, the era in which he had come of age helped shape him.

多角形の大小無数の沼が、短い夏の日差しを反射して青や紫に染まる。
takakkei no daishou musuu no numa ga, mijikai natsu no hizashi o hansha shite ao ya murasaki ni somaru.
Countless polygonal ponds of varying sizes show blue and purple under the brief summer’s sun.

表現も日常的な言葉ではなく、古風な美辞がちりばめられ、非常に形式ばっていた。
hyougen mo nichijouteki na kotoba dewa naku, kofuu na biji ga chiribamerare, hijou ni keishiki batte ita.
He did not speak in colloquial Japanese, but in a highly formal language studded with ornamental classical phrases.

今後の世界経済は、各国の財務大臣の長ったらしい討議ではなく、ジッピーの自然発生的なエネルギーの爆発によって形作られるようになる。
kongo no sekai keizai wa, kakkoku no zaimu daijin no nagatarashii tougi dewa naku, jippii no shizen hasseiteki na enerugii no bakuhatsu ni yotte katachizukurareru you ni naru.
The global economy from here forward will be shaped less by the ponderous deliberations of finance ministers and more by the spontaneousexplosion of energy from the zippies.
