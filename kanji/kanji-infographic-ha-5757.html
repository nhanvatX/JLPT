Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 破 (ha)

August 26, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 破 (ha)]

MEANING:

rend, rip, tear, break, destroy, defeat, frustrate

HOW TO READ:

On: ha
Kun: yabu.ru, yabu.reru, wa.reru

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

破り (yaburi): escaping, defying
破る (yaburu): to tear, to violate
打破 (daha): break-down, defeat
破損 (hason): damage
破棄 (haki): annulment, revocation
破壊 (hakai): destruction, disruption
破片 (hahen): fragment, splinter
破裂 (haretsu): explosion, rupture
破滅 (hametsu): ruin, destruction
破格 (hakaku): special, exceptional
破綻 (hatan): failure, bankruptcy
大破 (taiha): serious damage, drubbing
撃破 (gekiha): crushing
爆破 (bakuha): destructive blast, explosion
突破 (toppa): breaking through, penetration
破産 (hasan): bankruptcy, insolvency
破局 (hakyoku): catastrophe, breakup
破毀 (haki): disposal
破摧 (hasai): crushing, smashing
読破 (dokuha): finish reading a book
看破 (kanpa): penetration, see through
破れる (yabureru): to get torn, to tear
破壊的 (hakaiteki): destructive, devastating
突き破る (tsukiyaburu): to break through, to penetrate

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

私は、破産して平気でいられるほど裕福ではない。
watashi wa, hasan shite heiki de irareru hodo yuufuku dewa nai.
I am not rich enough to ruin myself.

つまり戦争は、彼女の人生を永久に破壊していたのであった。
tsumari sensou wa, kanojo no jinsei o eikyuu ni hakai shite ita no de atta.
The war had, after all, permanently shattered her life.

ガラスや金属の破片が飛んできて彼の目や耳や喉を切った。
garasu ya kinzoku no hahen ga tonde kite kare no me ya mimi ya nodo o kitta.
Flying glass and shards of metal slashed his eyes and ears and throat.

１９９７年から２０００年にかけて、保険の大手三社が破綻した。
Sen kyuuhyaku kyuujuu nana nen kara nisen nen ni kakete, hoken no oote sansha ga hatan shita.
Three major insurers failed between 1997 and 2000.

ですから、遺言状のような部厚い書類を破毀する方法がなかったのです。
desu kara, yuigonjou no you na buatsui shorui o haki suru houhou ga nakatta no desu.
There was therefore no means of destroying a thick document such as a will.

翻訳サービスを充分に使わせてもらった。パートナーが発破をやるのに作業員を二人手配した。
hon’yaku saabisu o juubun ni tsukawasete moratta. paatonaa ga happa o yaru no ni sagyouin o futari tehai shita.
I am completely delighted with the translation service. My partner arranged for two laborers to come and help with some demolition.
