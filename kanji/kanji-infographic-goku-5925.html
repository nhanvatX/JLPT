Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 獄 (goku)

August 30, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 獄 (goku)]

MEANING:

prison, jail

HOW TO READ:

On: goku

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

地獄 (jigoku): hell
獄中 (gokuchuu): while in jail
監獄 (kangoku): prison
牢獄 (rougoku): jail, prison
投獄 (tougoku): imprisonment
獄道 (gokudou): underworld, evil
刑獄 (keigoku): jail, gaol
獄衣 (gokui): prison uniform
破獄 (hagoku): jail-breaking
獄死 (gokushi): death in jail
獄舎 (gokusha): prison
入獄 (nyuugoku): imprisonment
煉獄 (rengoku): purgatory
脱獄 (datsugoku): prison breaking, jailbreak
典獄 (tengoku): warden
獄吏 (gokuri): jailer, gaoler
禁獄 (kingoku): imprisonment
出獄 (shutsugoku): release (from prison)
疑獄 (gigoku): scandal, graft case
下獄 (gegoku): being sent to prison
脱獄囚 (datsugokushuu): escaped prisoner
仮出獄 (karishutsugoku): parole, release on bail
生き地獄 (ikijigoku): hell on earth
焦熱地獄 (shounetsujigoku): burning hell, inferno

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

あなたの考えでは、この地獄の図はわたしに何かを探せと暗示していると？
anata no kangae dewa, kono jigoku no zu wa watashi ni nanika o sagase to anji shite iru to.
Tell me, do you believe this map of hell is suggesting I seek something?

数年前には、徴兵反対の科で入獄させられる危険にさらされていた。
suunenmae ni wa, chouhei hantai no ka de nyuugoku saserareru kiken ni sarasarete ita.
Several years earlier, I had been in danger of going to prison for draft resistance.

この計画によって、何千というヴェトナム人の死者と入獄者が出た。
kono keikaku ni yotte, nanzen to iu vetonamujin no shisha to nyuugokusha ga deta.
The program had resulted in the death or imprisonment of tens of thousands of Vietnamese.

それらの罪は、煉獄の各層をのぼることによってのみ償うことができます。
sorera no tsumi wa, rengoku no kakusou o noboru koto ni yotte nomi tsugunau koto ga dekimasu.
Only by ascending through each level of purgatory can you atone for your sins.

彼は自宅軟禁を命じられたが、彼を敵する者たちはバスチー監獄に投獄しろと息巻いた。
kare wa jitaku nankin o meijirareta ga, kare o teki suru monotachi wa basuchii kangoku ni tougoku shiro to ikimaita.
He was placed under house arrest; his enemies wanted to see him in the Bastille.

巨大な牢獄国家北朝鮮で、コンピュータ専門家や脳外科医がほんとうにわずかな給料しかもらっていないのを考えてみるがいい！
kyodai na rougoku kokka kitachousen de, konpyuuta senmonka ya nougekai ga hontou ni wazuka na kyuuryou shika moratte inai no o kangaete miru ga ii.
Imagine how little a North Korean computer expert or brain surgeon is paid inside that huge prison of a nation!
