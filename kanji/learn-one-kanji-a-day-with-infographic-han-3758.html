Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 反 (han)

October 22, 2016   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 反 (han)]

MEANING:

anti-

HOW TO READ:

On: han, hon, tan, ho
Kun: so.ru, so.rasu, kae.su, kae.ru, -kae.ru

WORDS:

返る (kaeru): to come back
返す (kaesu): to return (something)
反応 (hannou): reaction, response
反抗 (hankou): resistance, defiance
反対 (hantai): opposition, contrast
反響 (hankyou): echo, reverberation
反射 (hansha): reflex, reflection
反感 (hankan): antipathy, revolt
反論 (hanron): objection, rebuttal
反動 (handou): reaction, recoil
反撃 (hangeki): counterattack
反面 (hanmen): opposite side
違反 (ihan): violation, offense
造反 (zouhan): rebellion
反転 (hanten): rolling over
反乱 (hanran): rebellion, uprising
反駁 (hanbaku): refutation, retort
反省 (hansei): reflection, remorse
反証 (hanshou): proof to the contrary
反発 (hanpatsu): opposition, revolt
反する (hansuru): to be contrary to
反対論 (hantairon): opposing argument
反対側 (hantaigawa): opposite side
反射的 (hanshateki): reflecting
反抗的 (hankouteki): rebellious, defiant
相反する (aihansuru): to conflict

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

あなたに反対したんじゃありません。
anata ni hantai shitan ja arimasen.
I was not contradicting you.

木村は反対側のドアから出て行った。
kimura wa hantaigawa no doa kara dete itta.
Kimura vanished through the doors at the other end.

彼が、私の名前にもなんの反応も示さなかった。
kare ga, watashi no namae ni mo nan no hannou mo shimesanakatta.
He also showed no reaction to my name.

南阿の反乱の時に使われた新しい暗号なんだ。
nan’a no hanran no toki ni tsukawareta atarashii angou nan da.
It’s a new code that was used in the South African rebellion.

そりゃ、ひどく妙だね。自然の法則に反しているようじゃないか。
sorya, hidoku myou da ne. shizen no housoku ni hanshite iru you janai ka.
That’s weird. It seems against nature.
