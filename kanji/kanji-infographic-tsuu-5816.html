Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 通 (tsuu)

August 28, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 通 (tsuu)]

MEANING:

traffic, pass through, avenue, commute, counter for letters, notes, documents, etc.

HOW TO READ:

On: tsuu, tsu
Kun: too.ru, too.ri, -too.ri, -doo.ri, too.su, too.shi, -doo.shi, kayo.u

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

通り (toori): street, avenue
通い (kayoi): commuting
通す (toosu): to let pass
通る (tooru): to go past
通算 (tsuusan): total
通貨 (tsuuka): currency
通路 (tsuuro): passage, pathway
通商 (tsuushou): commerce, trade
通信 (tsuushin): correspondence, communication
交通 (koutsuu): traffic, transportation
通常 (tsuujou): common, general
通報 (tsuuhou): report, bulletin
普通 (futsuu): general, ordinary
通称 (tsuushou): nickname, alias
通知 (tsuuchi): notice, notification
通過 (tsuuka): passage through, transit
通帳 (tsuuchou): passbook, bankbook
通話 (tsuuwa): telephone call, calling
通勤 (tsuukin): commuting to work
通告 (tsuukoku): announcement, notice
通達 (tsuutatsu): notification, directive
開通 (kaitsuu): opening, taking effect
通学 (tsuugaku): commuting to school
通行 (tsuukou): passage, passing
通訳 (tsuuyaku): interpretation
共通 (kyoutsuu): commonness, community
流通 (ryuutsuu): circulation of money or goods, distribution
見通し (mitooshi): forecast, outlook, prospect
交通費 (koutsuuhi): traveling expenses
文字通り (mojidoori): literally
交通事故 (koutsuujiko): traffic accident

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

あなたの場合は、普通の場合とは違います。
anata no baai wa, futsuu no baai towa chigaimasu.
Your case is not an ordinary one.

彼女は彼に、最後通告を送った。家賃を払うか、さもなければ退去するようにと。
kanojo wa kare ni, saigo tsuukoku o okutta. yachin o harau ka, samona kereba taikyo suru you ni to.
She gave him an ultimatum: pay the rent or leave.

彼はわたしの過去の任務記録に目を通し、注意深く検討した。
kare wa watashi no kako no ninmu kiroku ni me o tooshi, chuui bukaku kentou shita.
He read my record of previous assignments carefully.

ご婦人が一人、通路の向うの端のお手洗いに行かれたと思います。
gofujin ga hitori, tsuuro no mukou no hashi no o tearai ni ikareta to omoimasu.
One of the ladies went to the toilet at the far end, I think.

空には太陽が輝いていて、彼らの通常のデートの時間帯ではなかった。
sora ni wa taiyou ga kagayaite ite, karera no tsuujou no deeto no jikantai dewa nakatta.
It was morning, the sun high in the sky, not the time of day that they normally met.

模範的首都でさえその一部では、夜、大通りの真ん中を歩くと両側のビルが見えなかったりする。
mohanteki shuto de sae sono ichibu dewa, yoru, oodoori no mannaka o aruku to ryousoku no biru ga mienakattari suru.
Even in parts of the showcase capital, you can stroll down the middle of a main street at night without being able to see the buildings on either side.
