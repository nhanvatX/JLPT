Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 格 (kaku)

September 7, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 格 (kaku)]

MEANING:

status, rank, capacity, character, case (law, grammar)

HOW TO READ:

On: kaku, kou, kyaku, gou

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

格 (kaku): status, rank, method, rule
価格 (kakaku): price, value, cost
格差 (kakusa): qualitative difference, disparity
資格 (shikaku): qualifications, requirements
性格 (seikaku): character, personality, nature
規格 (kikaku): standard, norm
格好 (kakkou): shape, form, posture
合格 (goukaku): success, passing (exam), eligibility
格闘 (kakutou): hand-to-hand fighting
人格 (jinkaku): personality, character
厳格 (genkaku): severe, rigid, strictness
格安 (kakuyasu): cheap, reasonable
体格 (taikaku): physique, constitution
格段 (kakudan): special, exceptional, remarkable
格子 (koushi): lattice, grid pattern
失格 (shikkaku): disqualification, elimination
降格 (koukaku): demotion
格別 (kakubetsu): exceptional
適格 (tekikaku): eligible, qualified
風格 (fuukaku): personality, style
品格 (hinkaku): dignity, quality of character
破格 (hakaku): extraordinary, special
骨格 (kokkaku): skeleton, physique
昇格 (shoukaku): raising of status
本格 (honkaku): original method or procedure
本格化 (honkakuka): regularization, getting up speed
本格的 (honkakuteki): genuine, real, regular, normal, typical
低価格 (teikakaku): low price
人格者 (jinkakusha): man of character, person
無資格 (mushikaku): unqualified, unlicensed
不合格 (fugoukaku): failure, rejection, disqualification
規格品 (kikakuhin): standardized article or goods

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

ほぼすべてのものが画一的な規格品だった。
hobo subete no mono ga kakuitsuteki na kikakuhin datta.
Everything was pretty much standard issue.

籠の格子のすきまから野獣が外をのぞいている。
kago no koushi no suki ma kara yajuu ga soto o nozoite iru.
Through the bars, the wild animal looks out.

戦時中に高騰した金の価格が暴落することは、目にみえていた。
senjichuu ni koutou shita kin no kakaku ga bouraku suru koto wa, me ni miete ita.
The price of gold, which had soared during the war, would be bound to fall.

彼が示す猫との類似性は、肉づきがよく滑らかな体と狡猾な性格である。
kare ga shimesu neko to no ruijisei wa, nikuzuki ga yoku nameraka na karada to koukatsu na seikaku de aru.
What resemblance he bears to a cat is a certain plump sleekness of body and craftiness of character.

彼は、「私を愛さないものは生きる資格がない！」と言ったこともある。
kare wa, watashi o aisanai mono wa ikiru shikaku ga nai to itta koto mo aru.
“Those who do not love me do not deserve to live!” he once said.

不景気に陥ったデトロイトでは、住宅の下落は２００５年１２月には早くも始まっていた。２００７年７月に私がこの町を訪れたころ、住宅価格はすでに１０パーセントあまりも落ち込んでいた。
fukeiki ni ochiitta detoroito dewa, juutaku no geraku wa nisen go nen juunigatsu ni wa hayaku mo hajimatte ita. nisen nana nen shichigatsu ni watashi ga kono machi o otozureta koro, juutaku kakaku wa sude ni juppaasento amari mo ochikonde ita.
In depressed Detroit, the housing slide started earlier, in December 2005, and had already dragged house prices down by more than ten percent when I visited the city in July 2007.
