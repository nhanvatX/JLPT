Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 品 (hin)

September 11, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary


[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 品 (hin)]

MEANING:

goods, refinement, dignity, article, counter for meal courses

HOW TO READ:

On: hin, hon
Kun: shina

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

品 (shina): article, item, goods, stock
作品 (sakuhin): work, performance, production
商品 (shouhin): commodity, goods, stock
製品 (seihin): finished goods, manufactured goods, product
用品 (youhin): articles, supplies, parts
食品 (shokuhin): commodity, foodstuff
部品 (buhin): parts, accessories, components
品目 (hinmoku): item, list of articles
品種 (hinshu): kind of goods
薬品 (yakuhin): medicine, chemicals
景品 (keihin): freebie, giveaway, gift
出品 (shuppin): exhibit, display
品物 (shinamono): goods, article, thing
品質 (hinshitsu): (material) quality
賞品 (shouhin): prize, trophy
物品 (buppin): goods, articles
産品 (sanpin): products
上品 (jouhin): elegant, refined
備品 (bihin): fixtures, furnishings
手品 (tejina): magic, conjuring trick
新品 (shinpin): new article
金品 (kinpin): money and goods
下品 (gehin): vulgarity, meanness
品々 (shinajina): various articles
品性 (hinsei): character
返品 (henpin): returned goods
品位 (hin’i): grace, dignity, nobility
気品 (kihin): elegance, refinement
品格 (hinkaku): dignity, quality of character
遺品 (ihin): articles of the deceased
品切れ (shinagire): out of stick
医薬品 (iyakuhin): medical supplies
食料品 (shokuryouhin): foodstuff, groceries
必需品 (hitsujuhin): necessities, essentials
乳製品 (nyuuseihin): dairy products
日用品 (nichiyouhin): daily necessities
貴重品 (kichouhin): valuables, treasures
衣料品 (iryouhin): clothing goods
商品券 (shouhinken): gift certificate
中古品 (chuukohin): secondhand goods
高品質 (kouhinshitsu): high quality
遺留品 (iryuuhin): lost articles
健康食品 (kenkoushokuhin): health foods
目玉商品 (medamashouhin): bargain goods, featured product
食料品店 (shokuryouhinten): grocer, procery store
電気製品 (denkiseihin): electronic goods

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

下書きなどという贅沢は問題外、紙は貴重品だった。
shitagaki nado to iu zeitaku wa mondaigai, kami wa kichouhin datta.
Rough drafts were out of the question; paper was too precious.

初期のある作品には、次のような説明がついている。
shoki no aru sakuhin ni wa, tsugi no you na setsumei ga tsuite iru.
One early example of the genre bears the following inscription.

そのため食糧輸送が困難であり、工業化後は製品輸送に支障を来たす。
sono tame shokuryou yusou ga konnan de ari, kougyoukago wa seihin yusou ni shishou o kitasu.
It is therefore difficult to transport food—and after industrialization, difficult to move manufactured goods.

彼の挙措ふるまいは以前より上品になるどころか、妙にずうずうしくさえなっていた。
kare no kyoso furumai wa izen yori jouhin ni naru dokoro ka, myou ni zuuzuushiku sae natte ita.
He behaved not exactly with more dignity but with more effrontery.

この男は、暗い通りで若い男性を呼びとめ、物品を奪おうとした。すると意外にもそれは非番の警官であった。
kono otoko wa, kurai toori de wakai dansei o yobitome, buppin o ubaou to shita. suru to igai ni mo sore wa hiban no keikan de atta.
The man went on to describe how he accosted a young man on a dark street, intending to rob him, only to discover that he was assaulting an off-duty policeman.

また、貧困層は収入のかなりの部分を食糧や日用品に費やすので、ウォルマートの「毎日が安売り」はそういった人々にとってたいへん貴重なのだ。
mata, hinkonsou wa shuunyuu no kanari no bubun o shokuryou ya nichiyouhin ni tsuiyasu node, uorumaato no mainichi ga yasuuri wa sou itta hitobito ni totte taihen kichou na no da.
Moreover, Wal-Mart’s ‘every day low prices’ make the biggest difference to the poor, since they spend a higher proportion of income on food and other basics.

彼が売っているのはほとんどがコモディティ化された商品だから、大量に扱えば薄い利幅でもどうにかなる。
He was selling mostly commodity items so that, given his volumes, he could handle the slim profit margin.
kare ga utte iru no wa hotondo ga komoditika sareta shouhin dakara, tairyou ni atsukaeba usui rihaba demo dounika naru.

解読者が長靴のすぐれた品質について絶賛し、驚異的な生産統計値をすらすらと読み上げた。
The narrator raved about the superb quality of the boots and reeled off the impressive production statistics.
kaidokusha ga nagagutsu no sugureta hinshitsu ni tsuite zessan shi, kyouiteki na seisan toukeichi o surasura to yomiageta.

先祖代々から受け継いだ豪邸には、皿やワイン、陶磁器、美術品など格安で競り落とそうとして人々が殺到した。
Now my ancestral stately home was thrown open for throngs of bargain hunters to bid for the plate, the wine, the china and the works of art.
senzo daidai kara uketsuida goutei ni wa, sara ya wain, toujiki, bijutsuhin nado kakuyasu de seriotosou to shite hitobito ga sattou shita.

日本と中国は、他の東アジア諸国と同様、海洋貿易への依存度がきわめて高く、ペルシャ湾や環太平洋地域から原材料を輸入し、欧米に製品を輸出している。
Along with other East Asian economies, Japan and China are heavily dependent on maritime trade, shipping goods to the United States and Europe and importing raw material from the Persian Gulf and the rest of the Pacific Basin.
nihon to chuugoku wa, hoka no higashi ajia shokoku to douyou, kaiyou boueki e no izondo ga kiwamete takaku, perushawan ya kantai heiyou chiiki kara genzairyou o yunyuu shi, oubei ni seihin o yushutsu shite iru.
