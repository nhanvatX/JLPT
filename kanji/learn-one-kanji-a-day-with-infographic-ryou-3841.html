Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 量 (ryou)

November 15, 2016   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 量 (ryou)]

MEANING:

quantity, measure, weight, amount, consider, estimate, surmise

HOW TO READ:

On: ryou
Kun: haka.ru

HOW TO REMEMBER:

We’ll decide whether to hire you or not based on the amount of work you can do on your computer (里) until nightbreak (旦).

WORDS:

量 (ryou): quantity, amount, volume
量る (hakaru): to measure, to weigh
量り (hakari): measurements, weighing
量子 (ryoushi): quantum
重量 (juuryou): weight
多量 (taryou): large quantity
度量 (doryou): generosity, tolerance
裁量 (sairyou): admeasure, discretion
量販 (ryouhan): volume sales
軽量 (keiryou): light weight
推量 (suiryou): guess
力量 (rikiryou): ability, capacity
小量 (shouryou): small quantity
量産 (ryousan): mass production
大量 (tairyou): large quantity
分量 (bunryou): amount, quantity
数量 (suuryou): quantity, volume
測量 (sokuryou): measurement, surveying
減量 (genryou): loss in quantity
量感 (ryoukan): voluminous, massive
酌量 (shakuryou): consideration, pardon
消費量 (shouhiryou): amount of consumption

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

私はあなたの力量を信用します。
watashi wa anata no rikiryou o shinyou shimasu.
I believe in your ability.

写実的効果を出すには、一定の取捨裁量が必要なんだ。
shajitsuteki kouka o dasu ni wa, ittei no shusha sairyou ga hitsuyou nan da.
A certain selection and discretion must be used in producing a realistic effect.

いいえ、酌量はなりません。私は本当に腹がたちます。
iie, shakuryou wa narimasen. watashi wa hontou ni hara ga tachimasu.
I will make no allowance. I am very mad indeed.

彼は精神的にも、肉体的にもかなり多量の力をもつ人物だ。
kare wa seishinteki ni mo, nikutaiteki ni mo kanari taryou no chikara o motsu jinbutsu da.
He has a considerable amount of rude strength, both physically and mentally.

あなたの度量がそのからだのように大きく、精神がその顔のように立派だ。
anata no doryou ga sono karada no you ni ookiku, seishin ga sono kao no you ni rippana da.
Your heart is as big as your body, and your soul as fine as your face.
