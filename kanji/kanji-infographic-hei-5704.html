Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 兵 (hei)

August 25, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 兵 (hei)]

MEANING:

soldier, private, troops, army, warfare, strategy, tactics

HOW TO READ:

On: hei, hyou
Kun: tsuwamono

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

兵 (hei): soldier, army
兵士 (heishi): soldier
兵器 (heiki): arms, weapons
派兵 (hahei): dispatch of troops
兵員 (heiin): military personnel
兵隊 (heitai): soldier, sailor
歩兵 (hohei): infantry, food soldier
兵力 (heiryoku): military force, strength of an army
海兵 (kaihei): sailor, marine
兵役 (heieki): military service, conscription
徴兵 (chouhei): recruitment, enlistment
将兵 (shouhei): officers and men
騎兵 (kihei): cavalry
新兵 (shinpei): new recruit, conscript
核兵器 (kakuheiki): nuclear weapons
海兵隊 (kaiheitai): Marine Corps
化学兵器 (kagakuheiki): chemical weapons

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

この人々は、行方不明の兵隊の親戚たちであった。
kono hitobito wa, yukue fumei no heitai no shinsekitachi de atta.
They were relatives of still-missing soldiers.

戦争の一年目を通じ、兵が脱走する比率は驚くほど高かった。
sensou no ichinenme o tsuuji, hei ga dassou suru hiritsu wa odoroku hodo takakatta.
The level of desertion during that first year of the war was staggering.

中国軍は道の両側に強力な兵力を配置し、一行を待ち伏せていた。
chuugokugun wa michi no ryougawa ni kyouryoku na heiryoku o haichi shi, ikkou o machibusete ita.
The Chinese had set up a formidable force on both sides of the road, waiting to ambush them.

兵士と同様、大学生は学外にあっても制服を着用することになっていた。
heishi to douyou, daigakusei wa gakugai ni atte mo seifuku o chakuyou suru koto ni natte ita.
Like soldiers, university students were supposed to wear their uniforms even when they were off campus.

軍艦を建造するだけでなく、十分な訓練と経験を積んだ海兵隊を組織しなくてはならない。
gunkan o kenzou suru dake de naku, juubun na kunren to keiken o tsunda kaiheitai o soshiki shinakute wa naranai.
You have build ships as well as create well-trained and experienced sailors.

もし国際テロが増え続け、さらに多くの人命を奪うようになったらどうなるのか。アルカイダは、いまも大量破壊兵器を入手したいと工作している。
moshi kokusai tero ga fuetsuzuke, sarani ooku no jinmei o ubau you ni nattara dou naru no ka. arukaida wa, ima mo tairyou hakai heiki o nyuushu shitai to kousaku shite iru.
What if international terrorism strikes more frequently and lethally, as Al Qaeda continues its quest for weapons of mass destruction?
