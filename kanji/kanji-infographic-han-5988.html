Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 半 (han)

September 4, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 半 (han)]

MEANING:

half, middle, odd number, semi-, part-

HOW TO READ:

On: han
Kun: naka.ba

JLPT LEVEL: N5

COMPOUNDS:

半 (han): half, semi-
半ば (nakaba): middle, half, semi
半分 (hanbun): half
半値 (hanne): half price
前半 (zenhan): first half
大半 (taihan): majority, mostly
半数 (hansuu): half the number
半年 (hantoshi): half year
半面 (hanmen): one side, half
半島 (hantou): peninsula
夜半 (yahan): midnight, dead of night
半期 (hanki): half-term
半端 (hanpa): remnant, fragment, fraction
半額 (hangaku): half amount
半月 (hangetsu): half month
半減 (hangen): reduction by half
半生 (hansei): half a lifetime
半々 (hanhan): fifty-fifty
半日 (hannichi): half day
時半 (jihan): about an hour, short time
後半 (kouhan): second half, latter half
過半数 (kahansuu): majority
四半期 (shihanki): quarter (of a year)
下半身 (kahanshin): lower half of body
半狂乱 (hankyouran): half-crazed, frantic
上半身 (jouhanshin): upper half of body
朝鮮半島 (chousenhantou): Korean peninsula
中途半端 (chuutohanpa): halfway, unfinished
半信半疑 (hanshinhangi): half in doubt, dubious, half-convinced

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

このころには、事件を聞きつけて半狂乱になった母も駆けつけていた。
kono koro ni wa, jiken o kikitsukete hankyouran ni natta haha mo kaketsukete ita.
By now my mother had arrived after hearing what happened, frantic with worry.

徴兵カードの半分を父への贈り物の印として柩の上に置くことにしよう。
chouhei kaado no hanbun o chichi e no okurimono no shirushi toshite hitsugi no ue ni oku koto ni shiyou.
I will leave half of my draft card on the coffin with my father as a token of the gift.

今日の子供たちは２０代半ばまで学校に通い、それでもまだ一文の稼ぎもない有様だ。
kyou no kodomotachi wa nijuu dai nakaba made gakkou ni kayoi, sore demo mada ichimon no kasegi mo nai arisama da.
Today many children go to school until their mid-twenties and still have not earned a dime.

それから六年の間、イベリア半島に向けて人員や物資を輸送する必要がひんぱんに生じた。
sore kara rokunen no aida, iberia hantou ni mukete jin’in ya busshi o yusou suru hitsuyou ga hinpan ni shoujita.
For the better part of the next six years, there would be a recurrent need to get men and material to the Iberian Peninsula.

２０世紀前半に起こった紛争のほとんどが、ヨーロッパにおけるドイツの地位を争点としていた。
nijuu seiki zenhan ni okotta funsou no hotondo ga, yooroppa ni okeru doitsu no chii o souten to shite ita.
Most of the conflicts in the first half of the twentieth century were about Germany’s status in Europe.

アメリカの労働者の５７パーセントにはその贅沢が許されていない。じっさい、彼らの大半は給与を失うか有給休暇を使わないかぎり、子供の面倒をみるために休みをとることができない。
amerika no roudousha no gojuu nana paasento ni wa sono zeitaku ga yurusarete inai. jissai, karera no taihan wa kyuuyo o ushinau ka yuukyuu kyuuka o tsukawanai kagiri, kodomo no mendou o miru tame ni yasumi o toru koto ga dekinai.
Fifty-seven percent of American workers don’t have that luxury; indeed, most of them can’t take a day off to look after a child without losing pay or using vacation days.
