Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 細 (sai)

August 29, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 細 (sai)]

MEANING:

dainty, get thin, taper, slender, narrow, detailed, precise

HOW TO READ:

On: sai
Kun: hoso.i, hoso.ru, koma.ka, koma.kai

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

細か (komaka): small, fine, detailed
細い (hosoi): thin, slender
細め (hosome): thinnish
細かい (komakai): small, fine, minute
細かく (komakaku): minutely, finely
細胞 (saibou): cell (biology)
詳細 (shousai): detail, particulars
細菌 (saikin): bacterium, germ
細部 (saibu): details
細工 (saiku): work, tactics, trick
繊細 (sensai): delicate, fine, subtle
明細 (meisai): details, particulars
細心 (saishin): meticulous, careful, discreet
精細 (seisai): detail
些細 (sasai): trivial, slight
子細 (shisai): reasons, significance
細身 (hosomi): narrow, thin, slender
細目 (saimoku): particulars, details
微細 (bisai): minute, micro, detailed
細々 (komagoma): minutely, in detail
零細 (reisai): insignificant
細見 (saiken): close inspection, scrutiny
細密 (saimitsu): detailed knowledge
細やか (sasayaka): meagre, modest
細長い (hosonagai): long and narrow
心細い (kokorobosoi): helpless, hopeless
細胞内 (saibounai): inside the cell

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

概要を仕上げたから、詳細に取り掛かろう。
gaiyou o shiageta kara, shousai ni torikakarou.
Now that we’ve finished our overview, let’s get into the details.

まっすぐ通った鼻は、細めの顔にすこし大きすぎる。
massugu kayotta hana wa, hosome no kao ni sukoshi ookisugiru.
His straight-ribbed nose is too large for his narrow face.

彼は、手の中で引っくり返し引っくり返しして、仔細に調べた。
kare wa, te no naka de hikkurikaeshi hikkurikaeshi shite, shisai ni shirabeta.
He turned it over in his hands, examining it closely.

時計を細工したのは、車掌に化けた犯人とは別の人間なのです！
tokei o saiku shita no wa, shashou ni baketa hannin to wa betsu no ningen na no desu.
It was not the Wagon Lit murderer who tampered with the watch!

大人物に些細なことができるようになり、そのこともまたヒエラルキーを平らにする。これまでよりもずっと多くのことを、自分ひとりでできるようになったからだ。
daijinbutsu ni sasai na koto ga dekiru you ni nari, sono koto mo mata hierarukii o taira ni suru. kore made yori mo zutto ooku no koto o, jibun hitori de dekiru you ni natta kara da.
Hierarchies are also being leveled by big people being able to act really small—in the sense that they are enabled to do many more things on their own.

コストを下げるためには、建物を一から設計しなければならなかった。あらゆるサイズの機械に動力を供給できる小さな電動機を設置する安価な細長い平屋の工場が必要だった。
kosuto o sageru tame ni wa, tatemono o ichi kara sekkei shinakereba naranakatta. arayuru saizu no kikai ni douryoku o kyoukyuu dekiru chiisa na dendouki o secchi suru anka na hosonagai hiraya no koujou ga hitsuyou datta.
To get all the savings, you needed to redesign enough buildings. You needed to have long, low, cheaper-to-build single-story factories, with small electric motors powering machines of all sizes.
