Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 優 (yuu)

September 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary


[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 優 (yuu)]

MEANING:

tenderness, excel, surpass, actor, superiority, gentleness

HOW TO READ:

On: yuu, u
Kun: yasa.shii, sugu.reru, masa.ru

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

優 (yasa): gentle, affectionate
優る (masaru): to excel, to surpass, to exceed
女優 (joyuu): actress
優勝 (yuushou): overall victory, championship
優遇 (yuuguu): favorable treatment, warm reception
優利 (yuuri): advantageous, better, profitable
優雅 (yuuga): elegance, grace, refinement
優秀 (yuushuu): superiority, excellence
優位 (yuui): predominance, superiority
男優 (dan’yuu): actor
優越 (yuuetsu): supremacy, being superior to
優良 (yuuryou): superior, excellent, fine
優勢 (yuusei): superiority, superior power
優先 (yuusen): preference, priority
優退 (yuutai): bowing out, retiring voluntarily
優待 (yuutai): hospitality, warm reception
優劣 (yuurestu): merits, superiority and inferiority
優美 (yuubi): grace, refinement, elegance
名優 (meiyuu): great or famous actor, star
俳優 (haiyuu): actor, actress, performer
優しい (yasashii): tender, kind, gentle
優れる (sugureru): to surpass, to excel
最優遇 (saiyuuguu): most favorable treatment
優等生 (yuutousei): honor student
優先的 (yuusenteki): preferential
優越感 (yuuetsukan): superiority complex, sense of superiority
優先権 (yuusenken): preferential right

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

彼の燃えるような怒りは、優しい言葉ではなだめられなかった。
kare no moeru you na ikari wa, yasashii kotoba dewa nadamerarenakatta.
His burning anger could not be calmed with kind words.

優れた能力を持つ公務員にはそれ相応の待遇を与えるべきだ。
sugureta nouryoku o motsu koumuin ni wa sore souou no taiguu o ataeru beki da.
We should reward outstanding ability within the civil service appropriately.

彼は成績優秀卒業者の伝統的な途として工兵軍団の任務を選んだ。
kare wa seiseki yuushuu sotsugyousha no dentouteki na to to shite kouhei gundan no ninmu o eranda.
He had chosen the traditional route for a graduate of academic stature, a commission in the Corps of Engineers.

有名な女優が出演するその映画は大ヒットするだろうと期待されていた。
yuumei na joyuu ga shutsuen suru sono eiga wa dai hitto suru darou to kitai sarete ita.
The movie was expected to draw big crowds because it starred a popular actress.

蝋燭の光を浴びて、磨きぬかれた四つのなめらかな面が優美な輝きを放っている。
rousoku no hikari o abite, migakinukareta yotsu no nameraka na men ga yuubi na kagayaki o hanatte iru.
The surface is elegant and smooth with four polished sides that shines in the candlelight.

だがこの問題も、異例の補償制度、つまり報酬と売上実績を連動させ、純利より出来高を優先する、という方法を取り入れたため、部分的に修正できた。
da ga kono mondai mo, irei no hoshou seido, tsumari houshuu to uriage jisseki o rendou sase, junri yori dekidaka o yuusen suru, to iu houhou o toriireta tame, bubunteki ni shuusei dekita.
This, however, was partially countered by an unusual compensation system, which linked remuneration to investments and sales, putting a priority on turnover rather than net profits.

指揮官が大胆に指導する戦争の優等生で、なおかつ自分の命をも賭ける性向が備わっている場合には、彼は神秘性を獲得する。
When a leader is an accomplished student of war, leads boldly, and also savors gambling his own life, he acquires a mystique.
shikikan ga daitan ni shidou suru sensou no yuutousei de, naokatsu jibun no inochi o mo kakeru seikou ga sonawatte iru baai ni wa, kare wa shinpisei o kakutoku suru.

あれは単なる養護施設じゃない。優秀な子供が集められ、Ｌの後継になるものを育てるための施設って感じだ。
That’s no ordinary orphanage. It’s more of an institution where highly brilliant kids are raised to become the next L.
are wa tannaru yougo shisetsu janai. yuushuu na kodomo ga atsumerare, eru no koukei ni naru mono o sodateru tame no shisetsu tte kanji da.

ふたりが働いているあいだ娘の世話をしてくれる優秀な在宅ベビーシッターが見つかったが、突如としてフルタイムの従業員に支払いをしなければならなくなったため、家計は逼迫した。
We found a wonderful inhome babysitter to look after our daughter while we were at work, but with a full-time employee suddenly on our payroll, money got tight.
futari ga hataraite iru aida musume no sewa o shite kureru yuushuu na zaitaku bebiishittaa ga mitsukatta ga, totsujo toshite furutaimu no juugyouin ni shiharai o shinakereba naranaku natta tame, kakei wa hippaku shita.

彼女は、激しい力といったものを、落ち着いた態度の中にひそめていたが、それにもかかわらず、優雅な洗練された肉体に宿る、あらあらしい野性の精神といった印象をあたえるのだった――こういうことがみんな、わたしの記憶の中で、いまもなお燃えている。
The intense power of stillness she possessed, which nevertheless conveyed the impression of a wild untamed spirit in an exquisitely civilised body–all these things are burnt into my memory.
kanojo wa, hageshii chikara to itta mono o, ochitsuita taido no naka ni hisomete ita ga, sore ni mo kakawarazu, yuuga na senren sareta nikutai ni yadoru, araarashii yasei no seishin to itta inshou o ataeru no datta ―― kou iu koto ga minna, watashi no kioku no naka de, ima mo nao moete iru.
