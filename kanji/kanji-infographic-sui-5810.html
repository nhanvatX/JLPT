Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 推 (sui)

August 28, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 推 (sui)]

MEANING:

conjecture, infer, guess, suppose, support, push (for)

HOW TO READ:

On: sui
Kun: o.su

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

推す (osu): to infer, to support
推移 (suii): transition, change
推薦 (suisen): recommendation, referral
推定 (suitei): presumption, assumption
推計 (suikei): estimate, estimation
推量 (suiryou): guess
推力 (suiryoku): driving force
推察 (suisatsu): guess, conjecture
推理 (suiri): reasoning, inference
推測 (suisoku): guess, conjecture
推進 (suishin): driving force, implementation
推挙 (suikyo): recommendation
推考 (suikou): surmising, speculation
推論 (suiron): reasoning, deduction
推敲 (suikou): polish, improvement
推奨 (suishou): recommendation, endorsement
推算 (suisan): estimation
推服 (suifuku): admiration
推進者 (suishinsha): promoter, pioneer
推薦者 (suisensha): referee
推薦状 (suisenjou): letter of recommendation
推理小説 (suirishousetsu): detective novel

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

もっとも、死因は、いまでもお知らせできますがね。頭蓋骨骨折です。それに、凶器も推定できると思います。
motto mo, shiin wa, ima demo oshirase dekimasu ga ne. zugaikotsu kossetsu desu. sore ni, kyouki mo suitei dekiru to omoimasu.
I can tell you the cause of death now. Fracture of the base of the skull. And I think I can make a guess at the weapon.

多くの推論はあったものの、証拠はほとんどなかった。
ooku no suiron wa atta mono no, shouko wa hotondo nakatta.
There were many theories, but still little evidence.

正解を記さなかったために、わたしはいくつか推薦を失った。
seikai o shirusanakatta tame ni, watashi wa ikutsu ka suisen o ushinatta.
I lost some endorsements by not giving the right answer.

しかし、彼女が、どんな人物かを推測するのは困難ではありません。
shikashi, kanojo ga, donna jinbutsu ka o suisoku suru no wa konnan dewa arimasen.
But it should not be difficult to guess who she is.

他方、空襲による非戦闘員の死者については、いろいろな推計がある。
tahou, kuushuu ni yoru hi sentouin no shisha ni tsuite wa, iroiro na suikei ga aru.
On the other hand, estimates vary considerably where civilian deaths in air raids are concerned.

だが世界で最初に誕生した福祉超大国、もとよりこの原理を最大限に推し進めて大成功した国は、じつはイギリスではなく日本だった。
da ga sekai de saisho ni tanjou shita fukushi choutaikoku, motoyori kono genri o saidaigen ni oshisusumete daiseikou shita kuni wa, jitsu wa igirisu dewa naku nihon datta.
However, the world’s first welfare superpower, the country that took the principle furthest and with the greatest success, was not Britain but Japan.
