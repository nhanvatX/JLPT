Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 工 (kou)

September 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary


[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 工 (kou)]

MEANING:

craft, construction, katakana e radical (no. 48)

HOW TO READ:

On: kou, ku, gu

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

工場 (koujou): factory, plant, mill
工事 (kouji): construction work, construction
工業 (kougyou): (manufacturing) industry
商工 (shoukou): commerce and industry
工作 (kousaku): work, construction, handicraft
着工 (chakkou): start of (construction) work
加工 (kakou): manufacturing, processing
工夫 (kufuu): scheme, device, figuring out
人工 (jinkou): artificial, man-made, human work
工学 (kougaku): engineering
大工 (daiku): carpenter
工程 (koutei): manufacturing process, work schedule
細工 (saiku): work, craftsmanship, tactics
施工 (shikou): construction, constructing
工科 (kouka): engineering course
工房 (koubou): workshop, studio
工兵 (kouhei): combat engineer
工務 (koumu): engineering, engineering works
工員 (kouin): factory worker
工賃 (kouchin): wages, pay
木工 (mokkou): woodworking, woodworker
鉄工 (tekkou): ironworking, ironworker
起工 (kikou): setting to work
完工 (kankou): completion
工具 (kougu): tool, implement
工費 (kouhi): construction cost
工芸 (kougei): industrial arts, craft
工合 (guai): condition, state, health
工学部 (kougakubu): department of technology, engineering or science
人工的 (jinkouteki): artificial, unnatural
工作員 (kousakuin): spy, covert operative
重工業 (juukougyou): heavy industry
小細工 (kozaiku): tricks, artfulness
修理工 (shuurikou): repair man
工業国 (kougyoukoku): industrialized nation
人工呼吸 (jinkoukokyuu): artifical respiration

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

彼は、四年間見習いとして働いた後、大工になった。
kare wa, yo nenkan minarai toshite hataraita ato, daiku ni natta.
After hour years as an apprentice, he became a carpenter.

松田に会って話をすることを工員たちは求めた。彼は拒否した。工員たちはかれを非情だと思った。
matsuda ni atte hanashi o suru koto o kouintachi wa motometa. kare wa kyohi shita. kouintachi wa kare o hijou da to omotta.
The workers wanted to see Matsuda to discuss it with him. He said no. They thought he was heartless.

ＵＰＳの修理工はすべて東芝の審査を経ている。消費者からの苦情は激減した。
yuupiiesu no shuurikou wa subete toushiba no shinsa o hete iru. shouhisha kara no kujou wa gekigen shita.
The UPS repairmen and women are all certified by Toshiba, and its customer complaints have gone down dramatically.

カムシャフトは、高度の加工部品で、エンジンの性能を左右する。
kamushafuto wa, koudo no kakou buhin de, enjin no seinou o sayuu suru.
Camshafts are highly engineered products which are critical to the performance of the engine.

工作員たちは若かったが、多くは驚くほど優秀だと彼は考えていた。
kousakuintachi wa wakakatta ga, ooku wa odoroku hodo yuushuu da to kare wa kangaete ita.
The agents were young, but he thought a number of them were surprisingly good.

厳重に閉鎖されていた経済を中国が開放したとき、世界の工業国の企業は、そこが輸出先として素晴らしい新市場であることに着目した。
genjuu ni heisa sarete ita keizai o chuugoku ga kaihou shita toki, sekai no kougyoukoku no kigyou wa, soko ga yushutsusaki toshite subarashii shin ichiba de aru koto ni chakumoku shita.
When China first opened its tightly closed economy, companies in industrialized countries saw it as an incredible new market for exports.

それから５０年のあいだ、何十万人ものインド人がこの国立工業大学や私立の工業大学で学んできた。
In the fifty-five years since then, hundreds of thousands of Indians have competed to gain entry and then graduate from these public Institutes of Technology and their private-sector equivalents.
sore kara gojuu nen no aida, nanjuu mannin mo no indojin ga kono kokuritsu kougyou daigaku ya shiritsu no kougyou daigaku de manande kita.

幾重にも重なったニスやほこりが洗い落とされると、長年のあいだその下に隠れていた見事な寄木細工が姿を現したのである。
Once the layers of varnish and dirt had gone, we discovered some beautiful marquetry beneath that had not been seen for many years.
ukue ni mo kasanatta nisu ya hokori ga araiotosareru to, naganen no aida sono shita ni kakurete ita migoto na yosegizaiku ga sugata o arawashita no de aru.

彼は自分のスパイたちに、フランス植民地時代の「黒い胸」を模倣する目的のために供与される清算不要の資金から、工作費を支払っていた。
He paid his spies with nonaccountable funds he was given for the purpose in imitation of the “black chest” system of the French colonial army.
kare wa jibun no supaitachi ni, furansu shokuminchi jidai no kuroi mune o mohou suru mokuteki no tame ni kyouyo sareru seisan fuyou no shikin kara, kousakuhi o shiharatte ita.

肝心なのは、地球の隅々から工場や小売店に間に合うように配送する総コストを下げることだ。それも、競合他社よりも低いコストでなければならない。
The key is that the total cost of delivering all your parts on time from all four corners of the globe to your factories or retail outlets has to be low, and certainly lower than those of your competitors.
kanjin na no wa, chikyuu no sumizumi kara koujou ya kouriten ni maniau you ni haisou suru sou kosuto o sageru koto da. sore mo, kyougou tasha yori mo hikui kosuto denakereba naranai.
