Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 断 (dan)

September 7, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 断 (dan)]

MEANING:

severance, decline, refuse, apologize, warn, dismiss, prohibit, decision, judgement, cutting

HOW TO READ:

On: dan
Kun: ta.tsu, kotowa.ru, sada.meru

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

断る (kotowaru): to refuse, to reject
断つ (tatsu): to sever, to cut off
断り (kotomari): rejection, refusal
判断 (handan): judgement, decision, conclusion
断固 (danko): firm, determined, resolute
断続 (danzoku): intermittent
断念 (dannen): giving up, abandoning (hope, plans)
横断 (oudan): crossing, transverse
予断 (yodan): guessing, prediction
中断 (chuudan): interruption, suspension
無断 (mudan): without permission, without notice
油断 (yudan): negligence, unpreparedness
断層 (dansou): gap, discrepancy
断行 (dankou): decisive action, carry out
独断 (dokudan): one’s own judgement, dogmatic, arbitrary
不断 (fudan): constancy, persistency
断水 (dansui): water outage
断熱 (dannetsu): insulation
分断 (bundan): dividing into parts
断然 (danzen): firmly, absolutely
断絶 (danzetsu): extinction, discontinuation
断定 (dantei): conclusion, decision
切断 (setsudan): cutting, severance, amputation
寸断 (sundan): cut to pieces
縦断 (juudan): flying through, cutting across
断交 (dankou): severing relations
断食 (danjiki): fasting
即断 (sokudan): snap judgement
遮断 (shadan): isolation, cut off, blockade
断片 (danpen): fragment, shred, crumb, bits and pieces
裁断 (saidan): judgement, decision
決断 (ketsudan): decision, determination
診断 (shindan): diagnosis
断言 (dangen): assertion, affirmation
断じて (danjite): absolutely, positively
断らず (kotowarazu): without permission
断える (taeru): to die out, to cease
不断着 (fudangi): casual wear, ordinary clothes
断片的 (danpenteki): fragmentary
横断歩道 (oudanhodou): pedestrian crossing

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

断定するためには、ある実験をしなければならないのです。
dantei suru tame ni wa, aru jikken o shinakereba naranai no desu.
To find out definitely I shall have to make certain experiments.

われわれは、コストが上がりすぎてそのプロジェクトを断念した。
wareware wa, kosuto ga agarisugite sono purojekuto o dannen shita.
We abandoned the project because the cost rose too much.

その登山者の凍った足の指二本は、切断されなければならなかった。
sono tozansha no kootta ashi no yubi nihon wa, setsudan sarenakereba naranakatta.
Two of the mountain climber’s frozen toes had to be amputated.

道を遮断して第八連隊を師団残部から分断する作戦かもしれない。
michi o shadan shite daihachi rentai o shidan zanbu kara bundan suru sakusen kamoshirenai.
They might be thinking of cutting the road, severing the Eighth Regiment from the rest of the division.

彼は頼まれたことはけっして断らない人、友人をけっして裏切らない人として知られた人格者だった。
kare wa tanomareta koto wa kesshite kotowaranai hito, yuujin o kesshite uragiranai hito toshite shirareta jinkakusha datta.
He was well known as a kindly man who would never turn anyone away or betray a friend.

芸術の力はすばらしい。だが大恐慌によって二つに分断された社会をまとめるためには、芸術よりも強靭な力が明らかに必要だった。
geijutsu no chikara wa subarashii. da ga dai kyoukou ni yotte futatsu ni bundan sareta shakai o matomeru tame ni wa, geijutsu yori mo kyoujin na chikara ga akiraka ni hitsuyou datta.
The power of art is a wonderful thing. But clearly something more powerful than art was going to be needed to put together a society that had been split in two by the Depression.
