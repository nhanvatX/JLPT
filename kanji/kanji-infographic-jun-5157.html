Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 純 (jun)

June 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 純 (jun)]

MEANING:

genuine, purity, innocence, net (profit)

HOW TO READ:

On: jun

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

純 (jun): pure, genuine
不純 (fujun): impurity, dishonesty
純粋 (junsui): pure, true, genuine
単純 (tanjun): simplicity, simple
純愛 (jun’ai): pure love
純益 (jun’eki): clear profit, net income
純正 (junsei): genuine, pure, perfect
純化 (junka): purification
純情 (junjou): pure heart, innocence
純真 (junshin): purity, sincerity
純潔 (junketsu): purity, chastity
純金 (junkin): pure gold, fine gold
純朴 (junboku): honest, simple
純増 (junzou): net increase
純血 (junketsu): pure-blooded
至純 (shijun): absolute purity
清純 (seijun): purity, innocence
純然 (junzen): absolute, complete
純銀 (jungin): pure silver
不純物 (fujunbutsu): foreign matter, impurities
単純化 (tanjunka): simplification
純文学 (junbungaku): pure literature
単純性 (tanjunsei): simplicity

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

あなたの超純情もお兄さんそっくりだよね。
anata no choujunjou mo oniisan sokkuri da yo ne.
You’re just like your super naive brother.

家屋は単純な造りで実用を旨とし、色彩にとぼしい。
kaoku wa tanjun na tsukuri de jitsuyou o mune to shi, shikisai ni toboshii.
The houses are simple, utilitarian, and monochromatic.

彼女は純粋知性科学の本を出す出版社を探していた。覚えてるか？
kanojo wa junsui chisei kagaku no hon o dasu shuppansha o sagashite ita. oboeteru ka.
She was looking for a publisher for a book on Noetic Science. Do you remember her?

三浦は金と不純物と水銀の混ざったような人物で、状況を分析し、それへの政策を処方する仕事では誰よりも優れていた。
miura wa kin to fujunbutsu to suigin no mazatta you na jinbutsu de, joukyou o bunseki shi, sore e no seisaku o shohou suru shigoto dewa dare yori mo sugurete ita.
Miura is a mixture of gold, dross and mercury. No one was better at analyzing a situation and prescribing a policy to deal with it.

中国は、成長するにつれて国産資源では賄えなくなり、原材料の純輸入国に転じた。
chuugoku wa, seichou suru ni tsurete kokusan shigen dewa makanaenaku nari, genzairyou no jun’yunyuukoku ni tenjita.
As China has grown, it has outstripped its own resources and become a net importer of raw materials.

社会的保守派が正しかったことがひとつあるとしたら、それは、わが国の現在の文化が情緒と家計に主婦の果たす卓越した貢献——犠牲と単純労働——をきちんと評価しそこねてきた点だ。
shakaiteki hoshuha ga tadashikatta koto ga hitotsu aru to shitara, sore wa, wagakuni no genzai no bunka ga joucho to kakei ni shufu no hatasu takuetsu shita kouken —— gisei to tanjun roudou —— o kichinto hyouka shisokonete kita ten da.
If there’s one thing that social conservatives have been right about, it’s that our modern culture sometimes fails to fully appreciate the extraordinary emotional and financial contributions—the sacrifices and just plain hard work—of the stay-at-home mom.
