Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 質 (shitsu)

September 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary


[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 質 (shitsu)]

MEANING:

substance, quality, matter, temperament

HOW TO READ:

On: shitsu, shichi, shi
Kun: tachi, tada.su, moto, warifu

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

質 (shitsu): quality, value
実質 (jisshitsu): substance, essence
質問 (shitsumon): question, inquiry
物質 (busshitsu): material, substance
体質 (taishitsu): constitution (physical), predisposition
水質 (suishitsu): water quality
本質 (honshitsu): essence, true nature
人質 (hitojichi): hostage, prisoner
素質 (soshitsu): character, qualities
資質 (shishitsu): nature, disposition
質量 (shitsuryou): mass
品質 (hinshitsu): (material) quality
地質 (chishitsu): geological features
音質 (onshitsu): sound quality
材質 (zaishitsu): lumber quality
質素 (shisso): simplicity, modesty
特質 (tokushitsu): characteristic, feature
悪質 (akushitsu): bad quality, malignancy
異質 (ishitsu): different (quality, nature)
性質 (seishitsu): nature, property, disposition
変質 (henshitsu): deterioration, change in quality
上質 (joushitsu): fine quality
質屋 (shichiya): pawnshop
良質 (ryoushitsu): good quality, superior quality
質的 (shitsuteki): qualitative
気質 (kishitsu): spirit, character, trait
質疑 (shitsugi): question, interpellation
実質的 (jisshitsuteki): substantial
神経質 (shinkeishitsu): nervousness, sensitiveness
高品質 (kouhinshitsu): high quality
物質的 (busshitsuteki): material, physical
抗生物質 (kouseibusshitsu): antibiotic

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

異常体質といってもいい。彼にとって、並の人間の一日が二日に相当した。
ijou taishitsu to itte mo ii. kare ni totte, nami no ningen no ichinichi ga futsuka ni soutou shita.
His constitution was extraordinary. It permitted him to turn each day into two days for an ordinary man.

確信と懐疑の組み合わせは、本質的に不安定である。
kakushin to kaigi no kumiawase wa, honshitsuteki ni fuantei de aru.
The combination of belief and skepticism is inherently unstable.

まるで地質断層が大地震を引き起こしたかのようだった。
marude chishitsu dansou ga daijishin o hikiokoshita ka no you datta.
It was as if a geological fault had caused a massive earthquake.

それは、みんなが順々に、三度ずつ質問をし合うゲームです。
sore wa, minna ga junjun ni, sando zutsu shitsumon o shi au geemu desu.
It’s a game where everyone in turn is asked three questions.

がっしりした体格の開けっ広げで人好きのする山崎の様子は、本人の気質をよく表していた。
gasshiri shita taikaku no akeppiroge de hitozuki no suru yamazaki no yousu wa, honnin no kishitsu o yoku arawashite ita.
Yamazaki’s husky frame and open, pleasing features matched his temperament.

このインフラは、最適な場所から最高の品質を最良の価格で調達できるようにするだけでなく、実務と知識の大幅な共有を可能にします。
kono infura wa, saiteki na basho kara saikou no hinshitsu o sairyou no kakaku de choutatsu dekiru you ni suru dake denaku, jitsumu to chishiki no oohaba na kyouyuu o kanou ni shimasu.
This infrastructure is not only going to facilitate sourcing of work to the best price, best quality, from the best place, it is also going to enable a great amount of sharing of practices and knowledge.

新たに到着するＣＩＡ職員はラオスを実質的に準軍事的問題だと見なしていた。
The newly arriving CIA officers really looked at Laos as a paramilitary problem.
arata ni touchaku suru shiiaiei shokuin wa raosu o jisshitsuteki ni jun gunjiteki mondai da to minashite ita.

起業家の素質のあるマネジャーは、資本主義をひと口味わっただけで酔いしれてしまい、なんでもためそうとする。
Those managers who did have an entrepreneurial flair got drunk on their first sip of capitalism and were ready to try anything.
kigyouka no soshitsu no aru manejaa wa, shihon shugi o hitokuchi ajiwatta dake de yoishirete shimai, nandemo tamesou to suru.

この国の国民性と国民的体験の特殊性をもっとも偏愛しているのは、この国の文化本質主義者や新ナショナリストたちである。
No one makes more of a fetish of the supposed singularity of the national character and the national experience than the country’s own cultural essentialists and neonationalists.
kono kuni no kokuminsei to kokuminteki taiken no tokushusei o motto mo hen’ai shite iru no wa, kono kuni no bunka honshitsu shugisha ya shin nashonarisutotachi de aru.

キエフの閑静な住宅地では、ウクライナ版の疫病管理予防センターを見学した。高校の科学実験室のようなたたずまいの、質素な三階建ての施設だった。
In a quiet, residential neighborhood of Kiev, we received a tour of the Ukraine’s version of the Centers for Disease Control, a modest three-story facility that looked like a high school science lab.
kiefu no kansei na juutaku chi dewa, ukuraina ban no ekibyou kanri yobou sentaa o kengaku shita. koukou no kagaku jikkenshitsu no you na tatazumai no, shisso na sangaidate no shisetsu datta.
