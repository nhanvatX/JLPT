Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 免 (men)

August 29, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 免 (men)]

MEANING:

excuse, dismissal

HOW TO READ:

On: men
Kun: manuka.reru, manuga.reru

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

免許 (menkyo): license, permit, certificate
免除 (menjo): exemption, exoneration
免疫 (men’eki): immunity, immunization
免税 (menzei): tax exemption, duty exemption
免職 (menshoku): dismissal, sacking
御免 (gomen): declining (something)
免責 (menseki): exemption from responsibility, disclaimer
免罪 (menzai): acquittal, pardon
放免 (houmen): release (from custody), setting free
赦免 (shamen): remission
全免 (zenmen): complete exemption
罷免 (himen): dismissal, discharge
免状 (menjou): diploma, license
免役 (men’eki): release from office
特免 (tokumen): special exemption
減免 (genmen): reduction and exemption
免じる (menjiru): to dismiss, to exempt
免れる (manukareru): to escape from, to evade
免許証 (menkyoshou): license, permit
免疫性 (men’ekisei): immunity
免税品 (menzeihin): duty-free articles
免税店 (menzeiten): duty-free store
無免許 (mumenkyo): unlicensed, without a license
運転免許 (untenmenkyo): driver’s license
兵役免状 (heiekimenjou): draft exemption

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

いまでは、実際に徴兵されることは免じられている。
ima dewa, jissai ni chouhei sareru koto wa menjirarete iru.
I’m immune now from actually being drafted.

出荷日に市場価格が予想より低い場合でも、農家は損失を免れる。
shukkabi ni shijou kakaku ga yosou yori hikui baai demo, nouka wa sonshitsu o manukareru.
If the market price on the day of delivery is lower than expected, the farmer is protected.

最終的には政治警察もそれ以上の追及はやめ、彼を無罪放免にした。
saishuuteki ni wa seiji keisatsu mo sore ijou no tsuikyuu wa yame, kare o muzai houmen ni shita.
The political police in the end decided not to push the case and released him without charges.

身分証明書、免許証、申請書、政府の許可証など、写真が必要なことは多いんです。
mibun shoumeisho, menkyoshou, shinseisho, seifu no kyokashou nado, shashin ga hitsuyou na koto wa ooin desu.
We noticed that there was a big demand for having pictures taken for identification purposes, for licenses, for applications and government permits.

誰がキラなのか知らないが、僕はもうこの会議から抜けたい。犯罪者として捕まるのは御免だ。
dare ga kira na no ka shiranai ga, boku wa mouko no kaigi kara nuketai. hanzaisha to shite tsukamaru no wa gomen da.
I don’t know who Kira is but I want out of these meetings. I don’t want to go to jail.

会社の経営陣は政府に対して、１６１２年に予定されている１０年間の収支計算書を送る義務を免除してほしい、という請願を送り、政府はそれを許可した。
kaisha no keieijin wa seifu ni taishite, sen roppyaku juu ninen ni yotei sarete iru juu nenkan no shuushi keisansho o okuru gimu o menjo shite hoshii, to iu seigan o okuri, seifu wa sore o kyoka shita.
When the Company’s directors petitioned the government to be released from their obligation to publish ten-year accounts in 1612, permission was granted.
