Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 益 (eki)

August 2, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 益 (eki)]

MEANING:

benefit, gain, profit, advantage

HOW TO READ:

On: eki, yaku
Kun: ma.su

COMPOUNDS:

利益 (rieki): profit, gains
増益 (zoueki): increased profit
収益 (shuueki): earnings, proceeds, revenue
減益 (gen’eki): decrease in profits
差益 (saeki): marginal profits
国益 (kokueki): national interest
公益 (koueki): public, public good
損益 (son’eki): profit and loss
有益 (yuueki): beneficial, profitable
権益 (ken’eki): (one’s) interests
無益 (mueki): useless, futile, vain
実益 (jitsueki): actual profit, benefit
益金 (ekikin): profit
共益 (kyoueki): common profit
益々 (masumasu): increasingly, more and more
不利益 (furieki): disadvantage, handicap
共益費 (kyouekihi): condo fees
受益者 (juekisha): beneficiary
経常利益 (keijourieki): current profits, ordinary income
過剰利益 (kajourieki): excess profit
公益事業 (kouekijigyou): public utilities

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

現実には、人間は誰もが、自分の利益のために動いている。
genjitsu ni wa, ningen wa daremo ga, jibun no rieki no tame ni ugoite iru.
The truth is what all human beings are after is their own interest.

労働者はそれぞれ、出資金と投資収益を記録した新しい手帳を受け取った。
roudousha wa sorezore, shusshikin to toushi shuueki o kiroku shita atarashii techou o uketotta.
Each worker received a shiny new book in which the contributions and investment returns were recorded.

私は何がイギリスの国益かの判断では厳しく臨み、国益を断固として守るつもりです。
watashi wa nani ga igirisu no kokueki ka no handan dewa kibishiku nozomi, kokueki o danko to shite mamoru tsumori desu.
I intend to be very discriminating in judging what are British interests and I shall be resolute in defending them.

人類の問題が私の商売だったのだ。公益が商売のはずだったんだ。慈善、あわれみ、寛大、慈悲、これがみんな私の商売だったはずだ。
jinrui no mondai ga watashi no shoubai datta no da. koueki ga shoubai no hazu dattan da. jizen, awaremi, kandai, jihi, kore ga minna watashi no shoubai datta hazu da.
Mankind was my business. The common welfare was my business; charity, mercy, forbearance, and benevolence, were, all, my business.

自分の支持者にとって有益な文言を組み込んだり、彼らに損害を与える文言を削除したりするよう、委員会の委員長に頼むこともできる。
jibun no shijisha ni totte yuueki na mongon o kumikondari, karera ni songai o ataeru mongon o sakujo shitari suru you, iinkai no iinchou ni tanomu koto mo dekiru.
You can ask the committee chairman to include language that helps your constituents or eliminate language that hurts them.
