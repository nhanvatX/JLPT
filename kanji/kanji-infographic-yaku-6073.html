Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 薬 (yaku)

September 5, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 薬 (yaku)]

MEANING:

medicine, chemical, enamel, gunpowder, benefit

HOW TO READ:

On: yaku
Kun: kusuri

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

薬 (kusuri): medicine, drugs, pill
薬物 (yakubutsu): medicines, drugs
弾薬 (dan’yaku): ammunition
製薬 (seiyaku): medicine manufacture, drug manufacture
薬品 (yakuhin): medicine, chemicals
火薬 (kayaku): gunpowder, powder
薬剤 (yakuzai): medicine, drug
薬局 (yakkyoku): pharmacy, drugstore
新薬 (shin’yaku): a new medicine
医薬 (iyaku): medicine
投薬 (touyaku): administration, medication, dosage
毒薬 (dokuyaku): poison
服薬 (fukuyaku): taking medicine
麻薬 (mayaku): dope, narcotic drugs, opium
農薬 (nouyaku): argriculture chemical, agrichemical
爆薬 (bakuyaku): explosive, blasting powder
薬草 (yakusou): medicinal plants
目薬 (megusuri): eye drops, eyewash
薬用 (yakuyou): medicinal use
薬指 (kusuriyubi): ring finger
薬学 (yakugaku): (study of) pharmacy
医薬品 (iyakuhin): medical supplies
漢方薬 (kanpouyaku): Chinese herbal medicine
薬剤師 (yakuzaishi): chemist, pharmacist
特効薬 (tokkouyaku): specific medicine, wonder drug, silver bullet
風邪薬 (kazegurusi): cold medicine
解毒薬 (gedokuyaku): antidote

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

その翌日には弾薬が再配布され、北上を命じられた。
sono yokujitsu ni wa dan’yaku ga saihaifu sare, hokujou o meijirareta.
The very next day they were reissued their ammo and ordered north.

その薬の作用で、わたしは手足がだるくなり、眠りに落ちた。
sono kusuri no sayou de, watashi wa teashi ga daruku nari, nemuri ni ochita.
The medicine made my limbs feel heavy, and then I fell asleep.

棺を墓地まで運ぶ弾薬車には、灰色の六頭の馬がつながれている。
hitsugi o hakachi made hakobu dan’yakusha ni wa, haiiro no rokutou no uma ga tsunagarete iru.
Six gray horses are hitched to a caisson that will carry the coffin to the grave.

毒薬を買う人には、台帳に署名をしてもらう慣例じゃないのですか？
dokuyaku o kau hito ni wa, daichou ni shomei o shite morau kanrei janai no desu ka.
Is it not customary for anyone purchasing poison to sign a book?

彼の薬局では、三人の徒弟が薬草や動物から抽出したエキスを調合した薬を売っていた。
kare no yakkyoku dewa, sannin no totei ga yakusou ya doubutsu kara chuushutsu shita ekisu o chougou shita kusuri o utte ita.
Here he sold medicines, herbs and animal extracts, which were processed in a workshop by three apprentices.

検屍官の問いにこたえて、彼は、はっきりと言った。自分は、有資格の薬剤師だが、前にいた助手が召集されたので、ついこのごろ、この個人の薬局に来たばかりだといった。
kenshikan no toi ni kotaete, kare wa, hakkiri to itta. jibun wa, yuushikaku no yakuzaishi da ga, mae ni ita joshu ga shoushuu sareta node, tsui kono goro, kono kojin no yakkyoku ni kita bakari da to itta.
He explained that he was a qualified pharmacist, but had only recently come to this particular shop, as the assistant formerly there had just been called up for the army.
