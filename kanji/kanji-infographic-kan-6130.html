Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 慣 (kan)

September 6, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 慣 (kan)]

MEANING:

accustomed, get used to, become experienced

HOW TO READ:

On: kan
Kun: na.reru, na.rasu

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

慣れ (nare): practice, experience
慣行 (kankou): customary practice, habit, traditional event
習慣 (shuukan): custom, habit, manners
慣例 (kanrei): custom, precedent
慣習 (kanshuu): usual (historical) custom
慣性 (kansei): inertia
慣用 (kan’you): common, customary
旧慣 (kyuukan): old customs
慣らす (narasu): to accustom
慣らう (narau): to imitate, to follow
慣れる (nareru): to grow accustomed to, to get used to
慣わし (narawashi): customary practice, habit
慣れっこ (narekko): being used to, being accustomed to
不慣れ (funare): inexperienced, unfamiliar with
慣用句 (kan’youku): idiomatic usage, common usage
悪習慣 (akushuukan): bad habit, evil practices
商慣習 (shoukanshuu): commercial practice
慣例的 (kanreiteki): typical, customary
慣習法 (kanshuuhou): common law
習慣性 (shuukansei): habit-forming, habitual
見慣れる (minareru): to become used to seeing
慣用語句 (kan’yougoku): idiom, idiomatic phrase
聞き慣れる (kikinareru): to get used to hearing
慣れ親しむ (nareshitashimu): to become familiar with, to get used to

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

彼らも人間であり、人間は習慣性の生き物だ。
karera mo ningen de ari, ningen wa shuukansei no ikimono da.
They are human and human beings are creatures of habit.

住民は、一日に五時間も続く停電に慣れっこになっていた。
juumin wa, ichinichi ni gojikan mo tsuzuku teiden ni narekko ni natte ita.
People grew accustomed to five-hour power cuts.

それはまったく理にかなっていないかもしれないが、誰もが覚えているかぎり昔からの国務省の慣習だった。
sore wa mattaku ri ni kanatte inai kamoshirenai ga, daremo ga oboete iru kagiri mukashi kara no kokumushou no kanshuu datta.
That might not make a whole lot of sense, but it’s been the practice of the State Department for as long as anyone can remember.

アメリカ空軍は、戦略爆撃こそが戦争に勝利する最適の方法であるという慣例でもつくっていたのかもしれない。
amerika kuugun wa, senryaku bakugeki koso ga sensou ni shouri suru saiteki no houhou de aru to iu kanrei demo tsukutte ita no kamoshirenai.
The U.S. Air Force might have decided as an institution that strategic bombing was the proper way to win wars.

労働組合の硬直的な慣行の結果を生じる過剰な人員配置は、隠された失業である。
roudou kumiai no kouchokuteki na kankou no kekka o shoujiru kajou na jin’in haichi wa, kakusareta shitsugyou de aru.
The overmanning resulting from trade union restrictive practices was concealed unemployment.

世界がいったんフラット化してしまい、共同作業の新しい形が誰にでも手に入るようになったいま、慣わしやプロセスやスキルを最も早く身につける者が勝者になるだろう。
sekai ga ittan furattoka shite shimai, kyoudou sagyou no atarashii katachi ga dare ni demo te ni hairu you ni natta ima, narawashi ya purosesu ya sukiru o motto mo hayaku mi ni tsukeru mono ga shousha ni naru darou.
Because once the world has been flattened and the new forms of collaboration made available to more and more people, the winners will be those who learn the habits, processes, and skills most quickly.
