Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 運 (un)

July 29, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 運 (un)]

MEANING:

carry, luck, destiny, fate, lot, transport, progress, advance

HOW TO READ:

On: un
Kun: hako.bu

COMPOUNDS:

運 (un): fortune, luck
運び (hakobi): progress, carriage, step
運ぶ (hakobu): to carry, to transport
運命 (unmei): fate, destiny
運動 (undou): motion, exercise
運転 (unten): operation, motion, driving
機運 (kiun): opportunity
不運 (fuun): unlucky, misfortune, bad luck
運賃 (unchin): fare, freight rates
運航 (unkou): operating
幸運 (kouun): good luck, fortune
運河 (unga): canal, waterway
命運 (meiun): fate, doom
海運 (kaiun): maritime, marine transportation
運行 (unkou): service (bus, train), operation
運輸 (un’yu): transportation
悪運 (akuun): bad luck
運休 (unkyuu): service suspended
運送 (unsou): transport, shipping
運営 (un’ei): management, administration
運搬 (unpan): transport, carriage
運用 (un’you): making use of, application
運良く (un’yoku): luckily
運転士 (untenshi): driver, train driver
運動会 (undoukai): sports day
運輸省 (un’yushou): Ministry of Transport
運転手 (untenshu): driver, chauffeur
運動場 (undoujou): sports ground, playground
選挙運動 (senkyoundou): participation in election campaign

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

彼は非同盟運動の礎を築く力となった。
kare wa hidoumei undou no ishizue o kizuku chikara to natta.
He helped found the nonaligned movement.

いよいよわれわれの運命の定まる時が来た。
iyoiyo wareware no unmei no sadamaru toki ga kita.
The instant approaches which will decide our fate.

こんどは、運が彼にそむくようになるかもしれない。
kondo wa, un ga kare ni somuku you ni naru kamoshirenai.
This time the luck may turn against him.

運転免許の期限が先週切れたので、今は車の運転ができない。
unten menkyo no kigen ga senshuu kireta node, ima wa kuruma no unten ga dekinai.
I can’t drive now because my license expired last week.

１９９０年代末には、二方面からインドに幸運の光が差し込んだ。
sen kyuuhyaku kyuujuu nendai matsu ni wa, ni houmen kara indo ni kouun no hikari ga sashikonda.
By the late 1990s, Lady Luck was starting to shine on India from two directions.
