Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 肉 (niku)

August 25, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 肉 (niku)]

MEANING:

meat

HOW TO READ:

On: niku
Kun: shishi

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

肉 (niku): flesh, meat
牛肉 (gyuuniku): beef
筋肉 (kinniku): muscle, sinew
皮肉 (hiniku): cynicism, sarcasm
豚肉 (butaniku): pork
肉体 (nikutai): the body, the flesh
肉食 (nikushoku): meat eating, eating of meat
肉眼 (nikugan): naked eye
焼肉 (yakiniku): yakiniku, grilled meat
肉声 (nikusei): natural voice
肉親 (nikushin): blood relationship, blood relative
鶏肉 (toriniku): chicken meat
肉屋 (nikuya): butcher
果肉 (kaniku): flesh of fruit
肉類 (nikurui): (kinds of) meat
肉迫 (nikuhaku): pressing hard, closing in upon
肉筆 (nikuhitsu): one’s own writing, autograph
肉弾 (nikudan): human bullet
羊肉 (youniku): lamb (meat)
食肉 (shokuniku): meat (for consumption)
皮肉る (hinikuru): to speak with sarcasm
肉付き (nikuzuki): bodily build, degree of fleshiness
肉体的 (nikutaiteki): corporeal, material
苦肉の策 (kuniku no saku): last resort
肉体労働 (nikutairoudou): physical labor

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

スーパーで豚肉を買いました。
suupaa de butaniku o kaimashita.
I bought pork at the supermarket.

小柄ですが肉づきがよくて、万事にぬけ目のない男です。
kogara desu ga nikuzuki ga yokute, banji ni nukeme no nai otoko desu.
He was mall, stout-built, very quick in his ways, with no hair on his face.

来る年も来る年も、妻たちが、子供たちが、そして両親たちが、肉親の帰りを待った。
kuru toshi mo kuru toshi mo, tsumatachi ga, kodomotachi ga, soshite ryoushintachi ga, nikushin no kaeri o matta.
Year after year, wives, children, and parents waited for kin to return.

トモヤは肉体の犠牲を払ったが、手首ごときはこの壮大な計画の些細な代償にすぎない。
tomoya wa nikutai no gisei o haratta ga, tekubi gotoki wa kono soudai na keikaku no sasai na daishou ni suginai.
Tomoya had made a flesh sacrifice, although a single hand was a small price in the grand scheme.

皮肉なことに、高齢化の問題がアメリカよりさらに深刻な経済的影響を及ぼしている国が、一つだけある。日本だ。
hiniku na koto ni, koureika no mondai ga amerika yori sara ni shinkoku na keizaiteki eikyou o oyoboshite iru kuni ga, hitotsu dake aru. nihon da.
Ironically, there’s only one country where the problem of an ageing population has more serious economic implications than the United States. That country is Japan.

ふつうであれば、エンジンに落雷した場合には、着陸したあとでエンジニアを呼び、肉眼で検査する必要がある。被害の状況を判断し、離陸を遅らせて修理しなければならないかどうかを判断する。
futsuu de areba, enjin ni rakurai shita baai ni wa, chakuriku shita ato de enjinia o yobi, nikugan de kensa suru hitsuyou ga aru. higai no joukyou o handan shi, ririku o okurasete shuuri shinakereba naranai ka dou ka o handan suru.
Under normal circumstances, after an engine gets hit by lightning you would have to land the plane, call in an engineer, do a visual inspection, and make a decision about how much damage might have been done and whether the plane has to be delayed in order to do a repair.
