Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 聴 (chou)

August 26, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 聴 (chou)]

MEANING:

listen, headstrong, naughty, careful inquiry

HOW TO READ:

On: chou, tei
Kun: ki.ku, yuru.su

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

聴く (kiku): to hear, to listen
聴取 (choushu): listening, hearing
聴手 (kikite): hearer, listener
聴衆 (choushuu): audience, attendance
傍聴 (bouchou): hearing, listening
視聴 (shichou): (television) viewing
聴覚 (choukaku): the sense of hearing
聴講 (choukou): lecture and attendance, auditing
聴聞 (choumon): listening, hearing
難聴 (nanchou): deafness
傾聴 (keichou): listening closely
可聴 (kachou): audible
聴力 (chouryoku): hearing ability
謹聴 (kinchou): listening attentively
聴解 (choukai): listening comprehension
盗聴 (touchou): interception, wiretap, bug
聴取る (kikitoru): to catch (a person’s words), to understand
視聴率 (shichouritsu): ratings (programme)
公聴会 (kouchoukai): public hearing
視聴者 (shichousha): viewer, (television) audience
補聴器 (hochouki): hearing aid
盗聴器 (touchouki): listening device, bug
視聴覚 (shichoukaku): senses of seeing and hearing, audiovisual
聴聞会 (choumonkai): hearing

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

一人残らず仕事をやめて謹聴しなければならない。
hitori nokorazu shigoto o yamete kinchou shinakereba naranai.
Everyone must stop working to listen.

大音量で音楽を聞きすぎると、聴覚障害になるかもしれない。
daionryou de ongaku o kikisugiru to, choukaku shougai ni naru kamoshirenai.
Listening to too much loud music can give you a hearing disability.

その会議室にはカメラと盗聴器が仕掛けてあり、いまの会議の会話と映像はすべてとりました。
sono kaigishitsu ni wa kamera to touchouki ga shikakete ari, ima no kaigi no kaiwa to eizou wa subete torimashita.
We have cameras and bugs set up in the meeting room. We have audio and visual recordings of this meeting.

光ファイバーは盗聴がきわめて難しいので、秘話通信にも適している。
hikari faibaa wa touchou ga kiwamete muzukashii node, hiwa tsuushin ni mo tekishite iru.
Fiber-optic cable is also ideal for secure communications, because it is very difficult to tap.

ＴＩＶＯによって、視聴者は見たいと思っているＣＦ以外のＣＦを飛ばしながら、好きな番組をデジタル録画できる。
TIVO ni yotte, shichousha wa mitai to omotte iru shiiefu igai no shiiefu o tobashinagara, suki na bangumi o dejitaru rokuga dekiru.
TiVo allows viewers to digitally record their favorite programs and skip the ads, except those they want to see.

人々が多様なものに向かうために視聴率は落ちるだろうが、テレビネットワークはコンテンツを所有していて再使用できる。
hitobito ga tayou na mono ni mukau tame ni shichouritsu wa ochiru darou ga, terebi nettowaaku wa kontentsu o shoyuu shite ite saishiyou dekiru.
You’ll see some falloff as people move toward variety, but the networks own the content and they can repurpose it.
