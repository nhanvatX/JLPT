Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 野 (ya)

February 28, 2017   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 野 (ya)]

MEANING:

plains, field, rustic, civilian life

HOW TO READ:

On: ya, sho
Kun: no, no-

HOW TO REMEMBER:

I turned the computer (里) on beforehand (予) so that I could work on it right away after returning from the field.

WORDS:

野 (no): plain, field
野卑 (yahi): vulgar, mean
野菜 (yasai): vegetable
野性 (yasei): wildness, rough
野外 (yagai): open air, fields, outside
野獣 (yajuu): beast, monster
視野 (shiya): field of vision
野望 (yabou): ambition, aspiration
荒野 (kouya): wasteland, wilderness
大野 (oono): large field
野犬 (yaken): stray dog
野原 (nohara): field
野球 (yakyuu): baseball
野生 (yasei): wild, going wild
分野 (bun’ya): field, sphere
野暮 (yabo): uncouth, boorish
野郎 (yarou): guy, fellow, bastard
野宿 (nojuku): sleeping outdoors
平野 (heiya): plain, open field
野心 (yashin): ambition, aspiration
野党 (yatou): opposition party
野蛮 (yaban): savage, uncivilized
野心家 (yashinka): ambitious person
焼け野原 (yakenohara): burnt field

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

山や海や森は野性の人間をつくる。
yama ya umi ya mori wa yasei no ningen o tsukuru.
The mountain, the sea, the forest make savage men.

祖父の楽しみは野外を散歩することだ。
sofu no tanoshimi wa yagai o sanpo suru koto da.
My grandfather’s pleasure consists in strolling in the fields.

眼のとどくかぎり、野にも道にも誰もいなかった。
me no todoku kagiri, no ni mo michi ni mo daremo inakatta.
As far as the eye could see there was not a person on the plain or on the path.

まるで野獣に牙をたてられたかのような鋭い痛みが胸に走った。
marude yajuu ni kiba o taterareta ka no you na surudoi itami ga mune ni hashitta.
A pain slashed at my heart as savagely as a wild animal’s fangs.

ずいぶん進歩なさったのね。ふだんは野卑なだけでしたけど、今夜は野蛮ですわ。
zuibun shinpo nasatta no ne. fudan wa yahi na dake deshita kedo, konya wa yaban desu wa.
You have improved. Generally you are only rude, but tonight you are brutal.
