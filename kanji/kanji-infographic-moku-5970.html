Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 目 (moku)

September 1, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 目 (moku)]

MEANING:

eye, class, look, insight, experience, care, favor

HOW TO READ:

On: moku, boku
Kun: me, -me, ma-

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

目 (me): eye
目途 (medo): aim, goal
目的 (mokuteki): purpose, goal, aim
目標 (mokuhyou): mark, objective, target
目玉 (medama): eyeball
駄目 (dame): no good, useless
科目 (kamoku): (school) subject, course
役目 (yakume): duty, subiness, role
注目 (chuumoku): notice, attention, observation
項目 (koumoku): (data) item, entry
着目 (chakumoku): attention
目下 (meshita): subordinate, inferior
目撃 (mokugeki): to observe, to witness
目前 (mokuzen): before one’s very eyes, imminence
一目 (hitome): a glance, a look, a glimpse
目眩 (memai): dizziness, giddiness
面目 (menmoku): face, honor, reputation
目下 (mokka): at present, now
盲目 (moumoku): blindness
目薬 (megusuri): eye drops
眼目 (ganmoku): core, gist, essence
目上 (meue): superior, senior
目録 (mokuroku): catalog, inventory, index
目先 (mesaki): near future, immediate
目安 (meyasu): criterion, standard
人目 (hitome): public gaze, public notice
品目 (hinmoku): item, commodity
裏目 (urame): reverse side, opposite
演目 (enmoku): program, programme
目差し (menazashi): look, gaze
目隠し (mekakushi): blindfold, eye bandage
目当て (meate): mark, guide, aim, goal
目覚し (mezamashi): alarm clock, opening one’s eyes
目指す (mezasu): to aim at, to have an eye on
目立つ (medatsu): to stand out
目叩く (metataku): to blink, to wink
効き目 (kikime): effect, virtue, efficacy
目的地 (mokutekichi): place of destination
多目的 (tamokuteki): multipurpose
真面目 (majime): diligent, serious, earnest
目覚める (mezameru): to wake up
目掛ける (megakeru): to aim at
目茶苦茶 (mechakucha): absurd, unreasonable, nonsensical

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

彼女の目には涙が浮かんでいた。強い母性的な魂がゆすぶられたのだ。
kanojo no me ni wa namida ga ukande ita. tsuyoi boseiteki na tamashii ga yusuburareta no da.
Tears had come into her eyes. Her strong, motherly soul was moved.

大きな足は婚家の面目をつぶすものだ。
ooki na ashi wa konka no menboku o tsubusu mono da.
Large feet are considered to bring shame on the husband’s household.

彼はまた、窓から手榴弾を敵目掛けて投下した。
kare wa mata, mado kara shuryuudan o teki megakete touka shita.
He bombed the enemies by tossing hand grenades out the windows.

彼がどんなに打撃をうけたかということは、一目で明らかであった。
kare ga donna ni dageki o uketa ka to iu koto wa, hitome de akiraka de atta.
He had felt the blow keenly, I could see.

国民保険に関する議論は、単に社会的な公正さを目指すという目標を超えて広がりをみせた。
kokumin hoken ni kansuru giron wa, tan ni shakaiteki na kouseisa o mezasu to iu mokuhyou o koete hirogari o miseta.
The arguments for state insurance extended beyond mere social equity.

わたしはこの時、大蔵省の金融政策の運営の仕方に疑問を抱いていたのだが、それ以上に、政府の財政政策の運営目標だったＰＳＢＲの見込み値がどんどん上昇することを心配していた。
watashi wa kono toki, ookurashou no kin’yuu seisaku no un’ei no shikata ni gimon o idaite ita no da ga, sore ijou ni, seifu no zaisei seisaku no un’ei mokuhyou datta piiesubiiaru no mikomichi ga dondon joushou suru koto o shinpai shite ita.
My doubts at this time about the Treasury’s conduct of monetary policy, however, were more than matched by the concern I felt at the steady growth in its estimates of the PSBR — the target by which we steered our fiscal policy.
