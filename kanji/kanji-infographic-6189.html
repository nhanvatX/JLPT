Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 回 (kai)

September 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 回]

MEANING:

-times, round, game, revolve, counter for occurrences

HOW TO READ:

On: kai, e
Kun: mawa.ru, -mawa.ru, -mawa.ri, mawa.su, -mawa.su, mawa.shi-, -mawa.shi, matoo.ru, ka.eru

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

回 (kai): counter for occurrences, a time, an instance
回り (mawari): circumference, surroundings, rotation
回す (mawasu): to turn, to rotate, to curculate
回路 (kairo): circuit (electric)
回転 (kaiten): rotation, revolution, turning
撤回 (tekkai): withdrawal, revocation, repeal
一回 (ikkai): once, one time, one round
回復 (kaifuku): restoration, rehabilitation, recovery
今回 (konkai): now, this time, lately
回線 (kaisen): circuit, line
回顧 (kaiko): recollection
奪回 (dakkai): recovery, rescue, recapture
回想 (kaisou): reflection, reminiscence
回答 (kaitou): reply, answer
回避 (kaihi): evasion, avoidance
回帰 (kaiki): return to, recursion, comeback, revival
回生 (kaisei): resurrection, regeneration
前回 (zenkai): previous time, last time
初回 (shokai): first time, initial attempt
何回 (nankai): how many times
回収 (kaishuu): collection, recovery, retrieval
巡回 (junkai): going around, patril, tour
毎回 (maikai): every time, each round
回数 (kaisuu): number of times, frequency, count
回戦 (kaisen): match, game
利回り (rimawari): interest, (investment) yield, profits
後回し (atomawashi): putting off, postponing
根回し (nemawashi): making necessary arrangements, laying the groundwork
言回し (iimawashi): expression, phraseology
乗回す (norimawasu): to drive (a car) around, to ride (a bicycle) around
走り回る (hashirimawaru): to run around

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

鶏が、黒豚とともに、庭を走り回っている。
niwatori ga, kurobuta to tomo ni, niwa o hashirimawatte iru.
Chickens share the yards with the hogs.

気象班が天候悪化を予報し、命令は撤回された。
kishou han ga tenkou akka o yohou shi, meirei wa tekkai sareta.
The order was cancelled when meteorologists forecast bad weather.

私は、喜んで、あの男を十二回も刺してやったことでしょう。
watashi wa, yorokonde, ano otoko o juuni kai mo sashite yatta koto deshou.
I would have stabbed that man twelve times willingly.

人気のないところに落とせば、回収もそうむずかしいことではない。
ninki no nai tokoro ni otoseba, kaishuu mo sou muzukashii koto dewa nai.
If it falls somewhere desolate, it won’t be hard for them to retrieve it.

回転するローターの羽はエンジンの轟音を地上にぶつけて遠くまで響かせないのである。
kaiten suru rootaa no hane wa enjin no gouon o chijou ni butsukete tooku made hibikasenai no de aru.
The whirling rotor blades drive the sound of the engines into the earth.

まず、ゲームの内容と説明をインドにある自分の会社とアメリカの取引先が電子メールでやりとりできるだけの回線容量が必要だった。
mazu, geemu no naiyou to setsumei o indo ni aru jibun no kaisha to amerika no torihikisaki ga denshi meeru de yaritori dekiru dake no kaisen youryou ga hitsuyou datta.
The first was to have enough installed bandwidth so I could e-mail game content and instructions back and forth between my own company in India and my American clients.
