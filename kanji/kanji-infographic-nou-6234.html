Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 濃 (nou)

September 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary


[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 濃 (nou)]

MEANING:

concentrated, thick, dark, undiluted

HOW TO READ:

On: nou
Kun: ko.i

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

濃い (koi): deep, dark, thick, dense
濃度 (noudo): concentration, thickness, density
濃厚 (noukou): density, richness, concentration
濃縮 (noushuku): concentration (of a solution)
濃淡 (noutan): light and shade, depth
濃密 (noumitsu): thick, rich, crowded
濃艶 (nouen): charming, bewitching
濃化 (nouka): thicken, concentrate
濃霧 (noumu): heavy fog, thick fog
濃紺 (noukon): dark blue
濃煙 (nouen): thick smoke
濃茶 (koicha): dark matcha
濃やか (komayaka): friendly, heartfelt
色濃い (irokoi): marked, strongly tending to
脂濃い (aburakkoi): greasy, fatty, oily
濃い目 (koime): strongish, somewhat strong
塩濃度 (ennoudo): salt concentration
濃褐色 (noukasshoku): dark brown
高濃度 (kounoudo): high concentration
低濃度 (teinoudo): low concentration
濃い作り (koitsukuri): heavy makeup

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

兵士たちは、金で縁どりされた濃紺の第一級正装である。
heishitachi wa, kin de fuchidori sareta noukon no daiikkyuu seisou de aru.
The soldiers are in full dress, dark blue trimmed with gold.

他の民間人が濃い色のスーツを着ていたのに、彼のだけは明るいグレーだった。
hoka no minkanjin ga koi iro no suutsu o kite ita no ni, kare no dake wa akarui guree datta.
Unlike the other civilians in suits of dark colors, he was dressed in light gray.

ふだんなら、ここのあたたかく湿った濃密な空気には安らぎを覚える。いまは汗ばんでいる。
fudan nara, koko no atatakaku shimetta noumitsu na kuuki ni wa yasuragi o oboeru. ima wa asebande iru.
The heavy, damp warmth of this place usually feels comforting to me. Tonight, I’m sweating.

遠心分離機は非情な高速で回転し、核爆弾の製造が可能な濃度にまでウランを濃縮する。
enshin bunriki wa hijou na kousoku de kaiten shi, kaku bakudan no seizou ga kanou na noudo ni made uran o noushuku suru.
Centrifuges spin at incredibly fast speeds, enriching uranium to a high enough level that it can be used to build a bomb.

司令部では地位が上れば上るほど、戦争は終わり、残るのは若干の残敵掃討のみとの気分が濃厚だった。
shireibu dewa chii ga noboreba noboru hodo, sensou wa owari, nokoru no wa jakkan no zanteki soutou nomi to no kibun ga noukou datta.
The higher you went in headquarters, the stronger was the feeling that the war was over, and that the only job left was a certain amount of mopping up.

軽油からは黒くて濃い、のどの焼けるような煙がたちのぼり、たちまち空中に充満した。
The diesel gave off a thick, black, acrid smoke that quickly filled the air.
keiyu kara wa kurokute koi, nodo no yakeru you na kemuri ga tachinobori, tachimachi kuuchuu ni juuman shita.

そのため、流入する水に溶けている塩やカルシウムなどが湖にたまり、それらの濃度が非常に高い。
For that reason, salt, calcium and so on dissolved in the inflowing water collect in the lake in very high concentrations.
sono tame, ryuunyuu suru mizu ni tokete iru shio ya karushiumu nado ga mizuumi ni tamari, sorera no noudo ga hijou ni takai.

イランはウラン濃縮を行っており、その量と濃度は、合理的に考えれば、彼らの意図を疑うのに十分だった。
Iran enriched uranium at levels and quantities that led reasonable people to harbor well-founded suspicions of its intentions.
iran wa uran noushuku o okonatte ori, sono ryou to noudo wa, gouriteki ni kangaereba, karera no ito o utagau no ni juubun datta.

コム近くにあるイランの秘密濃縮施設の存在を公に暴露すれば騒ぎになることはわかっており、我々はそれを有利に使えると考えた。
We knew that public disclosure of Iran’s secret enrichment facility near Qom would cause an uproar—one we hoped to use to our advantage.
komu chikaku ni aru iran no himitsu noushuku shisetsu no sonzai o ooyake ni bakuro sureba sawagi ni naru koto wa wakatte ori, wareware wa sore o yuuri ni tsukaeru to kangaeta.

その上空を観光用セスナで飛んでいると、真っ青な大海原にひときわ濃いブルーブラックのインクを円形に垂らしたような不思議な風景に出会う。
As your tourist Cessna flies over, you encounter an intriguing sight: a round blot of what looks like very dark blue-black ink that has been dropped into the broad expanse of blue ocean.
sono joukuu o kankou you sesuna de tonde iru to, massao na daikaigen ni hitokiwa koi buruu burakku no inku o enkei ni tarashita you na fushigi na fuukei ni deau.
