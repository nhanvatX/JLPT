Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 場 (jou)

September 15, 2016   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 場 (jou)]

MEANING:

place, location

HOW TO READ:

On: jou, chou
Kun: ba

HOW TO REMEMBER:

The best place to bury your piggy bank (昜) is the soil (土) in your backyard.

WORDS:

場合 (baai): case, situation
場所 (basho): place, location
立場 (tachiba): standpoint, position
広場 (hiroba): plaza, square
登場 (toujou): entry (on stage)
場面 (bamen): scene, setting
工場 (koujou): factory, mill
現場 (genba): actual spot, crime scene
職場 (shokuba): workplace
農場 (noujou): farm (agriculture)
入場 (nyuujou): entrance, admission
置場 (okiba): place for something
劇場 (gekijou): theatre
場内 (jounai): on the ground
会場 (kaijou): meeting place
場外 (jougai): off the ground
出場 (shutsujou): stage appearance
相場 (souba): market price
帳場 (chouba): reception
馬場 (baba): horse-riding ground
猟場 (ryouba): hunting ground
場違い (bachigai): inappropriate

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

遠く離れた場所へ一人で行きたい。
tooku hanareta basho e hitori de ikitai.
I want to make an excersion of my own to a remote place.

僕の立場は少し公けなんだ。
boku no tachiba wa sukoshi ooyake nan da.
I’ve got some slight official standing.

山本の姿は、はなはだしく場違いだった。
yamamoto no sugata wa, hanahadashiku bachigai datta.
Yamamoto looked quite extraordinarily out of place.

彼が登場すると必ずひと悶着起きる。
kare ga toujou suru to kanarazu hito monchaku okiru.
There is always trouble when he is present.

広場全体が男や女の頭で埋まっていた。
hiroba zentai ga otoko ya onna no atama de umatte ita.
The entire square was paved with heads.
