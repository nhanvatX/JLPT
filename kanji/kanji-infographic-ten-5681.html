Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 点 (ten)

August 25, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 点 (ten)]

MEANING:

spot, point, mark, speck, decimal point

HOW TO READ:

On: ten
Kun: tsu.keru, tsu.ku, ta.teru, sa.su, tobo.su, tomo.su, bochi

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

点 (ten): spot, mark, point
点く (tsuku): to be lit, to be lighted
汚点 (oten): stain, flaw, disgrace
時点 (jiten): point in time, occasion
観点 (kanten): point of view
得点 (tokuten): score, points made
視点 (shiten): opinion, point of view
拠点 (kyoten): position, location, base
焦点 (shouten): focus, focal point
点数 (tensuu): marks, points, score
点検 (tenken): inspection, examination
地点 (chiten): site, spot
弱点 (jakuten): weak point, weakness
一点 (itten): dot, point, speck
採点 (saiten): marking, grading
頂点 (chouten): top, summit
満点 (manten): perfect score
利点 (riten): advantage, point in favor
欠点 (ketten): faults, defect, weakness
重点 (juuten): important point, emphasis
同点 (douten): deadlock, tie, draw
点滴 (tenteki): raindrops, falling drop of water
難点 (nanten): fault, weakness
接点 (setten): point of agreement, interaction
終点 (shuuten): terminus, last stop
力点 (rikiten): leverage, emphasis
論点 (ronten): point in question
点滅 (tenmetsu): flashing, blinking
争点 (souten): point at issue
原点 (genten): origin, starting point
点ける (tsukeru): to turn on, to switch on
問題点 (mondaiten): the problem (at issue), problematic issue
現時点 (genjiten): present point, at the present time
交差点 (kousaten): crossing, intersection
決勝点 (kesshouten): winning point

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

あの交差点を左に曲がってください。
ano kousaten o hidari ni magatte kudasai.
Please turn left at that intersection.

だが実はこのことはイランの根本的な弱点でもある。
da ga jitsu wa kono koto wa iran no konponteki na jakuten demo aru.
But that is actually Iran’s basic weakness.

あれはご自分の経歴について汚点だと思われますか？
are wa gojibun no keireki ni tsuite oten da to omowaremasu ka?
Is it a blot on your record?

さまざまな点で、債券市場が強大だという見方は正しかった。
samazama na ten de, saiken ichiba ga kyoudai da to iu mikata wa tadashikatta.
In many ways, it was true that the bond market was powerful.

軍事的な観点からすれば、引き揚げは順調に行われた例といえた。
gunjiteki na kanten kara sureba, hikiage wa junchou ni okonawareta rei to ieta.
From a logistical standpoint, the repatriation process was an impressive accomplishment.

２００１年、フォードは、動きの鈍い錯綜した流通ネットワークをＵＰＳに一任し、問題点を探ってサプライチェーンを潤滑にするために、管理部門にＵＰＳを招いた。
nisen ichi nen, foodo wa, ugoki no nibui sakusou shita ryuutsuu nettowaaku o yuupiiesu ni ichinin shi, mondaiten o sagutte sapuraicheen o junkatsu ni suru tame ni, kanri bumon ni yuupiiesu o maneita.
In 2001, Ford turned over its snarled and slow distribution network to UPS, allowing UPS to come deep inside Ford to figure out what its problems were and smooth out its supply chain.
