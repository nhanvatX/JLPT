Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 策 (saku)

September 5, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 策 (saku)]

MEANING:

scheme, plan, policy, step, means

HOW TO READ:

On: saku

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

策 (saku): plan, policy, measure
施策 (shisaku): policy, measure
対策 (taisaku): counter-plan, counter-measure
策戦 (sakusen): tactics, strategy
方策 (housaku): plan, policy
失策 (shissaku): blunder, slip, error
得策 (tokusaku): profitable plan, good plan
国策 (kokusaku): national policy
秘策 (hisaku): secret plan or measures
政策 (seisaku): political measures, policy
策定 (sakutei): decision, settling on
画策 (kakusaku): plan, scheme, maneuver
万策 (bansaku): all means
策謀 (sakubou): strategy, artifice
策動 (sakudou): maneuverings
策略 (sakuryaku): scheme, tactic, trick
散策 (sansaku): walking, strolling
建策 (kensaku): making a plan, recommendation
術策 (jussaku): artifice, intrigue
上策 (jousaku): excellent plan, best policy
無策 (musaku): lacking means or measures or policies
善後策 (zengosaku): remedial measure, counter-measure
解決策 (kaiketsusaku): solution, solution strategy
テロ対策 (terotaisaku): anti-terrorist
門戸開放政策 (monkokaihouseisaku): open door policy

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

ミユキは家に帰って、その策略を得々と自慢した。
miyuki wa ie ni kaette, sono sakuryaku o tokutoku to jiman shita.
Miyuki cheerfully boasted about the ruse when she got home.

この術策を意味する婉曲な表現は「共同計画」である。
kono jussaku o imi suru enkyoku na hyougen wa kyoudou keikaku de aru.
The euphemism for the ploy was “joint planning.”

また、近年の行き過ぎた行為をすべて金融政策のせいにすることも疑問だ。
mata, kinnen no ikisugita koui o subete kin’yuu seisaku no sei ni suru koto mo gimon da.
Nor is it credible to blame all the excesses of recent years on monetary policy.

しかも事件の成行きをいっそう混乱させる対策さえ講じられていました。
shikamo jiken no nariyuki o issou konran saseru taisaku sae koujirarete imashita.
The only additional thing to be done was to confuse the issue even further.

多くのバブル経済の原因が、中央銀行の手落ちや失策にあるのはこのためだ。
ooku no baburu keizai no gen’in ga, chuuou ginkou no teochi ya shissaku ni aru no wa kono tame da.
That is why so many bubbles have their origins in the sins of omission or commission of central banks.

かたや左派の急進派たちは、政治の分野に新たな勢力が登場し、それが政府の財政に拒否権を発動し、したがってほとんどの政策の決定に権勢を振るうことになった点を嘆いた。
kataya saha no kyuushinhatachi wa, seiji no bun’ya ni arata na seiryoku ga toujou shi, sore ga seifu no zaisei ni kyohiken o hatsudou shi, shitagatte hotondo no seisaku no kettei ni kensei o furuu koto ni natta ten o nageita.
Meanwhile, Radicals on the Left bemoaned the rise of a new power in the realm of politics, which wielded a veto power over government finance and hence over most policy.
