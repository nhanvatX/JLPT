Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 善 (zen)

May 20, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 善 (zen)]

MEANING:

virtuous, good, goodness

HOW TO READ:

On: zen
Kun: yo.i, i.i, yo.ku, yoshi.tosuru

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

善 (zen): good, goodness, right, virtue
善い (yoi): good, excellent, nice
善き (yoki): goodness
偽善 (gizen): hypocrisy
改善 (kaizen): betterment, improvement
最善 (saizen): the very best
善意 (zen’i): good intentions, good will
善良 (zenryou): goodness, virtue
善悪 (zen’aku): good and evil
親善 (shinzen): friendship
次善 (jizen): second best
慈善 (jizen): charity, philanthropy
善戦 (zensen): fighting a good fight
善行 (zenkou): good deed, good conduct
善人 (zennin): good person, virtuous person
善処 (zensho): handling carefully
独善 (dokuzen): self-righteousness
善隣 (zenrin): good neighbor
善業 (zengou): good deeds
善くも (yokumo): how dare…
偽善者 (gizensha): hypocrite, dissembler
慈善家 (jizenka): charitable person
慈善事業 (jizenjigyou): philanthropic work
善し悪し (yoshiashi): right or wrong, good or bad

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

誰もが皆、善良な心を持っているというのは、考えが甘い。
daremo ga minna, zenryou na kokoro o motte iru to iu no wa, kangae ga amai.
It’s naive to say that everyone has a good heart.

銀行強盗をするための最善の条件は、自ら銀行を保有することだ。
ginkou goutou o suru tame no saizen no jouken wa, mizukara ginkou o hoyuu suru koto da.
The best way to rob a bank is to own one.

図星をさされて激怒するのは、あなたのような偽善者だけですよ。
zuboshi o sasarete gekido suru no wa, anata no you na gizensha dake desu yo.
Only hypocrites like you will become enraged when called by their right names.

わたしは、アフリカ救済の慈善の催しをあのレストランでやったことがあります。
watashi wa, afurika kyuusai no jizen no moyooshi o ano resutoran de yatta koto ga arimasu.
I have once hosted a benefit at that restaurant for African relief.

われわれの経済の状況を改善するためには堅実な財政戦略が必要だった。
wareware no keizai no joukyou o kaizen suru tame ni wa kenjitsu na zaisei senryaku ga hitsuyou datta.
A firm financial strategy was necessary to improve our economic performance.

彼はこの善い、古い都にも、または他のいかなる善い、古い都にも、町にも村にも、この善い古い世界にもかつてなかったくらいの善い友となり、善い主人となり、善い人間となった。
kare wa kono yoi, furui miyako ni mo, matawa hoka no ikanaru yoi, furui miyako ni mo, machi ni mo mura ni mo, kono yoi furui sekai ni mo katsute nakatta kurai no yoi tomo to nari, yoi shujin to nari, yoi ningen to natta.
He became as good a friend, as good a master, and as good a man, as the good old city knew, or any other good old city, town, or borough, in the good old world.
