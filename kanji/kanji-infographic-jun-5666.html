Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 順 (jun)

August 24, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 順 (jun)]

MEANING:

obey, order, turn, right, docility, occasion

HOW TO READ:

On: jun

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

順に (junni): in order, in turn
順位 (jun’i): order, rank, position
手順 (tejun): process, procedure
順次 (junji): in order, sequential
順序 (junjo): order, sequence
順当 (juntou): proper, right, reasonable
順守 (junshu): compliance, adherence
順調 (junchou): favorable, doing well
不順 (fujun): irregularity, unseasonableness
順番 (junban): order of things, turn (in line)
筆順 (hitsujun): stroke order
順路 (junro): (regular) route
順応 (junnou): adaptation, adjustment
順法 (junpou): obeying the law
席順 (sekijun): seating order
温順 (onjun): docile, tame
語順 (gojun): word order
孝順 (koujun): obedience, filial piety
従順 (juujun): obedient, meek, submissive
順風 (junpuu): favorable wind
道順 (michijun): itinerary, route
順々に (junjun ni): in order, in turn
雨天順延 (utenjun’en): rescheduled in case of rain
演算手順 (enzantejun): algorithm

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

私は優先順位について上官と争うことはしない。
watashi wa yuusen jun’i ni tsuite joukan to arasou koto wa shinai.
I never tried to fight my superiors’ priorities.

僕はこのへんの家々の順序を記憶しておきたいと思うんだ。
boku wa kono hen no ieie no junjo o kioku shite okitai to omoun da.
I should like just to remember the order of the houses here.

わたしたちの乗った輸送機は朝もやのなかを順調に進み、心配をするにはいたらなかった。
watashitachi no notta yusouki wa asa moya no naka o junchou ni susumi, shinpai o suru ni wa itaranakatta.
As our plane cruised through the hazy morning, it was hard to feel concerned.

資本主義的な指針で自動車部品工場を運営する手順を理解している中国人マネジャーが払底していた。
shihon shugiteki na shishin de jidousha buhin koujou o un’ei suru tejun o rikai shite iru chuugokujin manejaa ga futtei shite ita.
There was a critical shortage of Chinese managers who understood how to run an auto parts factory along capitalist lines.

彼女は、父の出身背景が災いし、順々に夢を打ち砕かれていった三人の姉の失望をまのあたりにしていた。
kanojo wa, chichi no shusshin haikei ga wazawai shi, junjun ni yume o uchikudakarete itta sannin no ane no shitsubou o ma no atari ni shite ita.
She had seen her three sisters’ disappointment as one by one they discovered that their father’s background would thwart their ambitions.

日本では、平等主義を理想の政策目標とし、社会的に体制順応主義の文化を擁するため、規則に従うことがよしとされる。
nihon dewa, byoudou shugi o risou no seisaku mokuhyou to shi, shakaiteki ni taisei junnou shugi no bunka o you suru tame, kisoku ni shitagau koto ga yoshi to sareru.
In Japan egalitarianism was a prized goal of policy, while a culture of social conformism encouraged compliance with the rules.
