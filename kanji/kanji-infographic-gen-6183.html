Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 言 (gen)

September 7, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 言 (gen)]

MEANING:

say, word

HOW TO READ:

On: gen, gon
Kun: i.u, koto

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

言う (iu): to say, to call
言語 (gengo): language
無言 (mugon): silence
言葉 (kotoba): language, word, speech
証言 (shougen): evidence, testimony
提言 (teigen): proposal, motion
断言 (dangen): assertion, affirmation
一言 (hitokoto): single word, a few words, brief comment
言論 (genron): one’s speech, expression of views
寝言 (negoto): sleep-talking, nonsense
助言 (jogen): advice, suggestion
言及 (genkyuu): reference, allusion
宣言 (sengen): declaration, proclamation
発言 (hatsugen): utterance, speech, proposal
言動 (gendou): speech and conduct, words and deeds
流言 (ryuugen): groundless rumor
予言 (yogen): prediction, promise
遺言 (yuigon): will, testament
方言 (hougen): dialect
明言 (meigen): declaration, statement
伝言 (dengon): verbal message, word
公言 (kougen): declaration, profession
言わば (iwaba): so to speak
言渡す (iiwatasu): to announce, to tell, to sentence
言い分 (iibun): one’s say, one’s point
言表す (iiarawasu): to express (in words)
言い訳 (iiwake): excuse, explanation
合言葉 (aikotoba): password, motto, slogan
書き言葉 (kakikotoba): written word, written language
言付ける (iizukeru): to send word, to send a message
言い換える (iikaeru): to say in other words

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

町中が義憤と流言と非難で湧きかえった。
machinaka ga gifun to ryuugen to hinan de wakikaetta.
The town was boiling with indignation, rumor, and accusations.

こんなことを言うのは、権力の近くへ誘われているからではない。
konna koto o iu no wa, kenryoku no chikaku e sasowarete iru kara dewa nai.
I say this not because I am seduced by the proximity to power.

一体誰に訊ねたなら、この悲惨な状況を脱け出すための助言が得られようか。
ittai dare ni tazuneta nara, kono hisan na joukyou o nukedasu tame no jogen o erareyou ka.
To whom will I apply to help me out of this painful situation?

ねえ、その伝言の最後は、なんというんだっけ？もう一度、いってくれないか？
nee, sono dengon no saigo wa, nan to iun da kke. mou ichido, itte kurenai ka.
I say, what was the end of that message? Say it over again, will you?

このこと、あらゆる言語における世界の知識を容易に入手できるようにする、は、間違いなくグーグルの目標である。
kono koto, arayuru gengo ni okeru sekai no chishiki o youi ni nyuushu dekiru you ni suru, wa, machigainaku guuguru no mokuhyou de aru.
That is certainly Google’s goal—to make easily available all the world’s knowledge in every language.

言い換えれば、二度の世界大戦のときと同じく、福祉国家アメリカは保険会社が悲鳴を上げたときに救いの手を差し述べたのだった。だがこれは、自然災害においては皮肉な結果を生む。
iikaereba, nido no sekai taisen no toki to onajiku, fukushi kokka amerika wa hoken kaisha ga himei o ageta toki ni sukui no te o sashinobeta no datta. da ga kore wa, shizen saigai ni oite wa hiniku na kekka o umu.
In other words, just as happened during the world wars, the welfare state steps in when the insurers are overwhelmed. But this has a perverse result in the case of natural disasters.
