Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 盛 (sei)

September 4, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 盛 (sei)]

MEANING:

boom, prosper, copulate

HOW TO READ:

On: sei, jou
Kun: mo.ru, saka.ru, saka.n

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

盛ん (sakan): popular, prosperous
盛り (sakari): peak, prime, one’s best days
盛る (moru): to serve, to fill up, to prescribe
盛る (sakaru): to prosper, to flourish
全盛 (zensei): height of prosperity
繁盛 (hanjou): prosperity, flourishing, thriving
盛大 (seidai): grand, magnificent
盛時 (seiji): prime of life
盛況 (seikyou): success, prosperity
隆盛 (ryuusei): prosperity
盛夏 (seika): midsummer, height of summer
盛期 (seiki): prosperous period
豪盛 (gousei): luxury, magnificence
花盛り (hanazakari): flowers in full bloom
目盛り (memori): scale, gradations
大盛り (oomori): a large serving
山盛り (yamamori): heap, pile, stack
一盛り (hitomori): pile
最盛期 (saiseiki): golden age, best time for
全盛期 (zenseiki): heyday, golden age
盛り込む (morikomu): to incorporate, to include
盛り付け (moritsuke): serving of food
意気盛ん (ikisakan): in high spirits
盛り上げる (moriageru): to pile up, to heap up
盛り上がり (moriagari): climax, uprush
盛り上がる (moriagaru): to swell, to rise, to bulge

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

医院や薬局のほうも繁盛していた。
iin ya yakkyoku no hou mo hanjou shite ita.
I also owned a flourishing medical practice and a medicine shop.

大切な訪問者に対するソ連のもてなしは、いつも盛大だった。
taisetsu na houmonsha ni taisuru soren no motenashi wa, itsumo seidai datta.
Hospitality in the Soviet Union was always generous for important visitors.

盛んなころには、もっともすばらしい探偵の一人と思われたものですよ。
sakan na koro ni wa, motto mo subarashii tantei no hitori to omowareta mono desu yo.
He was considered one of the finest detectives of his day.

そのため、企業への支援を予算に盛り込むことを求める声が強かった。
sono tame, kigyou e no shien o yosan ni morikomu koto o motomeru koe ga tsuyokatta.
There was, therefore, a strong argument for a budget which helped business.

神経のある人間ならだれだってすぐに、旦那さんが奥さまに毒を盛ったぐらいわかるわ。
shinkei no aru ningen nara dare datte sugu ni, dannasan ga okusama ni doku o motta gurai wakaru wa.
Anyone with any sense could see at once that her husband had poisoned her.

田んぼのあぜのように盛り上がった沼の周囲はコケや地衣類によってオレンジや黄色を帯び、広大な荒原に美しい模様を作り出す。
tanbo no aze no you ni moriagatta numa no shuui wa koke ya chiirui ni yotte orenji ya kiiro o obi, koudai na kougen ni utsukushii moyou o tsukuridasu.
The raised borders around them, like ridges between rice paddies, have turned orange and yellow with moss and lichen, creating a lovely pattern across the vast wilderness.
