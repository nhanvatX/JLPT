Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 辺 (hen)

September 6, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 辺 (hen)]

MEANING:

environs, boundary, border, vicinity

HOW TO READ:

On: hen
Kun: ata.ri, hoto.ri, -be

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

辺 (hen): area, vicinity, region
辺り (atari): on the bank of, by the side of
浜辺 (hamabe): beach, foreshore
周辺 (shuuhen): circumference, environs, outskirts
天辺 (teppen): top, summit, peak
普辺 (fuhen): universal, general
岸辺 (kishibe): shore, bank
近辺 (kinpen): vicinity, neighborhood
左辺 (sahen): left side
窓辺 (madobe): by the window
辺鄙 (henpi): hard to reach place, remote place
野辺 (nobe): field
上辺 (uwabe): surface, outside
辺地 (henchi): remote place
右辺 (uhen): right side
無辺 (muhen): infinite, boundless
水辺 (mizube): waterside, waterfront
辺境 (henkyou): remote region, frontier
身辺 (shinpen): one’s person, around one’s person
底辺 (teihen): base, low class, low level
海辺 (umibe): beach, seashore
どの辺 (dohohen): whereabouts
周辺諸国 (shuuhenshokoku): surrounding countries

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

彼はベッドを飛び降り、窓辺に駆け寄った。
kare wa beddo o tobiori, madobe ni kakeyotta.
He jumped out of bed and ran to the window.

わたしは、海辺にいるんです。アミを捜しているんです。
watashi wa, umibe ni irun desu. ami o sagashite irun desu.
I’m on the beach. Looking for Ami.

ショウコの車から流れる大音量の音楽は、周辺の車の中にも響いた。
shouko no kuruma kara nagareru daionryou no ongaku wa, shuuhen no kuruma no naka ni mo hibiita.
Loud music from Shoko’s car resonated inside nearby cars.

祖父は身辺な存在だったが、わたしに多くの方向性を与えられるほど若くはなかったし、本人にもいろいろ問題があった。
sofu wa shinpen na sonzai datta ga, watashi ni ooku no houkousei o ataerareru hodo wakaku wa nakatta shi, honnin ni mo iroiro mondai ga atta.
As close as I was to my grandfather, he was both too old and too troubled to provide me with much direction.

彼は辺鄙な町での自分の集会でベトナム人の牧師たちから聞きだせることを喜んで伝えてくれた。
kare wa henpi na machi de no jibun no shuukai de betonamujin no bokushitachi kara kikidaseru koto o yorokonde tsutaetekureta.
He was happy to pass along what he could gather from the Vietnamese pastors of his congregations in the outlying towns.

奥さん、あなたがお聞きになったのは、隣りの、つまり殺された男の部屋のドアの辺りで、誰かの動く音ではなかったのでしょうね？
okusan, anata ga okiki ni natta no wa, tonari no, tsumari korosareta otoko no heya no doa no atari de, dareka no ugoku oto dewa nakatta no deshou ne.
You do not think, Madame, that what you heard was the noise of someone moving about next door—in the murdered man’s room?
