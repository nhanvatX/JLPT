Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 寝 (shin)

September 1, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 寝 (shin)]

MEANING:

lie down, sleep, rest, bed, remain unsold

HOW TO READ:

On: shin
Kun: ne.ru, ne.kasu, i.nu, mitamaya, ya.meru

JLPT LEVEL: N3

COMPOUNDS:

寝る (neru): to sleep
寝坊 (nebou): oversleeping, sleeping in late
寝言 (negoto): sleep-talking
寝室 (shinshitsu): bedroom
昼寝 (hirune): nap (afternoon), siesta
寝息 (neiki): sleeper’s breathing
寝具 (shingu): bedding
寝台 (shindai): bed, couch
寝袋 (nebukuro): sleeping bag
寝顔 (negao): sleeping face
寝床 (nedoko): bed, berth
就寝 (shuushin): going to bed, retiring
早寝 (hayane): go to bed early
仮寝 (karine): siesta, nap, catnap
寝巻き (nemaki): sleep-wear, pajamas
寝付く (netsuku): to fall asleep
寝返る (negaeru): to change sides, to double-cross
転び寝 (korobine): dozing
寝返り (negaeri): turning over while sleeping in bed, betrayal
寝込む (nekomu): to stay in bed, to sleep
寝転ぶ (nekorobu): to lie down
お寝小 (onesho): bed-wetting
二度寝 (nidone): going back to sleep
不寝番 (fushinban): night watch, sleepless vigil
朝寝坊 (asanebou): late riser, oversleeping
寝不足 (nebusoku): lack of sleep
寝かせる (nekaseru): to put to bed, to lay down
一寝入り (hitoneiri): nap
寝過ごす (nesugosu): to oversleep
寝泊まり (netomari): staying at, lodging at
寝静まる (neshizumaru): to fall asleep
寝苦しい (negurushii): unable to sleep well

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

わたしは夜寝る前に歯を磨きます。
watashi wa yoru neru mae ni ha o migakimasu.
I brush my teeth before going to bed at night.

起きなさいよ、なんだって寝坊してるのさ！
okinasai yo, nan datte nebou shiteru no sa.
Get up, why are you asleep?

毎日、昼飯の後には昼寝ができる。過重な仕事ではない。
mainichi, hirumeshi no nochi ni wa hirune ga dekiru. kajuu na shigoto dewa nai.
You could take you a nap every day after lunch. We won’t overwork you.

わたし自身、二度にわたって不寝番の狙撃兵に狙われたことがある。
watashi jishin, nido ni watatte fushinban no sogekihei ni nerawareta koto ga aru.
I was shot at a couple of times by a stay-awake sniper.

二階にはほかに、下仕官用の共同寝室がいくつか設えられている。
nikai ni wa hoka ni, geshikan you no kyoudou shinshitsu ga ikutsu ka shitsuraerarete iru.
The remainder of the second floor is divided into dormitory bays for the enlisted men.

会社員は会社で、教師は教室で寝泊りしていた。もっとも、それも寝泊まりできる会社や校舎が残っていればの話であった。
kaishain wa kaisha de, kyoushi wa kyoushitsu de netomari shite ita. motto mo, sore mo netomari dekiru kaisha ya kousha ga nokotte ireba no hanashi de atta.
Employees slept in their offices; teachers, in their schoolrooms—if, of course, they were fortunate enough to still have offices or schoolrooms to sleep in.
