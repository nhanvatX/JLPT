Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 私 (shi)

September 10, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary


[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 私 (shi)]

MEANING:

private, I, me

HOW TO READ:

On: shi
Kun: watakushi, watashi

JLPT LEVEL: N4

COMPOUNDS:

私 (watashi): I, me
私邸 (shitei): private residence
私的 (shiteki): personal, private, proprietary
私立 (shiritsu): private (establishment)
私鉄 (shitetsu): private railway
公私 (koushi): public and private
私有 (shiyuu): private ownership
私学 (shigaku): private school
私設 (shisetsu): private
私案 (shian): private plan
私費 (shihi): private expense
私服 (shifuku): civilian clothes, plain clothes
私見 (shiken): personal opinion
私財 (shizai): private funds, private property
私用 (shiyou): personal use
無私 (mushi): unselfish
私兵 (shihei): private army
私人 (shi’nin): private individual
私欲 (shiyoku): self-interest
私利 (shiri): personal profit
私情 (shijou): personal feelings
滅私 (messhi): selflessness
私語 (shigo): wishpering, whisper, murmur
私大 (shidai): private university
私たち (watashitachi): we, us
私文書 (shibunsho): private document
私生活 (shiseikatsu): one’s private life
私書箱 (shishobako): post office box
私企業 (shikigyou): private enterprise
公平無私 (kouheimushi): impartiality, fair play

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

私はいま、空いた時間にはブリッジをやりたい。
watashi wa ima, aita jikan ni wa burijji o yaritai.
What I now want to do in my spare time is to play bridge.

わが国の憲法は自由制度の中核に私有財産の所有を据えている。
wagakuni no kenpou wa jiyuu seido no chuukaku ni shiyuu zaisan no shoyuu o suete iru.
Our Constitution places the ownership of private property at the very heart of our system of liberty.

このうち宗教セクトと組織犯罪団は、それぞれ自前の私設軍隊を抱えていた。
kono uchi shuukyou sekuto to soshiki hanzaidan wa, sorezore jimae no shisetsu guntai o kakaete ita.
The religious sects and the organized-crime society also had their own private armies.

わたしの意見を説明するにあたって、私的な体験と好きなテレビＣＦの話を聞いてもらいたい。
watashi no iken o setsumei suru ni atatte, shiteki na taiken to suki na terebi shiiefu no hanashi o kiite moraitai.
In order to explain what I mean, let me tell a personal story and share one of my favorite television commercials.

わたしはその名前を知っていた。それは、ニューヨークで一番有名な、一番信用のある私立探偵社の一つである。
watashi wa sono namae o shitte ita. sore wa, nyuuyooku de ichiban yuumei na, ichiban shin’you no aru shiritsu tanteisha no hitotsu de aru.
I knew the name as that of one of the best-known and most reputable private detective agencies in New York.

こうした私的な農作業はとるにたらぬ規模だったから、共産主義体制の怒りを買うことはなかった。
These private agricultural efforts were small enough that they didn’t raise the ire of the Communist authorities.
kou shita shiteki na nousagyou wa toru ni taranu kibo datta kara, kyousan shugi taisei no ikari o kau koto wa nakatta.

中国は私有財産制や銀行などで資本主義の体裁を整え、一見すると資本主義国家のように見える。
China appears to be a capitalist country with private property, banks, and all the other accoutrements of capitalism.
chuugoku wa shiyuu zaisansei ya ginkou nado de shihon shugi no teisai o totonoe, ikken suru to shihon shugi kokka no you ni mieru.

今はまだ漠然としていますが、私たちの知っている事実をすべて解明しつくすことができる結論を見出したのです。
I see, nebulously as yet, a certain explanation that would cover the facts as we know them.
ima wa mada bakuzen to shite imasu ga, watashitachi no shitte iru jijitsu o subete kaimei shitsukusu koto ga dekiru ketsuron o miidashita no desu.

それから彼はつかつかと前へ出て、ギュッと私の手を握りしめ、熱誠こめて私の成功を祝福するといってくれました。
Then suddenly he plunged forward, wrung my hand, and congratulated me warmly on my success.
sore kara kare wa tsukatsuka to mae e dete, gyutto watashi no te o nigirishime, nessei komete watashi no seikou o shukufuku suru to itte kuremashita.

私立学校は、そこにいるだけで自分は特別で特権をあたえられている、という錯覚を育ててし まう場合がある。
Private schools sometimes nurture a sense that just because you are there, you are special and entitled.
shiritsu gakkou wa, soko ni iru dake de jibun wa tokubetsu de tokken o ataerarete iru, to iu sakkaku o sodatete shimau baai ga aru.
