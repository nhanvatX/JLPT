Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 課 (ka)

September 5, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 課 (ka)]

MEANING:

chapter, lesson, section, department, division, counter for chapters (of a book)

HOW TO READ:

On: ka

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

課 (ka): lesson, section, division
課す (kasu): to levy, to charge, to assess
課題 (kadai): subject, theme, issue, matter, task, challenge
課長 (kachou): section manager, section chief
課税 (kazei): taxation
課目 (kamoku): (school) subject, course, curriculum
課外 (kagai): extracurricular
学課 (gakka): lesson, school work
賦課 (fuka): levy, imposition
課業 (kagyou): schoolwork, lessons
課金 (kakin): charges, billing
日課 (nikka): daily lesson, daily work, daily routine
課制 (kasei): section system
課員 (kain): section staff
欠課 (kekka): cutting class, absence from school
考課 (kouka): evaluation, rating
正課 (seika): regular curriculum, required subject
分課 (bunka): subdivision, section, branch
課程 (katei): course, curriculum
課する (kasuru): to charge, to assign, to impose
課徴金 (kachoukin): charges (fees)
放課後 (houkago): after school
研修課 (kenshuuka): training section
修士課程 (shuushikatei): master’s course
課税控除 (kazeikoujo): tax exemption
課税所得 (kazeishotoku): taxable income

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

効果的な課税の実施を妨げるし、さらに一般国民から見た国の合法性を損なう。
koukateki na kazei no jisshi o samatageru shi, sara ni ippan kokumin kara mita kuni no gouhousei o sokonau.
It prevents effective taxation. And it reduces the legitimacy of the state in the eyes of the populace.

これは、わたしがスタンフォード大学で実際に学生に出した課題です。
kore wa, watashi ga sutanfoodo daigaku de jissai ni gakusei ni dashita kadai desu.
This is the assignment I gave students in one of my classes at Stanford University.

これらの困難がソ連の可動兵力に、きわめて窮屈な制約を課していた。
korera no konnan ga soren no kadou heiryoku ni, kiwamete kyuukutsu na seiyaku o kashite ita.
These difficulties set a narrow limit to the forces which the Soviet Union could move and maintain.

アメリカはいまでも、世界のイノベーションを率先して進める原動力です。最高の大学院教育課程、科学のインフラ、そして、それを活かす資本市場があります。
amerika wa ima demo, sekai no inobeeshon o sossen shite susumeru gendouryoku desu. saikou no daigakuin kyouiku katei, kagaku no infura, soshite, sore o ikasu shihon ichiba ga arimasu.
The U.S. is still the leading engine for innovation in the world. It has the best graduate programs, the best scientific infrastructure, and the capital markets to exploit it.

ＶＯＩＰを選ぶ消費者が増えるにつれて競争が激しくなり、時間と距離の単純計算で課金するのは難しくなった。
viiooaipii o erabu shouhisha ga fueru ni tsurete kyousou ga hageshiku nari, jikan to kyori no tanjun keisan de kakin suru no wa muzukashiku natta.
As consumers get more VoIP choices, the competition will be such that telecom companies won’t be able to charge for time and distance much longer.
