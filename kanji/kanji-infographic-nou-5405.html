Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 能 (nou)

July 31, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 能 (nou)]

MEANING:

ability, talent, skill, capacity

HOW TO READ:

On: nou
Kun: yo.ku

COMPOUNDS:

可能 (kanou): potential, possible
能力 (nouryoku): ability, faculty
機能 (kinou): function, facility
才能 (sainou): talent, ability
技能 (ginou): technical skill
不能 (funou): impossible, incompetency
性能 (seinou): ability, efficiency
本能 (honnou): instinct
堪能 (kannou): proficient, skillful
万能 (bannou): all-purpose, utility
能率 (nouritsu): efficiency
芸能 (geinou): public entertainment
知能 (chinou): intelligence, brains
有能 (yuunou): able, capable
無能 (munou): inefficiency, incompetence
可能性 (kanousei): likelihood, possibility
不可能 (fukanou): impossible
放射能 (houshanou): radioactivity
芸能人 (geinoujin): performer
高性能 (kouseinou): high efficiency
知能犯 (chinouhan): intellectual crime
設定可能 (setteikanou): configurable

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

わたしには、五ヶ国語に堪能ないとこがいます。
watashi ni wa, gokakokugo ni kannou na itoko ga imasu.
I have a cousin who is proficient in five languages.

南西には通過不能なヒマラヤ山脈がある。
nansei ni wa tsuuka funou na himaraya sanmyaku ga aru.
To the southwest are the impassable Himalayas.

さて、犯罪人の一等最初の本能はなんでしょう？　自分から嫌疑をそらすこと、そうではないでしょうか？
sate, hanzainin no ittou saisho no honnou wa nan deshou. jibun kara kengi o sorasu koto, sou de wa nai deshou ka.
Now, what is the first instinct of the criminal? To divert suspicion from himself, is it not so?

明確な因果関係を突き止めようとしても、ムダ骨に終わる可能性が高い。
meikaku na inga kankei o tsukitomeyou toshite mo, mudakotsu ni owaru kanousei ga takai.
It may in fact be futile to seek a simplistic causal relationship.

鉄道が町に来ると、まず目につくのは、生産能力の余剰です。
tetsudou ga machi ni kuru to, mazu me ni tsuku no wa, seisan nouryoku no yojou desu.
When the railroad comes to town, the first thing you see is extra capacity.

彼の父親は夢を追いきれなかったインテリといったタイプで、子供たちに、特に才能あふれた長男に、希望を託していた。
kare no chichioya wa yume o oi kirenakatta interi to itta taipu de, kodomotachi ni, toku ni sainou afureta chounan ni, kibou o takushite ita.
His father, something of a frustrated intellectual, was ambitious for his children, especially his talented eldest son.
