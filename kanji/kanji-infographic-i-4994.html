Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 異 (i)

June 2, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 異 (i)]

MEANING:

uncommon, different, queerness, strangeness, wonderful, curious, unusual

HOW TO READ:

On: i
Kun: koto, koto.naru, ke

JLPT LEVEL: N1

COMPOUNDS:

異 (i): strange, odd, unusual
異動 (idou): change
異変 (ihen): unusual event, accident
異議 (igi): objection, dissent, protest
差異 (sai): difference, disparity
特異 (tokui): unique, singular
異常 (ijou): strangeness, abnormality
異論 (iron): different opinion, objection
驚異 (kyoui): wonder, miracle
異様 (iyou): bizarre, strange, odd
異性 (isei): the opposite sex
異例 (irei): exception, illness, singular
異名 (imyou): another name, nickname
異状 (ijou): something wrong, change, abnormality
異国 (ikoku): foreign country
異質 (ishitsu): different (quality, nature)
異文 (ibun): strange, tale
異色 (ishoku): unique, different color
異物 (ibutsu): foreign substance
奇異 (kii): odd, strange, wonderful
異種 (ishu): different kind
相異 (soui): difference, variation
異なる (kotonaru): to differ, to vary
異和感 (iwakan): uncomfortable feeling, sense of discomfort

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

その件数が、テネシー州では異常に多い。
sono kensuu ga, teneshii shuu dewa ijou ni ooi.
A strikingly large proportion of them happen in Tennessee.

親が決めたことに異議をとなえるのは、親不孝である。
oya ga kimeta koto ni igi o tonaeru no wa, oyafukou de aru.
To question a parental decision is considered unfilial.

悪魔にとりつかれたような異様な考えを、彼女はいだいたのだろうか？
akuma ni toritsukareta you na iyou na kangae o, kanojo wa idaita no darou ka.
Had she some fantastic idea of demoniacal possession?

メキシコは、そうしたブラジルやインドのような国とは根本的に異なる。
mekishiko wa, sou shita burajiru ya indo no you na kuni to wa konponteki ni kotonaru.
Mexico is fundamentally different from any of these, like Brazil or India.

私は本能的に、何かの異変が目前にせまっていることを感じていたのである。
watashi wa honnouteki ni, nanika no ihen ga mokuzen ni sematte iru koto o kanjite ita no de aru.
My instinct told me that there were stirring times ahead.

驚異的なのは、検索できる情報の量だ。いや、とうてい検索しきれないのだが、近い将来、もっと賢い検索エンジンのおかげで、増える一方のさまざまなデータをいまよりもずっと楽にふるい分けられるようになるだろう。画像、ビデオ、不動産目録、交通情報、ハイスクールの新聞、病気の治療法。
kyouiteki na no wa, kensaku dekiru jouhou no ryou da. iya, toutei kensaku shikirenai no da ga, chikai shourai, motto kashikoi kensaku enjin no okage de, fueru ippou no samazama na deeta o ima yori mo zutto raku ni furuiwakerareru you ni naru darou. gazou, bideo, fudousan mokuroku, koutsuu jouhou, haisukuuru no shinbun, byouki no chiryouhou.
What is staggering is how much information there is out there—information that was never searchable, but will be in the future, thanks to ever smarter search engines, which will be able to sift through larger and larger mountains of different kinds of data—from pictures to videos to home listings to traffic reports to high school newspapers and health cures.
