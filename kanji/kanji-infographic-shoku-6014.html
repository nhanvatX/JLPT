Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 植 (shoku)

September 4, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 植 (shoku)]

MEANING:

plant

HOW TO READ:

On: shoku
Kun: u.eru, u.waru

JLPT LEVEL: N2

COMPOUNDS:

移植 (ishoku): transplanting, implantation
植木 (ueki): trees, potted plant
植物 (shokubutsu): plant, vegetation
入植 (nyuushoku): settlement, immigration
植樹 (shokuju): tree-planting
植民 (shokumin): colonization
鉢植 (hachiue): potted plant
植付 (uetsuke): planting
植林 (shokurin): afforesetation
植字 (shokuji): typesetting
植える (ueru): to plant, to grow
植わる (uwaru): to be planted
田植え (taue): rice planting
植民地 (shokuminchi): colony, colonial
動植物 (doushokubutsu): plants and animals, flora and fauna
植木屋 (uekiya): gardener
植物園 (shokubutsuen): botanical garden
植物油 (shokubutsuyu): vegetable oil
植木鉢 (uekibashi): flowerpot, plant pot
臓器移植 (zoukiishoku): organ transplant

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

いちばん奥は広い庭で、糸杉や梅が植わっていた。
ichiban oku wa hiroi niwa de, itosugi ya ume ga uwatte ita.
Farther back was a big garden with cypresses and winter plums.

外だって緑があふれているのに、さらに室内にも観葉植物がたくさん置かれている。
soto datte midori ga afurete iru no ni, sara ni shitsunai ni mo kan’you shokubutsu ga takusan okarete iru.
Plants grow everywhere in large plastic pots, as if there isn’t enough greenery outside.

あんたは、昨日の午後、お邸の南側にぐるっと、ベゴニヤの苗床を植えていたんだね？
anta wa, kinou no gogo, oyashiki no minamigawa ni gurutto, begoniya no naedoko o uete itan da ne.
You were planting a bed of begonias round by the south side of the house yesterday afternoon, were you not?

屋根が平たい家では、そこにも植木鉢をのせて野菜の作付け面積を増やすのだった。
yane ga hiratai ie dewa, soko ni mo uekibachi o nosete yasai no sakuzuke menseki o fuyasu no datta.
People whose roofs were flat would put pots up there to grow more vegetables.

井出のことを思い出し、どうすれば彼が十代の息子に必要な肝臓移植を手に入れられるかを懸命に考えた。
ide no koto o omoidashi, dou sureba kare ga juudai no musuko ni hitsuyou na kanzou ishoku o te ni hairerareru ka o kenmei ni kangaeta.
I remembered Ide, trying to figure out how to get his teenage son the liver transplant he needed.

戦後のパリ講和条約は、とりわけ植民地化された国々に民族自決権を付与するものとされた。
sengo no pari kouwa jouyaku wa, toriwake shokuminchika sareta kuniguni ni minzoku jiketsuken o fuyo suru mono to sareta.
The postwar Paris Peace Conference was supposed to grant colonized countries the right of self-determination.
