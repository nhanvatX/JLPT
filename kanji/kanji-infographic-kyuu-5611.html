Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 給 (kyuu)

August 22, 2018   Infographic, Kanji and Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn one Kanji a day with infographic: 給 (kyuu)]

MEANING:

salary, wage, gift, allow, grant, bestow on

HOW TO READ:

On: kyuu
Kun: tama.u, tamo.u, -tama.e

COMPOUNDS:

給与 (kyuuyo): pay, salary, wages
自給 (jikyuu): self-support
給付 (kyuufu): payment, provision, benefit
支給 (shikyuu): provision, supply, allowance
配給 (haikyuu): distribution, rationing
受給 (jukyuu): receiving payments
月給 (gekkyuu): monthly salary
時給 (jikyuu): hourly wage
需給 (jukyuu): supply and demand
給水 (kyuusui): water supply
高給 (koukyuu): high salary
補給 (hokyuu): supply, supplying
給料 (kyuuryou): salary, wages
給油 (kyuuyu): supply of oil
昇給 (shoukyuu): salary raise
給金 (kyuukin): (one’s) pay, wages
無給 (mukyuu): unpaid, nonsalaried
加給 (kakyuu): raising salaries
有給 (yuukyuu): salaried, with pay
本給 (honkyuu): basic salary
恩給 (onkyuu): pension
給費 (kyuuhi): provision of expenses, scholarship
日給 (nikkyuu): daily wages
減給 (genkyuu): pay cut, salary cut
発給 (hakkyuu): issuance
俸給 (houkyuu): salary, pay
給仕 (kyuuji): waiting on a table, serving (at dinner)
給食 (kyuushoku): school lunch, providing a meal
供給 (kyoukyuu): supply, provision
給油所 (kyuuyujo): petrol filling station
初任給 (shoninkyuu): initial salary
供給源 (kyoukyuugen): source of supply
給料日 (kyuuryoubi): payday
供給者 (kyoukyuusha): supplier
受給者 (jukyuusha): recipient of payments, pensioner
自給自足 (jikyuujisoku): self-sufficiency

EXAMPLE SENTENCES:

安い給料で働きますが、移動には金をかけます。
yasui kyuuryou de hatarakimasu ga, idou ni wa kane o kakemasu.
I work cheap and travel expensive.

わたしの小遣いは、給料日前にはゼロになる。
watashi no kozukai wa, kyuuryoubi mae ni wa zero ni naru.
My spending money shrinks down to nothing before payday.

仕事は２～３時間かかったと思う。アルバイト代は時給６００円だった。
shigoto wa ni, san jikan kakatta to omou. arubaito dai wa jikyuu roppyakuen datta.
The job took a couple of hours, and I was paid six hundred yen an hour.

綿花の供給を一時的に止めたものの、供給を再開するすべを失ってしまった。
menka no kyoukyuu o ichijiteki ni tometa mono no, kyoukyuu o saikai suru sube o ushinatte shimatta.
They had turned off the cotton tap, but then lost the ability to turn it back on.

このような福祉制度は、受給者の急増に対処できなかった。
kono you na fukushi seido wa, jukyuusha no kyuuzou ni taisho dekinakatta.
Such a welfare system is ill prepared to cope with a rapid increase in the number of claimants.

中国人労働者の月給の３５ないし４５パーセントが、こうしたヘルスケア費用をまかなうために地元の労務局に支払われる。
chuugokujin roudousha no gekkyuu no sanjuu naishi yonjuugo paasento ga, kou shita herusukea hiyou o makanau tame ni jimoto no roumukyoku ni shiharawareru.
Between 35 and 45 percent of a Chinese worker’s monthly wage goes directly to the local labor bureau to cover these benefits.
