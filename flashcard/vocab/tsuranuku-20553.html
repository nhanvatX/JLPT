Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 貫く (tsuranuku)

December 23, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 貫く (tsuranuku)]

Kana: つらぬく
Kanji: 貫く
Romaji: tsuranuku

Type: Verb
Meaning: to go through; to pierce; to penetrate; to carry out; to persist; to keep (faith); to stick to (principles); to maintain (independence)
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

大統領在職中、彼はこの姿勢を貫いた。
During his presidency, he maintained this attitude.
Daitouryou zaishoku-chuu, kare wa kono shisei o tsuranuita.

彼は最後の最後まで大統領のそばで戦争支持を貫いた。
He remained by the president’s side and supported the war until the very end.
Kare wa saigo no saigo made daitouryou no soba de sensou shiji o tsuranuita.

武力による領土拡大を認めない方針を貫くべきだ。
Japan should stick to a policy of not allowing territorial expansion by force.
Buryoku ni yoru ryoudo kakudai o mitomenai houshin o tsuranuku beki da.

ソ連があらゆる重要な問題について、頑固で強硬な姿勢を貫いてきた。
The Soviet Union has remained stubborn and firm on all important issues.
Soren ga arayuru juuyou na mondai ni tsuite, gankode kyoukou na shisei o tsuranuite kita.

彼は努力を重ね、真面目な生活を貫き、慎重に計画を練り、成功を収めた。
kare wa doryoku o kasane, majime na seikatsu o tsuranuki, shinchou ni keikaku o neri, seikou o osameta.
Through hard work, sober living and careful calculation, he succeeded.

大きな力が必要です。筋肉をつらぬいているのですから。
ooki na chikara ga hitsuyou desu. kinniku o tsuranuite iru no desu kara.
Great strength was needed for those blows. They have penetrated the muscle.

[Ảnh: 貫く (tsuranuku)]

私も、自分の仕事人生で同じ姿勢を貫いてきた。
watashi mo, jibun no shigoto jinsei de onaji shisei o tsuranuite kita.
I tried to maintain the same attitude throughout my career.

私はブラジルとトルコの提案に反対姿勢を貫き、彼に、コムでのごまかしを含むイランの不誠実な行動の数々を指摘した。
watashi wa burajiru to toruko no teian ni hantai shisei o tsuranuki, kare ni, komu de no gomakashi o fukumu iran no fuseijitsu na koudou no kazukazu o shiteki shita.
I walked through our objections to the Brazil-Turkey proposal and reminded him of the long record of Iranian double-dealing, including the deception at Qom.

Chinese: 贯 (guàn)
Korean: 꿰뚫다 (kkwettulhda)
In French: pénétrer
In Spanish: penetrar
In German: durchdringen
In Italian: penetrare
