Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 立場 (tachiba)

January 22, 2016   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 立場 (tachiba)]

Kana: たちば
Kanji: 立場
Romaji: tachiba

Type: noun

Meaning: standpoint; position; situation

Example sentences:


きみはとても不利な立場にあるんだ。
You’re in a lot of trouble.
kimi wa totemo furi na tachiba ni arun da.

人助けができる立場にある。これは気に入る。
I’m in a position to help people, and I like that.

あなただって、わたしの立場になればそうするでしょう。
You’d do the same in my place.
anata datte, watashi no tachiba ni nareba sou suru deshou.

いつだって他人の立場に立って考えるのよね、彼は。
He always puts himself in the other fellow’s shoes.
itsu datte tanin no tachiba ni tatte kangaeru no yo ne, kare wa.

どうやら立場が逆転したようだね？
It looks like the tables have been turned.
douyara tachiba ga gyakuten shita you da ne.

I’d once had complete power over Kazuma, but now the roles had suddenly been reversed.
izen wa Kazuma ni taishite yuui ni tatte ita noni, futari no tachiba wa totsuzen gyakuten shita.

It was nothing. In my place you’d have all done the same thing.

I shall marry whom I please. I think you are forgetting your place.
