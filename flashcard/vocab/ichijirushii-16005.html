Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 著しい (ichijirushii)

November 14, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 著しい (ichijirushii)]

Kana: いちじるしい
Kanji: 著しい
Romaji: ichijirushii

Type: いadjective
Meaning: striking, remarkable, considerable (used for good, bad, and neutral observations)
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

「洋食レオナール」は、移転準備のため１１月末日で一時休業いたします。
“Yoshoku Leonard” will be temporarily closed at the end of November in preparation for relocation.
`Yōshoku reonāru’ wa, iten junbi no tame 11 getsumatsujitsu de ichiji kyūgyō itashimasu.

印刷メディアの重要性は著しく衰えた。
The importance of print media has declined significantly.
Insatsu media no jūyō-sei wa ichijirushiku otoroeta.

ドイツの支払能力が著しく低下していた。
Germany’s severely lost its ability to make payment.
Doitsu no shiharai nōryoku ga ichijirushiku teika shite ita.

この条項は我が国に著しく深刻な問題をもたらす。
This provision poses a very serious problem to our country.
Kono jōkō wa wagakuni ni ichijirushiku shinkokuna mondai o motarasu.

米国の外交にとって、リスクの完全な除去は不可能である。なぜなら米政府が存在する必要があるのは、しばしば安定と安全が著しく欠け、現地政府の支援もほぼ得られない場所だからだ。
beikoku no gaikou ni totte, risuku no kanzen na jokyo wa fukanou de aru. naze nara amerika seifu ga sonzai suru hitsuyou ga aru no wa, shibashiba antei to anzen ga ichijirushiku kake, genchi seifu no shien mo hobo erarenai basho dakara da.
The total elimination of risk is a non-starter for U.S. diplomacy, given the need for the U.S. government to be present in places where stability and security are often most profoundly lacking and host government support is sometimes minimal to non-existent.

やがて、彼ののんきな田舎者のイメージと冷たい理想的な面の著しい相違が、国内メディアの関心を集めた。
yagate, kare no nonki na inakamono no imeeji to tsumetai risouteki na men no ichijirushii soui ga, kokunai media no kanshin o atsumeta.
Soon enough, the contrast between his aw-shucks image and his coldly rational side hit the radar screen of the national press.

ベルリン・ローマ・東京を結ぶ枢軸国側は、これと著しい対照をなしていた。
berurin rooma toukyou o musubu suujiku kokugawa wa, kore to ichijirushii taishou o nashite ita.
In striking contrast was the situation of the Berlin-Rome-Tokyo triangle.

また今後５０年間で人口が著しく減少することが、経済の弱体化に拍車をかけるだろう。
mata kongo gojuu nenkan de jinkou ga ichijirushiku genshou suru koto ga, keizai no jakutaika ni hakusha o kakeru darou.
In addition, its population is going to fall dramatically in the next fifty years, further undermining its economic power.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 显赫 (xiǎnhè)
Korean: 현저하다 (hyeonjeohada)
In French: remarquable
In Spanish: notable
In German: bemerkenswert
In Italian: notevole
