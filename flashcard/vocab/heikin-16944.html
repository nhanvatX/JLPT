Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 平均 (heikin)

November 22, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 平均 (heikin)]

Kana: へいきん
Kanji: 平均
Romaji: heikin

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: average, mean, balance, equilibrium
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

日本の平均年収は４２４万円となっています。
The average annual income in Japan is 4.24 million yen.
Nihon no heikin nenshuu wa yonhyaku nijuu yon man-en to natte imasu.

女性の平均余命が急激に延びている。
Women’s life expectancy has soared.
Josei no heikin yomyou ga kyuugeki ni nobite iru.

うちの娘は各科目の成績評価の平均値「GPA」で4.3を維持した。
My daughter maintained a 4.3 GPA for each subject.
Uchi no musume wa kaku kamoku no seiseki hyouka no heikinchi `jiipiiei’ de yon ten san o iji shita.

労働力の平均教育水準が下がれば、生活水準も下がる。
If you downgrade the average educational level of your workforce, relative to your competition, your standard of living will decline.
roudouryoku no heikin kyouiku suijun ga sagareba, seikatsu suijun mo sagaru.

自分は平均以上だと信じるプロの投資家は増えるいっぽうで、手数料の取り立て屋は分け前を取りつづけ、市場はいままでと変わらず動いている。
Yet the number of individual investors and professional money managers who assumed that they were smarter than average only grew, the toll-takers kept taking their cut, and the market went on as before.
Jibun wa heikin ijou da to shinjiru puro no toushi-ka wa fueru ippou de, tesuuryou no toritate-ya wa wakemae o tori tsudzuke, ichiba wa ima made to kawarazu ugoite iru.

たしかにインドや中国の工学教育は、平均的にいって、アメリカの四年間のきちんとした大学教育の水準には達していないかもしれない。
tashika ni indo ya chuugoku no kougaku kyouiku wa, heikinteki ni itte, amerika no yo’nenkan no kichinto shita daigaku kyouiku no suijun ni wa tasshite inai kamoshirenai.
The average engineering degree in India or China today may not be the same quality as at the average accredited four-year American university.

わたしとちがって人生でめったに失敗をしたことのない平均的な大物政治家が、いま挙げたような感情に襲われたときの衝撃を想像してほしい。
watashi to chigatte jinsei de metta ni shippai o shita koto no nai heikinteki na oomono seijika ga, ima ageta you na kanjou ni osowareta toki no shougeki o souzou shite hoshii.
Imagine the impact of these same emotions on the average big-time politician, who, unlike me, has rarely failed at anything in his life.

[Ảnh: 平均 (heikin)]

この学説の最初の拠り所は、おおぜいが平均をしのごうと競った場合、そこで出た結果が平均になるという断定だった。理屈としては正しくても、まぎれもない真実とはいいきれない論拠である。
kono gakusetsu no saisho no yoridokoro wa, oozei ga heikin o shinogou to kisotta baai, soko de deta kekka ga heikin ni naru to iu dantei datta. rikutsu toshite wa tadashikute mo, magire mo nai shinjitsu to wa iikirenai ronkyo de aru.
These academics had started by positing the reasonable but not necessarily obvious truth that if a whole lot of people were trying to be better than average, they would become the average.

アメリカ、ヨーロッパ、日本の一般市民は、平均すると発展途上国の住民の３２倍の資源を消費し、廃棄物も３２倍生み出している。
amerika, yooroppa, nihon no ippan shimin wa, heikin suru to hatten tojoukoku no juumin no sanjuuni bai no shigen o shouhi shi, haikibutsu mo sanjuuni bai umidashite iru.
On the average, each citizen of the U.S., Western Europe and Japan consumes 32 times more resources, such as fossil fuels, and puts out 32 times more waste, than do inhabitants of the Third World.

In French: moyen
In Spanish: mediano
In German: mittlere
In Italian: medio
