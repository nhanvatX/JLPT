Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 組む (kumu)

December 21, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 組む (kumu)]

Kana: くむ
Kanji: 組む
Romaji: kumu

Type: Verb
Meaning: to cross (legs or arms); to link (arms); to put together; to construct; to assemble; to produce (e.g. TV program); to braid; to plait; to grapple; to wrestle; to unite; to join; to link up; to form an alliance; to set (e.g. type); to issue (e.g. money order)
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

あんたとわたしが手を組めば、息子さんをここから出せます。
anta to watashi ga te o kumeba, musukosan o koko kara dasemasu.
If you and I work together, we may be able to get your son out of here.

彼は両手を頭のうしろで組んでいる。
He has his hands folded behind his head.
Kare wa ryōte o atama nō shiro de kunde iru.

あなたたちは不動産会社と組んで大量のアパートを隠し持っている。
You guys have teamed up with a real estate company and have a lot of apartments hidden away.
Anata-tachi wa fudōsangaisha to kunde tairyō no apāto o kakushimotte iru.

粗末な木のデスクの向こうで、アヤトが長い脚を組んで座っている。
Ayato sits with his long legs crossed behind a rough wooden desk.
Somatsuna ki no desuku no mukō de, Ayato ga nagai ashi o kunde suwatte iru.

あなたの考えておられることは、法律上しかるべき骨組みを組むのに多少の時間がかかります。
It will take some time to put together the appropriate legal structure of what you’re considering.
Anata no kangaete ora reru koto wa, hōritsu-jō shikarubeki honegumi o kumu no ni tashō no jikan ga kakarimasu.

いまから総員配置の戦闘態勢で、規制を撤廃し、無制限の科学・工学教育緊急予算を組んで、船出しなければならない。
ima kara souin haichi no sentou taisei de, kisei o teppai shi, museigen no kagaku kougaku kyouiku kinkyuu yosan o kunde, funade shinakereba naranai.
Therefore, we should be embarking immediately on an all-hands-on-deck, no-holds-barred, no-budget-too-large crash program for science and engineering education.

[Ảnh: 組む (kumu)]

アメリカはあまりにも強力であり、どちらか一方と同盟を組めば、もう一方の敗北が確定する。
amerika wa amari ni mo kyouryoku de ari, dochira ka ippou to doumei o kumeba, mou ippou no haiboku ga kakutei suru.
The United States is so powerful that its alliance with either would mean the defeat of the other.

本当にわたしたちと手を組み暴くつもりなら、いまここに来るのが一番いいはずだと言うんだ。
hontou ni watashitachi to te o kumi abaku tsumori nara, ima koko ni kuru no ga ichiban ii hazu da to iun da.
Tell him that if he really wants to work with us and uncover them, then it would be best if he was here.
