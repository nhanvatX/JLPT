Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 悲惨 (hisan)

November 7, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 悲惨 (hisan)]

Kana: ひさん
Kanji: 悲惨
Romaji: hisan

Type: なadjective
Meaning: disastrous, tragic, miserable, wretched, pitiful, woeful
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

この悲惨な事件の際に、その人はどんなことをしたのかね。
kono hisan na jiken no sai ni, sono hito wa donna koto o shita no ka ne.
What part did he play in this sad drama?

そのような人物を昇進させても、悲惨な結果しか生まれない。
sono you na jinbutsu o shoushin sasete mo, hisan na kekka shika umarenai.
A promotion for someone like that would have made him miserable.

その戦争が悲惨な結果を招いた。
The war had tragic consequences.
Sono sensou ga hisan na kekka o maneita.

その暮らしぶりは悲惨なものだった。
That was a miserable way to live.
Sono kurashi-buri wa hisan na mono datta.

これだって、悲惨な結果になりかねないんだ。
This could end in a horrible way.
Kore datte, hisan na kekka ni nari kanenain da.

あの男は、悲惨な死を迎えてるのだ。本人がそれを正しいと考えたがために、だよ。
That man is going to have a miserable death because he felt it was right.
Ano otoko wa, hisan na shi o mukaeteru no da. Honnin ga sore o tadashii to kangaeta ga tame ni,da yo.

[Ảnh: 悲惨 (hisan)]

自由への扉を開けば、悲惨な歴史を歩んできたあの国に安定と平和がもたらされるのだろうか。
jiyuu e no tobira o hirakeba, hisan na rekishi o ayunde kita ano kuni ni antei to heiwa ga motarasareru no darou ka.
Would opening the door to freedom bring stability and peace to that tragic country?

歴史とは、主として人類の犯罪と愚行と悲惨の記録であるとはよく言われる言葉だ。
rekishi to wa, shu toshite jinrui no hanzai to gukou to hisan no kiroku de aru to wa yoku iwareru kotoba da.
History, which, we are told, is mainly the record of the crimes, follies, and miseries of mankind.

Korean: 비참 (bicham)

In Spanish: desastroso
In German: katastrophal
In Italian: disastroso
