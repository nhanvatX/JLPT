Learn JLPT N4 Vocabulary: 移す (utsusu)

November 28, 2017   Learn Japanese N4 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 移す (utsusu)]

Kana: うつす
Kanji: 移す / 遷す
Romaji: utsusu

Type: Verb
Meaning: to change, to swap, to substitute, to transfer, to change the object of one’s interest or focus, to spend or take time, to infect, to permeate something with the smell or color of something, to move on to the next or different stage of (a plan, etc.)
JLPT level: N4

Example sentences:

タケルは店内に目を移した。
Takeru turned his attention to the inside of the store.
Takeru wa tennai ni me o utsushita.

いつ実行に移したい？今夜か？
When do you want to do it? Tonight?
Itsu jikkō ni utsushitai? Kon’ya ka?

私は夢を描いているあいだにも彼は実行に移した。
While I was dreaming, he put it into action.
Watashi wa yume o kaite iru aida ni mo kare wa jikkō ni utsushita.

その頃には彼らは新宿通りに本社を移していた。
By then, they had moved their headquarters to Shinjuku-dori.
Sonokoro ni wa karera wa Shinjuku-dōri ni honsha o utsushite ita.

霊妙な古の呪文を唱えながら、羊皮紙を三本目の蝋燭にかざし、火を移した。
reimyou na inishie no jumon o tonaenagara, youhishi o sanbonme no rousoku ni kazashi, hi o utsushita.
While chanting an ethereal ancient incantation, I touched the vellum to the third candle, and it burst into flames.

レイノーはチャーチルがこれまでせき立ててきたノルウェー作戦を、すぐさま実施に移すよう英国を促す決意で、ロンドンの連合国最高軍事会議にのぞんだ。
reinoo wa chaachiru ga kore made sekitatete kita noruuee sakusen o, sugusama jisshi ni utsusu you igirisu o unagasu ketsui de, rondon no rengoukoku saikou gunji kaigi ni nozonda.
Reynaud went to London for a meeting of the Allied Supreme War Council, determined to press for the immediate execution of the Norwegian project that Churchill had so long been urging.

[Ảnh: 移す (utsusu)]

連合軍は、ドイツ空軍が別方面へ攻撃の重点を移したのをよいことに、鬼の居ぬ間の洗濯をほしいままにしたのである。
rengou gun wa, doitsu kuugun ga betsu houmen e kougeki no juuten o utsushita no o yoi koto ni, oni no inu aida no sentaku o hoshii mama ni shita no de aru.
The Allied forces enjoyed a temporary advantage as the weight of the German air force had been switched elsewhere.

そうするうちにアメリカの注意を引くことになり、アメリカは必然的にイランを危険な勢力と見なし、イランに関心を移すのである。
sou suru uchi ni amerika no chuui o hiku koto ni nari, amerika wa hitsuzenteki ni iran o kiken na seiryoku to minashi, iran ni kanshin o utsusu no de aru.
In the process, it draws the attention of the United States, which then inevitably focuses on Iran as a dangerous power.
