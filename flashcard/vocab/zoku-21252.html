Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 族 (zoku)

December 29, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 族 (zoku)]

Kana: ぞく
Kanji: 族
Romaji: zoku

Type: Noun
Meaning: tribe, clan, band
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

ヌカク族の人々が集団で、長いこと隔離されていたジャングルから文明社会にさまよい出た。
nukaku zoku no hitobito ga shuudan de, nagai koto kakuri sarete ita janguru kara bunmei shakai ni samayoideta.
The Nukak were a tribe that time forgot, cut off from the rest of humanity.

中国人はアステカ族を知らず、マヤ人はズールー族を知らなかった。
chuugokujin wa asuteka zoku o shirazu, mayajin wa zuuruu zoku o shiranakatta.
The Chinese didn’t know of the Aztecs, and the Mayas didn’t know of the Zulus.

[Ảnh: 族 (zoku)]

アパッチ族は押し寄せる合衆国軍に抵抗し、最後まで降伏しなかった部族だ。
apacchi zoku wa oshiyoseru gasshuukoku gun ni teikou shi, saigo made koufuku shinakatta buzoku da.
The Apache tribe was the last tribe to surrender to the oncoming U.S. government.

ユタ州とアリゾナ州にまたがるナバホ族インディアン居留地の中にあり、その独特な風景は西部開拓時代を描いた映画にしばしば登場してきた。
yuta shuu to arizona shuu ni matagaru nabaho zoku indian kyoryuuchi no naka ni ari, sono dokutoku na fuukei wa seibu kaitaku jidai o kaita eiga ni shibashiba toujou shite kita.
Located within a Navajo reservation that straddles the border between the states of Utah and Arizona, it is a unique landscape that has often been featured in movies about the pioneering era of the American West.

Korean: 족 (jog)
