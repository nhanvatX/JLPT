Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 銃 (juu)

November 12, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 銃 (juu)]

Kana: じゅう
Kanji: 銃
Romaji: juu

Type: Noun
Meaning: gun, rifle, small arms
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

その銃をよこしなさい。
Give me the gun.
Sono juu o yokoshi nasai.

手のなかで銃が熱くなりはじめた。
The gun was growing hot in my hands.
Te no naka de juu ga atsuku nari hajimeta.

この男が銃をつかうのは躊躇しない。
This man won’t mind using his gun.
Kono otoko ga juu o tsukau no wa chuucho shinai.

彼は床に手を伸ばし、銃をつかんだ。
He reached down and grabbed the gun.
Kare wa yuka ni te o nobashi, juu o tsukanda.

家の地下でコンクリートブロックに向けて撃ち、銃のぐあいを確認するのが、僕のお気に入りの仕事だった。
ie no chika de konkuriito burokku ni mukete uchi, juu no guai o kakunin suru no ga, boku no oki ni iri no shigoto datta.
My favorite job was testing the guns by firing them into cinder blocks in the family’s basement.

銃も携帯電話も禁止？銃はわかるが、なぜ携帯電話まで？
juu mo keitai denwa mo kinshi. juu wa wakaru ga, naze keitai denwa made.
No guns or cell phones allowed? Guns I understand, but why cell phones?

[Ảnh: 銃 (juu)]

第一波か二波がライフル銃で向かってくる。そのすぐ後ろにはライフルを持たない兵が控えていて倒れた兵の武器を拾って攻めてくる。
daiichi nami ka ni nami ga raifuru juu de mukatte kuru. sono sugu ushiro ni wa raifuru o motanai hei ga hikaete ite taoreta hei no buki o hirotte semete kuru.
The first wave or two would come at you with rifles, and right behind them were soldiers without rifles ready to pick up the weapons of those who had fallen and keep coming.

中国兵の多くは軽機銃で武装していた。
chuugokuhei no ooku wa keikijuu de busou shite ita.
Many of the Chinese were armed with light machine guns.

In French: pistolet
In Spanish: pistola
