Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 傾向 (keikou)

December 21, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 傾向 (keikou)]

Kana: けいこう
Kanji: 傾向
Romaji: keikou

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: tendency, trend, inclination
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

彼はすぐ思いあがる傾向がありました。
He was inclined to think a lot of himself.
Kare wa sugu omoi agaru keikou ga arimashita.

世間の人々は、隣近所のことに興味をもちすぎる傾向があるようですね。
I am afraid one learns to take too much interest in one’s neighbors’ affairs.
Seken no hitobito wa, tonari kinjo no koto ni kyoumi o mochi sugiru keikou ga aru you desu ne.

部族社会は定住する傾向があり、畑や牧畜地や漁場の近くにつくった村でほぼ一年中生活する。
Tribes tend to be sedentary, and to live for much or all of the year in villages located near their gardens, pastures, or fisheries.
Buzoku shakai wa teijuu suru keikou ga ari, hata ya bokuchikuchi ya ryouba no chikaku ni tsukutta mura de hobo ichinenjuu seikatsu suru.

勤労や投資よりも消費を、長期的よりも目先の満足を礼賛する傾向が強くなっている。
That is a pronounced tendency in recent years to extol consumption over hard work and investment, immediate gratification over long-term thinking and sacrifice.
Kinrou ya toushi yori mo shouhi o, chouki-teki yori mo mesaki no manzoku o raisan suru keikou ga tsuyoku natte iru.

こういった傾向はたいていの人間が、すこぶる賢い人間でさえ生まれつき持ちあわせているもので、竹之内だけに限ったことではない。
kou itta keikou wa taitei no ningen ga, sukoburu kashikoi ningen de sae umaretsuki mochi awasete iru mono de, takenouchi dake ni kagitta koto de wa nai.
This habit, however, is characteristic of a very great number of people, some of them very clever ones, not like Takenouchi.

ポートフォリオはそれぞれに違う。初期のころは便乗する傾向がある程度あったが、ほとんど自分の判断で投資を行ってきた。
pootoforio wa sorezore ni chigau. shoki no koro wa binjou suru keikou ga aru teido atta ga, hotondo jibun no handan de toushi o itte kita.
Their portfolios were not similar; despite a certain amount of coat-tailing in the early years, they had largely invested on their own.

[Ảnh: 傾向 (keikou)]

湾岸諸国歴訪の際、私がこうした傾向を指摘して、波紋を呼んだこともあった。
wangan shokoku rekihou no sai, watashi ga kou shita keikou o shiteki shite, hamon o yonda koto mo atta.
I made some waves when I noted this trend during a trip to the Gulf.

方針変更を声高に求めれば求めるほど、彼らはより頑なになる傾向があった。
houshin henkou o kowadaka ni motomereba motomeru hodo, karera wa yori kataku na ni naru keikou ga atta.
The more loudly we urged them to change course, the more likely they were to dig in their heels.
