Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: たどり着く (tadoritsuku)

December 23, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: たどり着く (tadoritsuku)]

Kana: たどりつく
Kanji: たどり着く / 辿り着く / 辿りつく
Romaji: tadoritsuku

Type: Verb
Meaning: to arrive at (after a struggle), to finally reach, to find one’s way to, to finally hit on (e.g. an idea)
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

夕べ、大陸の反対側の海岸にたどり着いた。
Last night we arrived on the shore of the other ocean.
Yuube, tairiku no hantaisoku no kaigan ni tadoritsuita.

男の子は自力で捜索隊のキャンプ場にたどりついた。
The boy made his way to the search party’s campsite on his own.
Otoko no ko wa jiriki de sousaku-tai no kyanpuba ni tadoritsuita.

彼らは二百メートルほど移動して、環状交差点にたどり着いた。
After traveling about 200 meters, they reached a roundabout.
Karera wa ni hyaku meetoru hodo idou shite, kanjou kousaten ni tadoritsuita.

私が彼を捕まえ、盗聴防止用の電話にたどり着くまでにどれぐらいの時間がかかるかを尋ねたところ、彼は少なくとも三時間はかかると言った。
When I reached him and asked how long it would take to get to a secure line, he said at least three hours.
Watashi ga kare o tsukamae, touchou boushi-you no denwa ni tadoritsuku made ni dore gurai no jikan ga kakaru ka o tazuneta tokoro, kare wa sukunakutomo san jikan wa kakaru to itta.

米軍の前線にたどり着こうとして、少なくとも一週間、たぶん十日はさまよっていたろう。痛みは間断なくつづき、猛烈な飢えを感じた。
beigun no zensen ni tadoritsukou to shite, sukunakutomo isshuukan, tabun tooka wa samayotte itarou. itami wa kandan naku tsuzuki, mouretsu na ue o kanjita.
I was out there at least a week, maybe ten days, trying to work my way back to American lines, in constant pain and voraciously hungry.

インド人、ロシア人、ポーランド人、中国人の若者が、均された競技場にようやくたどり着いたときに、世界のために参加はあきらめて消費を抑えてくれなどということは言えない。
indojin, roshiajin, poorandjin, chuugokujin no wakamono ga, narasareta kyougiba ni youyaku tadoritsuita toki ni, sekai no tame ni sanka wa akiramete shouhi o osaete kure nado to iu koto wa ienai.
One thing we will not be able to do is tell young Indians, Russians, Poles, or Chinese that just when they are arriving on the leveled playing field, they have to hold back and consume less for the greater global good.

その結果、第一回目の会議では予算問題を話し合うところまではまったくたどり着けなかった。
sono kekka, dai ikkaime no kaigi de wa yosan mondai o hanashiau tokoro made wa mattaku tadoritsukenakatta.
As a result we did not get around to talking about the budget at all at our first session.

おまえがそのくらいのことに辿り着くのも計算済みだ。
omae ga sono kurai no koto ni tadoritsuku no mo keisanzumi da.
I expected you to figure out this much.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 终于到达 (zhōngyú dàodá)
Korean: 당도하다 (dangdohada)
In French: arriver
In Spanish: llegar
In German: ankommen
In Italian: arrivare
