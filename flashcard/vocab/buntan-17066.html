Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 分担 (buntan)

November 23, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 分担 (buntan)]

Kana: ぶんたん
Kanji: 分担
Romaji: buntan

Type: Noun, のadjective, するverb
Meaning: taking on one’s share (e.g. of work), dividing (work, expenses, etc.) between, apportionment, allotment, allocation, assignment
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

妻は家事を完全に分担したがる。
The wife likes to share housework completely.
Tsuma wa kaji o kanzen ni buntan shitagaru.

最初から家事分担することにしていました。
From the beginning, I decided to share housework.
Saisho kara kaji buntan suru koto ni shite imashita.

あの男は仕事の分担方法を心得てる。
That man knows how to delegate.
Ano otoko wa shigoto no buntan houhou o kokoroe teru.

ガソリン代の一部を分担し、また運転を交換する用意もあります。
I’ll share part of the expenses driving.
Gasorin-dai no ichibu o buntan shi, mata unten o koukan suru youi mo arimasu.

あなたはどうも自分の分担以上にかついでいるようだ、トム。今度荷物を下ろしたら、中を見せてもらうことにしよう。
anata wa doumo jibun no buntan ijou ni katsuide iru you da, tomu. kondo nimotsu o oroshitara, naka o misete morau koto ni shiyou.
I suspect you have taken more than your share, Tom, and I shall look into it at our next packing.

やらなければならない仕事だし、私ひとりでは荷が重すぎる。だから、皆にも分担してほしいと頼んだ。
yaranakereba naranai shigoto da shi, watashi hitori de wa ni ga omosugiru. dakara, minna ni mo buntan shite hoshii to tanonda.
I still had to do it, and it was too heavy a load for me to lift alone. I asked them to carry more of it.

[Ảnh: 分担 (buntan)]

イギリスが欧州共同体において大きな影響力を行使し積極的な役割を果たしたいと願っても、イギリスにとってははなはだしく不公平な予算分担の問題が解決されるまでは、そうできないのだ。
igirisu ga oushuu kyoudoutai ni oite ooki na eikyouryoku o koushi shi sekkyokuteki na yakuwari o hatashitai to negatte mo, igirisu ni totte wa hanahadashiku fukouhei na yosan buntan no mondai ga kaiketsu sareru made wa, sou dekinai no da.
Although Britain wants to play a vigorous and influential role in the European Community, we can not do so until the problem of our grossly unfair budgetary contribution has been resolved.

この早期の段階でも、ＥＣとの論争の的は予算分担額だけではなかった。
kono souki no dankai demo, iishii to no ronsou no mato wa yosan buntangaku dake de wa nakatta.
Our budget contribution was not the only source of contention within the EC, even in those early days.
