Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 以降 (ikou)

November 14, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 以降 (ikou)]

Kana: いこう
Kanji: 以降
Romaji: ikou

Type: Noun
Meaning: on and after, as from, hereafter, thereafter, since
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

９・１１以降、外国の多くの学生が、ビザの取得が困難なためにアメリカで教育を受けるのを考えなおすようになった。
kyuu juuichi ikou, gaikoku no ooku no gakusei ga, biza no shutoku ga konnan na tame ni amerika de kyouiku o ukeru no o kangae naosu you ni natta.
Since 9/11 a lot of foreign students were having second thoughts about studying in the States due to the difficulties in obtaining visas.

１８８０年代以降、わが国の産業の優位は最初はアメリカ、次にドイツとの競争に直面した結果、着実に失われていた。
sen hachihyaku hachi juu nendai ikou, wagakuni no sangyou no yuui wa saisho wa amerika, tsugi ni doitsu to no kyousou ni chokumen shita kekka, chakujitsu ni ushinawarete ita.
Beginning in the 1880s, our industrial supremacy had been steadily eroding in the face of first American, then German competition.

[Ảnh: 以降 (ikou)]

とくに１７１７年に金本位制が導入されて以降、貨幣価値の切り下げはおこなわれなくなった。
toku ni sen nanahyaku juu nana nen ni kinhon’isei ga dounyuu sarete ikou, kahei kachi no kirisage wa okonawarenaku natta.
There would be no more debasement of the coinage, particularly after the adoption of the gold standard in 1717.

１９世紀半ばの南米戦争以降、アメリカは並はずれた経済成長を遂げた。
juukyuu seiki nakaba no nanbei sensou ikou, amerika wa nami hazureta keizai seichou o togeta.
Ever since the Civil War, the United States has been on an extraordinary economic surge.

オーストラリア政府が石炭の産出・輸出を２０３０年以降も継続すると表明した。
The Australian Government has announced that it will continue to produce and export coal after 2030.
oosutoraria seifu ga sekitan no sanshutsu yushutsu o nisen sanjuu nen ikou mo keizoku suru to hyoumei shita.

グーグルは、従業員のオフィス復帰を来年1月以降に再度延期すると明らかにした。
Google has announced that it will postpone its return-to-office plan again after January next year.
Guuguru wa, juugyouin no ofisu fukki o rainen ichigatsu ikou ni saido enki suru to akiraka ni shita.

国民投票以降、英国の小売業界は６万５０００人が職を失った。
Since the referendum, 65,000 people have lost their jobs in the UK retail industry.
Kokumin touhyou ikou, igirisu no kouri gyoukai wa roku man gosen nin ga shoku o ushinatta.

英国からの旅行客は来年1月1日以降、欧州連合加盟国への入国を禁じられる可能性がある。
Travelers from the United Kingdom may be banned from entering the European Union after January 1st next year.
Igirisu kara no ryokou kyaku wa rainen ichigatsu tsuitachi ikou, oushuu rengou kameikoku e no nyuukoku o kinjirareru kanousei ga aru.

１９５０以降に生まれた人の三分の一以上が、近代医薬品の恩恵で命を長らえているんです。
More than one third of the people born since nineteen fifty are alive only because of modern drugs.
sen kyuuhyaku gojuu ikou ni umareta hito no sanbun no ichi ijou ga, kindai iyakuhin no onkei de inochi o nagaraete irun desu.
