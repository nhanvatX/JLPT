Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 面倒 (mendou)

November 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary with Flashcard]

Kana: めんどう
Kanji: 面倒
Romaji: mendou

Type: Noun; adjective-na

Meaning: troublesome; trouble; difficulty; care; attention

Idiom:

面倒を見る (mendou o miru): to care for someone; to look after someone

Example sentences:


悪いけど、あとは一緒に行って、あの子の面倒を見てやってね！
I’m sorry, can you go with him and take care of him for me?
warui kedo, ato wa issho ni itte, ano ko no mendou o mite yatte ne.

子供の頃からいつも面倒みてくれた。
You’ve always looked out for me ever since we were kids.
kodomo no koro kara itsumo mendou mite kureta.

僕に代わってリサの面倒を見てくれないか？
Will you look after Lisa for me?
boku ni kawatte risa no mendou o mite kurenai ka.

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 面倒 (mendou)]

今、あれやこれや面倒なことを考えるのはよそう。
I won’t think of this or that bothersome thought right now.
ima, are ya kore ya mendou na koto o kangaeru no wa yosou.

わたしの面倒を見るってジムに約束してくれたのでしょう。
You promised Jim you’d take care of me.
watashi no mendou o miru tte jimu ni yakusoku shite kureta no deshou.

祖母は誰に面倒を見てもらうの？
Who’s going to take care of my grandmother?
sobo wa dare ni mendou o mite morau no.

ぼくは自分で自分の面倒を見ることぐらいはできますよ。
I can look after myself.
boku wa jibun de jibun no mendou o miru koto gurai wa dekimasu yo.

わたしが言ってるのは、だれがあなたの面倒を見てくれるのかということよ。
I mean who’s going to look after you?
watashi ga itteru no wa, dare ga anata no mendou o mite kureru no ka to iu koto yo.

いっしょに連れてって、そうしたらあなたの面倒みてあげられるから。
Take me with you, please, I’ll take care of you.
issho ni tsuretette, sou shitara anata no mendou mite agerareru kara.

Similar words:

厄介 (yakkai); 悩み (nayami); 保護 (hogo); 心配 (shinpai)

Meaning in other languages:

Korean: 귀찮다 (gwichanhda); …을 돌보다 (…eul dolboda)
In French: les soins
In Spanish: el cuidado
