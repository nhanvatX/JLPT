Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 忘れる (wasureru)

October 29, 2015   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Hình ảnh đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]
Kana: わすれる
Kanji: 忘れる
Romaji: wasureru

Type: Verb

Meaning: to forget; to be forgetful; to leave carelessley

Example sentences:


昨日電車に鞄を忘れました。
I forgot my bag on the train yesterday.
kinou densha ni kaban o wasuremashita.

どんなことがあっても、僕の今の言葉を忘れないで。
No matter what happens, don’t forget what I just said.
donna koto ga attemo, boku no ima no kotoba o wasurenaide.

いけない！ピアノのレッスンがあるのを忘れてた。
Oh no! I forgot that I had piano lessons.
ikenai. piano no resson ga aru no o wasureteta.

That’s not something I can forget even if I wanted to.

How many years it’s been? I already forgot when was the last time we met.

ぼくは子供のころのことをぜんぶ忘れていたんだよ。
I’d forgotten everything about being a kid.
boku wa kodomo no koro no koto o zenbu wasurete itan da yo.

あんなことはみんなまた忘れられればいい。
If I could only forget it all again.
anna koto wa minna mata wasurerarereba ii.

わたしがいま言ったことは、忘れてください。
Please forget what I have just said.
watashi ga ima itta koto wa, wasurete kudasai.
