Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 低下 (teika)

November 22, 2017   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 低下 (teika)]

Kana: ていか
Kanji: 低下
Romaji: teika

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: fall, decline, lowering, deterioration, degradation
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

小学校教員の学力が低下していると思う。
I think the academic ability of elementary school teachers is declining.
Shougakkou kyouin no gakuryoku ga teika shite iru to omou.

そのことは為替レートの引き上げにも役立つ金利の低下に重点を置くことを意味するのだ。
This meant giving priority to a reduction in interest rates, which would also help get down the exchange rate.
Sono koto wa kawase rēto no hikiage ni mo yakudatsu kinri no teika ni jūtenwooku koto o imi suru noda.

その目的もやはり住宅ローンの流通市場を拡大することで、少なくとも理論上は住宅ローンの金利を低下させることを目指した。
The effect was once again to broaden the secondary market for mortgages, and in theory at least to lower mortgage rates.
Sono mokuteki mo yahari jūtaku rōn no ryūtsū ichiba o kakudai suru koto de, sukunakutomo riron-jō wa jūtaku rōn no kinri o teika sa seru koto o mezashita.

このまま時が流れ、進歩が続けば、破壊的な出来事が起きる確率は急激に低下すると思う。
I believe that as time and progress go on there, the chance for a really disruptive event goes down exponentially.
Kono mama toki ga nagare, shinpo ga tsudzukeba, hakai-tekina dekigoto ga okiru kakuritsu wa kyūgeki ni teika suru to omou.

ヨルダン川西岸の失業率はガザ地区の半分以下に低下した。
yorudan gawa seigan no shitsugyou ritsu wa gaza chiku no hanbun ika ni teika shita.
Unemployment in the West Bank fell to less than half the rate in Gaza.

いずれにせよ、我々の関係は二度と元に戻らず、中東における我々の影響力は低下するだろう。
izure ni seyo, wareware no kankei wa nido to moto ni modorazu, chuutou ni okeru wareware no eikyouryoku wa teika suru darou.
Either way, our relationship will never be the same and our influence in the Middle East will erode.

[Ảnh: 低下 (teika)]

州が運営する通常の失業保険は、労働者の痛みを若干は和らげるが、転職先での賃金の低下や、職探しのあいだ医療保険料が払えないという最大の不安に応えていない。
shuu ga un’ei suru tsuujou no jitsugyou hoken wa, roudousha no itami o jakkan wa yawarageru ga, tenshokusaki de no chingin no teika ya, shokusagashi no aida iryou hoken ryou ga haraenai to iu saidai no fuan ni kotaete inai.
The standard state-run unemployment insurance program eases some of this pain for workers, but it does not address their bigger concerns of declining wages in a new job and the inability to pay for health insurance while they are unemployed and searching.

イギリスの経済的地位が低下したことは、われわれの国際的役割も締め付けられ、苦しめられるのが必至であることを意味した。
igirisu no keizaiteki chii ga teika shita koto wa, wareware no kokusaiteki yakuwari mo shimetsukerare, kurushimerareru no ga hisshi de aru koto o imi shita.
Britain’s weakened economic position meant that its international role was bound to be cramped and strained as well.
