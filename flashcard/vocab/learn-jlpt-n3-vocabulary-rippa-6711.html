Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 立派 (rippa)

January 6, 2016   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 立派 (rippa)]

Kana: りっぱ
Kanji: 立派
Romaji: rippa

Type: adjective-na

Meaning: splendid; fine; handsome; elegant; imposing; prominent; praiseworthy; legal; legitimate

Example sentences:


やっぱりお前ってすごい立派な兄だよなあ。
You really are a great big brother, you know?
yappari omae tte sugoi rippa na ani da yo naa.
