Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 年間 (nenkan)

November 20, 2017   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 年間 (nenkan)]

Kana: ねんかん
Kanji: 年間
Romaji: nenkan

Type: Noun
Meaning: (period of) a year, during an era
JLPT level: N5

Example sentences:

その後の三十年間、彼はファラオのように絶対的な権力でエジプトを支配していた。
sono ato no sanjuu nenkan, kare wa farao no you ni zettaiteki na kenryoku de ejiputo o shihai shite ita.
He ruled Egypt like a pharaoh with nearly absolute power for the next three decades.

国務長官を務めた４年間、海外にもかなり行ったが、前任の人たちに比べれば少ないほうだったし、後任の人たちとは比べるべくもないほどだった。
kokumu choukan o tsutometa yo’nenkan, kaigai ni mo kanari itta ga, zennin no hitotachi ni kurabereba sukunai hou datta shi, kounin no hitotachi to wa kuraberu beku mo nai hodo datta.
In my four years as Secretary I traveled a great deal, but not as much as some of my predecessors and nowhere near as much as my successors.

[Ảnh: 年間 (nenkan)]

これまでの２０年間のことは、注意深く検討する価値がある。
kore made no nijuu nenkan no koto wa, chuui bukaku kentou suru kachi ga aru.
It is important to scrutinize the last twenty years.

年間で数百万にもおよぶ文書が、拠点から本庁、本庁から拠点、拠点同士、その他の形でやりとりされていた。
nenkan de suuhyaku man ni mo oyobu bunsho ga, kyoten kara honchou, honchou kara kyoten, kyoten doushi, sono hoka no katachi de yaritori sarete ita.
Millions of these documents are sent every year by posts to headquarters, by headquarters to posts, between posts, and so on.

彼が一年間にいくら稼ぐと思う？
Do you know how much he makes a year?
Kare ga ichinenkan ni ikura kasegu to omou?

ママのすべてを非難して、五年間ほとんど口をきかなかった。
I criticized my mom for everything, and I didn’t say more than a few words to her for five years!
Mama no subete o hinan shite, go-nenkan hotondo kuchi o kikanakatta.

二人はもうかれこれ二十年間も幸せな結婚生活を送っている。
They have been happily married for almost twenty years.
Futari wa mou karekore ni juu-nenkan mo shiawasena kekkon seikatsu o okutte iru.

わたしは二年間も彼の秘書をやってきたので、彼のことは表も裏もよく知ってるんだ。
You see, I knew him so well. I’d been his secretary for two years.
Watashi wa ni-nenkan mo kare no hisho o yatte kita node, kare no koto wa omote mo ura mo yoku shitterun da.

ここ三年間、祖父は、金融利率が下がるものと確信していました。
For the last three years, my grandfather was convinced that interest rates were going to fall.
Koko san-nenkan, sofu wa, kin’yuu riritsu ga sagaru mono to kakushin shite imashita.
