Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 妥当 (datou)

November 14, 2017   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 妥当 (datou)]

Kana: だとう
Kanji: 妥当
Romaji: datou

Type: Noun, なadjective
Meaning: valid, proper, right, appropriate, reasonable
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

これは妥当な数字と言えるだろう。
That seems reasonable enough.
Kore wa datōna sūji to ierudarou.

この格言はいまも妥当性を失っていない。
This adage still holds true.
Kono kakugen wa ima mo datōsei o ushinatte inai.

その時、まさにご自身が下された判断が今はもう妥当ではないと思われるのはなぜですか？
Sono toki, masani go jishin ga kudasareta handan ga ima wa mō datōde wa nai to omowa reru no wa nazedesu ka?
Sonotoki, masani go jishin ga kudasa reta handan ga ima wa mō datōde wa nai to omowa reru no wa nazedesu ka?

われわれは、各省予算を妥当な範囲内に抑える必要性と、それが公共部門の賃金に及ぼす影響の証拠を委員会に提出することにした。
We decided to submit evidence to the Commission about the necessity of keeping departmental budgets within reasonable limits and what that meant for public sector pay.
Wareware wa, kakushō yosan o datōna han’inaini osaeru hitsuyō-sei to, sore ga kōkyō bumon no chingin ni oyobosu eikyō no shōko o iinkai ni teishutsu suru koto ni shita.

一番妥当な解釈でしょうね。ですが、実をいうと、わたしは、あなたがお受けとりになった、例のひどく妙な手紙というのを、ぜひもう一度拝見したいと思うんです。
ichiban datou na kaishaku deshou ne. desu ga, mi o iu to, watashi wa, anata ga ouketori ni natta, rei no hidoku myou na tegami to iu no o, zehi mou ichido haiken shitai to omoun desu.
It seems the most likely solution. But I must confess, sir, I’d like to have another look at that very odd letter you received.

この時点でこれを検討するのが妥当だと思っているようなら、きみはこの依頼人の弁護士として失格だな。
kono jiten de kore o kentou suru no ga datou da to omotte iru you nara, kimi wa kono iraijin no bengoshi to shite shikkaku da na.
As a lawyer, you are misrepresenting your own client if you think it makes sense to get into any of those issues at this time.

[Ảnh: 妥当 (datou)]

第二次世界大戦の犠牲者数は、全世界で軍人と民間人合わせて約５０００万人が妥当な推計とされる。
dainiji sekai taisen no giseishasuu wa, zen sekai de gunjin to minkanjin awasete yaku gosen man nin ga datou na suikei to sareru.
A reasonable estimate of World War II’s cost to the world was about fifty million dead.

それが一番妥当な解釈でしょうね。
sore ga ichiban datou na kaishaku deshou ne.
That’s the explanation that fits best.

Korean: 타당 (tadang)
In French: valide
In Spanish: válido
In German: gültig
In Italian: valido
