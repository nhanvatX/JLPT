Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 答える (kotaeru)

October 29, 2015   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Hình ảnh đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]
Kana: こたえる
Kanji: 答える
Romaji: kotaeru

Type: Verb

Meaning: to answer; to reply

Example sentences:


正直に答えろ！
Answer me honestly!
shoujiki ni kotaeru.

テキストを見ずに答えなさい。
Please answer without looking at the textbook.
tekisuto o mizu ni kotae nasai.

聞かれたことに答えればいいだけだろ。
Just answer whatever questions we’re asked.
kikareta koto ni kotaereba ii dake daro.

わたしは答えなかった。
I did not answer.
watashi wa kotaenakatta.

いいから質問に答えるんだ。
Just answer the question.
ii kara shitsumon ni kotaerun da.

アツシは答えるのにしばらく手間取った。
Atsushi took a moment or two to answer.

Please don’t answer if you don’t want to.

何でも聞いて！ちゃんと答える！
Just ask me anything! I’ll answer all your questions!
nandemo kiite. chanto kotaeru.

彼女はなんと答えてよいのやら分からなかったのでただ黙っていた。
She couldn’t think of any reply to this, so she remained silent.
kanojo wa nanto kotaete yoi no yara wakaranakatta node tada damatte ita.

一瞬私は、彼は答えないつもりではないかと思った。
For a moment I thought he wasn’t going to answer.
isshun watashi wa, kare wa kotaenai tsumori dewa nai ka to omotta.

[Audio MP3 — Đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]

 

彼は私たちの訊問にまことにすらすらと答えたですよ。
He answered our questions quite satisfactorily.
kare wa watashitachi no jinmon ni makoto ni surasura to kotaeta desu yo.

[Audio MP3 — Đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]

 

何時間も外で待たせたあげく、こっちの質問に一言も答えてくれないのか？
You made me wait out here for hours and you won’t even give a proper answer to my question?
nanjikan mo soto de mataseta ageku, kocchi no shitsumon ni hitokoto mo kotaete kurenai no ka.
