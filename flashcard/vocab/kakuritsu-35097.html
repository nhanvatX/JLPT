Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 確率 (kakuritsu)

January 7, 2024   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary


Kanji: 確率
Kana: かくりつ
Romaji: kakuritsu

Meaning: probability; likelihood; chances
Type: Noun
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

捕まる確率は低かった。
The chance of getting caught was remote.
Tsukamaru kakuritsu wa hikukatta.

それ以後、二国間に外交関係が確率された。
Since then, diplomatic relations have been established between the two countries.
Sore igo, ni kuni-kan ni gaikō kankei ga kakuritsu sa reta.

上院における共和党の議席数は４４だったから、医療制度改革法案を単独で提出すれば、フィリバスターを仕掛けてくる確率は高いだろう。
Since the Senate had forty-four Republicans, the probability that they would at least try to filibuster health care was high.
Jōin ni okeru kyōwatō no giseki-sū wa 44dattakara, iryō seido kaikaku hōan o tandoku de teishutsu sureba, firibasutā o shikakete kuru kakuritsu wa takaidarou.

１８世紀から１９世紀にかけて、遅ればせながら人々は降雨量や収穫高、死亡率を系統的に記録し、確率計算をやり始めた。
Only very belatedly – in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries – did they begin systematically to record rainfall, harvests and mortality in a way that made probabilistic calculation possible.
18 Seiki kara 19 seiki ni kakete, okurebasenagara hitobito wa kōu-ryō ya shūkaku-kō, shibō-ritsu o keitōteki ni kiroku shi, kakuritsu keisan o yari hajimeta.

人々が遭遇するほかの多くのリスクのほうが、ハリケーンよりはるかに確率が正確に計算できる。ハリケーンよりもひんぱんに起きるため、統計的パターンが明確だからだ。
Such probabilities can be calculated with greater precision for most of the other risks that people face mainly because they are more frequent, so statistical patterns are easier to discern.
Hitobito ga sōgū suru hoka no ōku no risuku no hō ga, harikēn yori haruka ni kakuritsu ga seikaku ni keisan dekiru. Harikēn yori mo hinpan ni okiru tame, tōkei-teki patān ga meikakudakarada.

Meaning in other languages:

In French: probabilité
In Spanish: probabilidad
In German: Wahrscheinlichkeit
In Italian: probabilità
