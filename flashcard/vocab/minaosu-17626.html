Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 見直す (minaosu)

November 28, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 見直す (minaosu)]

Kana: みなおす
Kanji: 見直す
Romaji: minaosu

Type: Verb
Meaning: to look again, to re-examine (policy, estimate, plan, etc.), to review, to get a better opinion of, to see something in a more positive light, to improve, to recover (market, illness, etc.)
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

議論に入る前に、配布物を一緒に見直したいと思います。
giron ni hairu mae ni, haifumono o issho ni minaoshitai to omoimasu.
Before we start the discussion, I’d like us to go over the handout together.

そういう恩返しの心がまだ健在だと知って、わたしも人間の本性を見直したよ。
sou iu ongaeshi no kokoro ga mada kenzai da to shitte, watashi mo ningen no honshou o minaoshita yo.
It renews my faith in human nature to know that gratitude still exists.

[Ảnh: 見直す (minaosu)]

われわれはこれには同意したが、肥大する社会保障予算はもう一度見直すことを決めた。
wareware wa kore ni wa doui shita ga, hidai suru shakai hoshou yosan wa mou ichido minaosu koto o kimeta.
We agreed but decided that we would have to take another look at the the burgeoning social security budget.

アメリカが国内の政治的圧力から、環太平洋地域との経済関係を見直す可能性がある。
amerika ga kokunai no seijiteki atsuryoku kara, kantai heiyou chiiki to no keizai kankei o minaosu kanousei ga aru.
It is possible that the United States, responding to domestic pressures, might try to reshape economic relations in the Pacific Basin.

われわれの課題は、数千、数万にも上る日本の地名を見直すことでした。
Our task was to review the tens of hundreds of thousands of Japanese place names.
Wareware no kadai wa, suusen, suuman ni mo noboru Nihon no chimei o minaosu koto deshita.

立ち上がって彼女は寝室へ行くと、マントルピースの上の絵をもう一度見直した。
She got up and went into the bedroom and studied the picture over the mantelpiece.
Tachiagatte kanojo wa shinshitsu e iku to, mantorupiisu no ue no e o mou ichido minaoshita.

農業政策全体を見直すことはできないし、他国の補助金を削ったり、わが国の農産物輸出に対する諸外国の関税障壁を撤廃したりすることもできない。
We can’t restructure the whole farm program, reduce the subsidies in other nations’ budgets, or eliminate all the foreign barriers to our food exports.
Nougyou seisaku zentai o minaosu koto wa dekinai shi, takoku no hojokin o kezuttari, wagakuni no nousanbutsu yushutsu ni taisuru sho gaikoku no kanzei shouheki o teppai shitari suru koto mo dekinai.

この時は、新会計年度が始まってすでに何ヶ月か経っていたが、われわれは労働党から引き継いだ公共投資計画を見直すことから始めなくてはなからなかった。
Even though we were several months into the current financial year, we had to begin by reopening the public expenditure plans we had inherited from the Labour Government.
Kono toki wa, shin kaikei nendo ga hajimatte sude ni nankagetsu ka tatte ita ga, wareware wa Roudoutou kara hikitsuida koukyou toushi keikaku o minaosu koto kara hajimenakute wa nakaranakatta.

In French: réviser
In Spanish: reseñar
In German: überprüfen
In Italian: rivedere
