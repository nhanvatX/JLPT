Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 夫婦 (fuufu)

November 13, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 夫婦 (fuufu)]

Kana: ふうふ
Kanji: 夫婦
Romaji: fuufu

Type: Noun
Meaning: married couple, spouses, husband and wife, couple, pair
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

新婚の夫婦はタウンハウスに移ったが、家の大部分の部屋を人に貸して、一部の部屋だけを使っていた。
shinkon no fuufu wa taun hausu ni utsutta ga, ie no daibubun no heya o hito ni kashite, ichibu no heya dake o tsukatte ita.
The newlyweds moved into a town house but rented most of it out to other tenants and lived in a tiny apartment.

現在の仕組みでは、夫婦合わせて４００万ドルまでは相続税なしで財産を譲り渡すことができる。
genzai no shikumi de wa, fuufu awasete yon hyaku man doru made wa souzokuzei nashi de zaisan o yuzuriwatasu koto ga dekiru.
As currently structured, a husband and wife can pass on four million dollars without paying any estate tax.

[Ảnh: 夫婦 (fuufu)]

両者の違いを理解するために、結婚２０年も経ってから離婚を決意した夫婦が、心理療法士のカウンセリングを受けに来た例を挙げよう。
ryousha no chigai o rikai suru tame ni, kekkon nijuu nen mo tatte kara rikon o ketsui shita fuufu ga, shinri ryouhoushi no kaunseringu o uke ni kita rei o ageyou.
To understand this distinction, consider a couple consulting a psychotherapist after twenty years of marriage, and now intending to get divorced.

支払い不能に陥った個人や夫婦に弁護士がついて、債権者と話し合いをする。
shiharai funou ni ochiitta kojin ya fuufu ni bengoshi ga tsuite, saikensha to hanashiai o suru.
One by one, the individuals and couples who have fallen into insolvency sit down with a lawyer who negotiates on their behalf with their creditors.

わたしはこの夫婦が好きだ。
I like this couple.
watashi wa kono fuufu ga suki da.

父と母は、夫婦として二十五年間連れ添ってきた。
My parents have been together twenty-five years.
chichi to haha wa, fuufu toshite nijuugo nenkan tsuresotte kita.

彼ら夫婦は、五年間で三人のかわいい子供を持った。
They had three children in five years.
karera fuufu wa, gonenkan de sannin no kawaii kodomo o motta.

わたしも同じ苦難を忍びたいわ。夫婦ですもの、ともに苦しむべきよ。
I like to suffer too. We’re married, we should suffer together.
watashi mo onaji kunan o shinobitai wa. fuufu desu mono, tomo ni kurushimu beki yo.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 夫妇 (fūfù)
Korean: 부부 (bubu)
In French: couple
In Spanish: pareja
In German: Ehepaar
In Italian: coppia
