Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 親しい (shitashii)

November 20, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 親しい (shitashii)]

Kana: したしい
Kanji: 親しい
Romaji: shitashii

Type: いadjective
Meaning: intimate, close (e.g. friend)
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

ごく親しい友人です。
goku shitashii yuujin desu.
He is my most intimate friend.

こうして、私はアキラやその家族と親しくなった。親交は大学に入っても、また、アキラが亡くなっても続き、最終的に５０年にもなった。
kou shite, watsahi wa akira ya sono kazoku to shitashiku natta. shinkou wa daigaku ni haitte mo, mata, akira ga naku natte mo tsuzuki, saishuuteki ni gojuu nen ni mo natta.
That was the beginning of a close friendship with Akira and his family that continued through college and for the next fifty years, long after Akira had died.

[Ảnh: 親しい (shitashii)]

まちがいない。サトシは親しい友で、この指輪を長年身につけていた。これのことはわたしもよく知っている。
machigai nai. satoshi wa shitashii tomo de, kono yubiwa o naganen mi ni tsukete ita. kore no koto wa watashi mo yoku shitte iru.
I’m certain of it. Satoshi is my closest friend, and he wore this ring for many years. I am quite familiar with it.

その部屋は６０人を超える家族や親しい友人でいっぱいとなり、すべての人がそれぞれの悲しみを胸に抱いていた。
sono heya wa rokujuunin o koeru kazoku ya shitashii yuujin de ippai to nari, subete no hito ga sorezore no kanashimi o mune ni daite ita.
The room was crowded with more than sixty family members and close friends, and every person carried his or her own private grief.

親しくなれば彼女以上の人はいない。
Once you get to know her, there’s no one better in the world.
shitashiku nareba kanojo ijou no hito wa inai.

しばらく一緒に過ごして、親しくなりたいと思っていた。
I was hoping we might spend some time together and get acquainted.
shibaraku issho ni sugoshite, shitashiku naritai to omotte ita.

わたしたち、これからうんと親しいお友達になれそう。
We’re going to be very good friends.
watashitachi, kore kara unto shitashii otomodachi ni naresou.

もともとあの男は、とくべつ姉と親しかったわけじゃないようです。
I don’t think that man had been very close to my sister.
motomoto ano otoko wa, tokubetsu ane to shitashikatta wake janai you desu.

Chinese: 亲密 (qīnmì)
Korean: 친하다 (chinhada)
In French: intime
In Spanish: íntimo
In German: intim
In Italian: intomo
