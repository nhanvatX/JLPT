Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 構築 (kouchiku)

December 9, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 構築 (kouchiku)]

Kana: こうちく
Kanji: 構築
Romaji: kouchiku

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: construction, building, putting up, erecting, creation, formulation, architecture (systems, agreement, etc)
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

平和の構築こそすべきだ。
We must build peace.
Heiwa no kouchiku koso subeki da.

人道的な価値に基づいたシステムを構築したい。
I want to build a system based on humanitarian values.
Jindouteki na kachi ni motodzuita shisutemu o kouchiku shitai.

私は、人と深い感情的な繋がりを構築したいです。
I want to build deep emotional connections with people.
Watashi wa, hito to fukai kanjou-teki na tsunagari o kouchiku shitai desu.

連邦住宅局から補助金付きローンの受給資格を得るために、壁を構築する必要があったからだ。
They had to build it to qualify for subsidized loans from the Federal Housing Administration.
Renpou juutakukyoku kara hojokin-tsuki roon no jukyuu shikaku o eru tame ni, kabe o kouchiku suru hitsuyou ga atta kara da.

政府は社会と労働者のあいだに社会契約が構築されるよううながした。
seifu wa shakai to roudousha no aida ni shakai keiyaku ga kouchiku sareru you unagashita.
The government has helped structure the social compact between business and the worker.

この方法には、現代世界が接触して変化をもたらす前の、遠い昔に存在した文化を再構築できるというメリットがある。
kono houhou ni wa, gendai sekai ga sesshoku shite henka o motarasu mae no, tooi mukashi ni sonzai shita bunka o saikouchiku dekiru to iu meritto ga aru.
This method offers the advantage of reconstructing a culture long before it was contacted and changed by the modern world.

[Ảnh: 構築 (kouchiku)]

中国は何世紀も前から主要な海軍国ではない。海軍を構築するには長い年月を要する。
chuugoku wa nan seiki mo mae kara shuyou na kaigunkoku de wa nai. kaigun o kouchiku suru ni wa nagai nengetsu o you suru.
China has not been a major naval power for centuries, and building a navy requires a long time.

実のところ、われわれは戦後、ドイツに競争と労働組合の再構築を教え、彼らの経済的成功の条件を整えるのを助けてあげたのだ。
jitsu no tokoro, wareware wa sengo, doitsu ni kyousou to roudou kumiai no saikouchiku o oshie, karera no keizaiteki seikou no jouken o totonoeru no o tasukete ageta no da.
Indeed, we had helped create the conditions for Germany after the war by introducing competition and restructuring their trade unions.

Chinese: 构筑 (gòuzhù)
Korean: 구축 (guchug)

In German: Konstruktion
In Italian: costruzione
