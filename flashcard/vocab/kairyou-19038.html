Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 改良 (kairyou)

December 11, 2017   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 改良 (kairyou)]

Kana: かいりょう
Kanji: 改良
Romaji: kairyou

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: improvement, reform
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

私たちはインフラを飛躍的に改良した。
We dramatically improved our infrastructure.
Watashitachi wa infura o hiyaku-teki ni kairyō shita.

彼らは引き続きプロダクトを改良し続けた。
They continued to iterate on product.
Karera wa hikitsudzuki purodakuto o kairyō shi tsudzuketa.

これは単に最近改良された軍事兵器に過ぎません。
It is simply a recently improved military weapon.
Kore wa tan’ni saikin kairyō sa reta gunji heiki ni sugimasen.

タケルはそのまま走りながらアイデアとプロダクトを改良し、毎月社長に進捗を報告した。
Takeru kept moving forward refining his idea and product, bringing updates month to month to the President.
Takeru wa sonomama hashirinagara aidea to purodakuto o kairyō shi, maitsuki shachō ni shinchoku o hōkoku shita.

これまでのようなやり方、自分たちの秘密のソースに心を配って改良するのを怠りがちなやり方で、何とかやり過ごすだけでは不十分だ。
kore made no you na yarikata, jibuntachi no himitsu no soosu ni kokoro o kubatte kairyou suru no o okotarigachi na yarikata de, nantoka yarisugosu dake de wa fujuubun da.
Therefore, our ability to get by doing things the way we’ve been doing them—which is to say, not always tending to our secret sauce and enriching it—will not suffice anymore.

国家社会はこれらのトレンドのなかでも、とくに人口増加や政治の中央集権化、技術や武器の改良といった点で、国家社会に比べて単純な伝統的社会より進んでいたからこそ、それらを征服し、住民を服従させ、奴隷にし、国家に組み込んだり追放したりし、国家が欲する土地に住んでいた人々を根絶することができたのである。
kokka shakai wa korera no torendo no naka demo, toku ni jinkou zouka ya seiji no chuuou shuukenka, gijutsu ya buki no kairyou to itta ten de, kokka shakai ni kurabete tanjun na dentouteki shakai yori susunde ita kara koso, sorera o seifuku shi, juumin o fukujuu sase, dorei ni shi, kokka ni kumikondari tsuihou shitari shi, kokka ga hossuru tochi ni sunde ita hitobito o konzetsu suru koto ga dekita no de aru.
Those trends, especially the large populations and political centralization and improved technology and weapons of states with respect to simpler societies, are what have enabled states to conquer those traditional types of societies and to subjugate, enslave, incorporate, drive out, or exterminate their inhabitants on lands coveted by states.

[Ảnh: 改良 (kairyou)]

そうすれば、みんなで改良できるし、何百万人もがダウンロードして利用できる。
sou sureba, minna de kairyou dekiru shi, nanhyakuman nin mo ga daunroodo shite riyou dekiru.
Anyone who has something to contribute can improve it and millions of others can just download it for their own use.

改良を続けるのはいっこうにかまわないが、フィードバックがなく、われわれのパッチが統合される様子もないのが心配だった。
kairyou o tsuzukeru no wa ikkou ni kamawanai ga, fiido bakku ga naku, wareware no pacchi ga tougou sareru yousu mo nai no ga shinpai datta.
We were happy to continue improving this thing, yet we were worried when we were not getting any feedback and seeing our patches integrated.

Chinese: 改良 (gǎiliáng)
Korean: 개량 (gaelyang)
In French: amélioration
In Spanish: mejora
In German: Verbesserung
In Italian: miglioramento
