Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 顧客 (kokyaku)

December 27, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 顧客 (kokyaku)]

Kana: こきゃく
Kanji: 顧客
Romaji: kokyaku

Type: Noun, のadjective
Meaning: customer, client, patron
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

顧客のヨットはどこにある？
Where is the customer’s yacht?
Kokyaku no yotto wa doko ni aru?

最初の顧客は、ほとんどが私の知り合いだった。
Most of my first customers were people I knew.
Saisho no kokyaku wa, hotondo ga watashi no shiriai datta.

ミツルはなんとかして顧客を増やしたいと考えた。
Mitsuru decided to do something about the lack of clients.
Mitsuru wa nantoka shite kokyaku o fuyashitai to kangaeta.

顧客のあいだに無用の不安を引き起こすのは感心しない。
I don’t like causing unnecessary anxiety among customers.
Kokyaku no aida ni muyou no fuan o hikiokosu no wa kanshin shinai.

うちが三本の業務を一本化させたのは、つい最近のことだ。銀行、金融商品の仲介、貸付をほんとうに一体化させ、値段を知らせたりサービスを提供したりするのではなく、顧客の望む総合エクスペリエンスを提供できる仕組みにまとめるテクノロジーがようやく手に入ったからだ。
uchi ga sanbon no gyoumu o ipponka saseta no wa, tsui saikin no koto da. ginkou, kin’yuu shouhin no chuukai, kashitsuke o hontou ni ittaika sa se, nedan o shirasetari saabisu o teikyou shitari suru no de wa naku, kokyaku no nozomu sougou ekusuperiensu o teikyou dekiru shikumi ni matomeru tekunorojii ga youyaku te ni haitta kara da.
Only recently, though, did we have the technology to really integrate all our three businesses—banking, lending, and brokerage— and pull them together in a way that didn’t just deliver the price, not just the service, but the total experience the customers wanted.

ＥＬＦ保険の日本進出、これは食い止めないと他社含め、ヨツバの顧客も多くが流れる。
iieruefu hoken no nihon shinshutsu, kore wa kuitomenai to tasha fukume, yotsuba no kokyaku mo ooku ga nagareru.
We must prevent ELF Insurance’s entry into the Japanese market. That will hurt not only Yotsuba, but many other companies as well.

[Ảnh: 顧客 (kokyaku)]

とくに私は、イラン産石油の主要な顧客に対して、供給を多様化してテヘランからの買い付けを減らすよう説得することを自分の任務としていた。
toku ni watashi wa, iransan sekiyu no shuyou na kokyaku ni tai shite, kyoukyuu o tayouka shite teheran kara no kaitsuke o herasu you settoku suru koto o jibun no ninmu to shite ita.
Most of all, I would make it my mission to convince the top consumers of Iran’s oil to diversify their supplies and buy less from Tehran.

最優先の目標は、わが社の顧客を満足させ続けることだ。
saiyuusen no mokuhyou wa, wagasha no kokyaku o manzoku sasetsuzukeru koto da.
Our overriding goal is to keep our customers happy.

常に顧客の視点から物事を見るようにしなさい。
tsune ni kokyaku no shiten kara monogoto o miru you ni shinasai.
Always look at things from a customer’s perspective.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 顾客 (gùkè)
Korean: 고객 (gogaeg)
In French: client
In Spanish: cliente
In German: Kunde
In Italian: cliente
