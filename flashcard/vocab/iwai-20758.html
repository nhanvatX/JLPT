Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 祝い (iwai)

December 25, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 祝い (iwai)]

Kana: いわい
Kanji: 祝い
Romaji: iwai

Type: Noun
Meaning: congratulation, congratulations, celebration, festival, congratulatory gift
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

まだ、選挙の勝利のお祝い気分だった。
mada, senkyo no shouri no oiwai kibun datta.
We were still in a mood to celebrate our election victory.

喪中というよりは、お祝いでしたね。
It was more like a celebration than a mourning.
Mochū to iu yori wa, oiwaideshita ne.

これじゃ、お祝いにならないじゃないですか。
This is no way to celebrate.
Kore ja, oiwai ni naranai janaidesu ka.

お祝いにお昼でもいっしょに食べない？
Why don’t we have lunch together and celebrate?
Oiwai ni ohiru demo issho ni tabenai?

いつ戻るんだ？きみのために、お祝いパーティーを計画しているんだ。
When are you coming back? I’m planning a celebration party for you.
Itsu modoru nda? Kimi no tame ni, oiwai pātī o keikaku shite iru nda.

就任のお祝いを言いかけたが、最後までいかないうちに彼女が本題に入った。
shuunin no oiwai o ii kaketa ga, saigo made ikanai uchi ni kanojo ga hondai ni haitta.
I managed only a couple of congratulatory words before she got to the reason for her call.

[Ảnh: 祝い (iwai)]

みんないくらかずつ今日の祝の気分を味わい、遠く離れたところで自分のことを思ってくれている人々のことを思い、その人々も自分のことを思い出して喜んでいてくれることを知っていた。
minna ikuraka zutsu kyou no iwai no kibun o ajiwai, tooku hanareta tokoro de jibun no koto o omotte kurete iru hitobito no koto o omoi, sono hitobito mo jibun no koto o omoidashite yorokonde ite kureru koto o shitte ita.
Everyone had shared to some extent in its festivities; and had remembered those he cared for at a distance, and had known that they delighted to remember him.

わたしと友人たちは、大学の卒業祝いパーティーを開いた。
watashi to yuujintachi wa, daigaku no sotsugyou iwai paatii o hiraita.
My friends and I had a party to celebrate our college graduation.

Chinese: 庆祝 (qìngzhù)
Korean: 축하 (chugha)
In French: célébration
In Spanish: celebracion
In German: Festlichkeit
In Italian: celebrazione
