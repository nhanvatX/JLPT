Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 金融 (kin’yuu)

November 7, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 金融 (kin’yuu)]

Kana: きんゆう
Kanji: 金融
Romaji: kinyuu

Type: Noun, のadjective
Meaning: financing, credit transacting, loaning of money, circulation of money
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

金融システムは破綻した。
The financial system has collapsed.
Kin’yū shisutemu wa hatan shita.

この国は国際金融業者の手に渡ってしまった。
This country has fallen into the hands of international financiers.
Kono kuni wa kokusai kin’yū gyōsha no te ni watatte shimatta.

アメリカの経済と金融が世界中で急速に膨張した。
America’s economy and finance expanded rapidly around the world.
Amerika no keizai to kin’yū ga sekaijū de kyūsoku ni bōchō shita.

１８９３年に金融恐慌が起きた。
A financial panic occurred in 1893.
1893-Nen ni kin’yū kyōkō ga okita.

国務省の仕事が終わったあと、私のところには金融機関を中心に大手企業から好待遇の話がたくさん舞い込んだ。
kokumushou no shigoto ga owatta ato, watashi no tokoro ni wa kin’yuu kikan o chuushin ni oote kigyou kara koutaiguu no hanashi ga takusan maikonda.
When I left the State Department, I was flattered by offers of top positions in major corporations, most of them in the financial world.

また、巨額の金を借りて行う金融取引の対象となった場合には、その買い手には高く売れるような時機を見計らって公募したり、他者に売却するという目論見があります。
kata, kyogaku no kane o karite okonau kin’yuu torihiki no taishou to natta baai ni wa, sono kaite ni wa takaku ureru you na jiki o mihakaratte koubo shi tari, tasha ni baikyaku suru to iu mokuromi ga arimasu.
Then there is the financial maneuverer, usually operating on large amounts of borrowed money, who plans to resell either to the public or to another corporation as soon as the time is favorable.

[Ảnh: 金融 (kin’yuu)]

世界の金融サービス市場で競争するために、競合他社が用いているコスト節減手段はすべて利用すべきだと思った。
sekai no kin’yuu saabisu ichiba de kyousou suru tame ni, kyougou tasha ga mochiite iru kosuto setsugen shudan wa subete riyou subeki da to omotta.
Competing in the global financial services market, we felt we had to take advantage of all the cost-saving opportunities that its competitors were using.

直ちに行動が必要だったのは国の金融、経済問題だった。
tadachi ni koudou ga hitsuyou datta no wa kuni no kin’yuu, keizai mondai datta.
It was the nation’s financial and economic affairs, however, which required immediate attention.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 金融 (jīnróng)
Korean: 금융 (geum-yung)
In French: finance
In Spanish: finanzas
In German: Finanzen
In Italian: finanza
