Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 扱う (atsukau)

March 20, 2016   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Hình ảnh đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]

Kana: あつかう
Kanji: 扱う
Romaji: atsukau

Type: verb

Meaning: to treat; to deal with; to handle; to take care of

Example sentences:


扱いにくい馬よ。
The horse is hard to ride.
atsukai nikui uma yo.

彼は扱いにくい男だった。
He was a difficult man.
kare wa atsukai nikui otoko datta.

あなたを冷たく扱って悪かった。
I’m sorry I gave you such a hard time.
anata o tsumetaku atsukatte warukatta.

この事態をどう扱えばいいのかしら？
How do I handle this?
kono jitai o dou atsukaeba ii no kashira.

どういう事件を扱っているんですか？
What kind of cases do you handle?
dou iu jiken o atsukatte irun desu ka.

なぜわたしを子供みたいに扱おうとしてるの？
Why are you treating me as though I was a child?
naze watashi o kodomo mitai ni atsukaou to shiteru no.

人間たちは私を同胞として扱ってくれたろうか。
Have mankind treated me as a brother?
ningentachi wa watashi o douhou toshite atsukatte kuretarou ka.

その首領がわしをこんなふうに扱えと君に言ったのかね。
Did your superior order you to treat me this way?
sono shuryou ga washi o konna fuu ni atsukae to kimi ni itta no ka ne.

この小説では、類人猿は話すことができ、人間をペットのように扱っている。
In this novel, apes can talk, and they treat humans like their pets.
kono shousetsu dewa, ruijin’en wa hanasu koto ga deki, ningen o petto no you ni atsukatte iru.

どう考えても、彼は重大な問題を扱える男だとは思えませんわ。
I don’t feel that he would be quite the person to deal with anything serious.
dou kangaete mo, kare wa juudai na mondai o atsukaeru otoko da to wa omoemasen wa.

Why do folks in this town treat me so scurvily and talk about me so?
doushite kono machi no hitotachi wa watashi o kou hidoku atsukattari, sakan ni uwasa shitari suru no kashira ne.
