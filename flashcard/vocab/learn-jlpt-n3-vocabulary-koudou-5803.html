Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 行動 (koudou)

November 15, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 行動 (koudou)]

Kana: こうどう
Kanji: 行動
Romaji: koudou

Type: Noun; Verb (行動する)

Meaning: action; conduct; behavior

Example sentences:


あなた、本能的に行動したのよ。
You reacted instinctively.
anata, honnouteki ni koudou shita no yo.

ゆうべのあなたは勇気ある行動に出たのだ。
It was a brave thing you did last night.

He’s not responsible for his action.

No one can prove what you were doing.

ゆっくりコーヒーでも飲んで、今後のわれわれのとるべき行動を考えよう。
Let’s have some coffee and think about what we should do.
yukkuri koohii demo nonde, kongo no wareware no toru beki koudou o kangaeyou.

I stopped by the garage to tell Sawada what I was going to do.
sawada ni kore kara no koudou o tsutaeru tame ni gareeji e tachiyotta.

Was Hiroki acting with the group all the time?

I want you to check on Kojima’s movements the afternoon and noon of that day.
jiken no atta hi no gogo kara yoru ni kakete no, kojima no koudou ni tsuite shirabete mite.

もしあることについて絶対的な確信がなければ、明確な行動をとることは難しいのです。
If you are not absolutely sure of a thing, it is so difficult to commit yourself to a definite course of action.
moshi aru koto ni tsuite zettaiteki na kakushin ga nakereba, meikaku na koudou o toru koto wa muzukashii no desu.

It was as if he was watching our every move and action. Maybe spying on us from nearby!
marude yatsu wa watashitachi no koudou ya hannou o majika de minagara sousa shite ita mitai janai ka.
