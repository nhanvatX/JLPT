Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 覚える (oboeru)

November 1, 2015   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 覚える (oboeru)]
Kana: おぼえる
Kanji: 覚える
Romaji: oboeru

Type: Verb

Meaning: to remember; to memorize; to learn by heart; to learn; to bear in mind

Example sentences:


何年も何年も前のことだ。よく覚えている。
It happened many many years ago. I still remember very well.
nannen mo nannen mo mae no koto da. yoku oboete iru.
