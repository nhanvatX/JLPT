Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 直前 (chokuzen)

January 23, 2018   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 直前 (chokuzen)]

Kana: ちょくぜん
Kanji: 直前
Romaji: chokuzen

Type: Noun
Meaning: just before
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

出産直前まで働かされることは珍しくなかった。
It was not uncommon for women to be forced to work until just before childbirth.
Shussan chokuzen made hatarakasa reru koto wa mezurashikunakatta.

投票日直前の世論調査では、両者はほぼ互角だった。
Opinion polls just before Election Day showed the two sides almost evenly matched.
Tōhyō-bi chokuzen no seronchōsade wa, ryōsha wa hobo gokakudatta.

平時になるとこの慣習が再開され、第一次世界大戦の直前、ドイツの多くの商船はロイズの保険に入っていた。
In peacetime the practice resumed, so that on the eve of the First World War most of Germany’s merchant marine was insured by Lloyd’s.
Heiji ni naruto kono kanshū ga saikai sa re, daiichijisekaitaisen no chokuzen, Doitsu no ōku no shōsen wa Roizu no hoken ni haitte ita.

マッカーサーには第二次世界大戦の直前、日本軍の意図と能力とを完全に読み違えた前科があった。
makkaasaa ni wa dainiji sekai taisen no chokuzen, nihon gun no ito to nouryoku to o kanzen ni yomichigaeta zenka ga atta.
MacArthur had been wrong before, completely wrong, on Japanese intentions and abilities right before World War II.

眠る直前に、ワインを一杯飲んで、すごく静かな優しい音楽を聞くよ。それから、心の中で悩み事を全部振り返る。そうすれば、心配事なしに眠れるんだ。
nemuru chokuzen ni, wain o ippai nonde, sugoku shizuka na yasashii ongaku o kiku yo. sore kara, kokoro no naka de nayami koto o zenbu furikaeru. sou sureba, shinpai koto nashi ni nemurerun da.
Just before sleeping, I have a glass of wine and listen to very quiet, gentle music. And then, in my mind, I go through everything that’s bothering me. After that, I can sleep without any worries.

わたしがアメリカに発つ直前に彼がやってきたのだった。
watashi ga amerika ni hatsu chokuzen ni kare ga yatte kita no datta.
Shortly before I left for America he came in to see me.

わたしは発見したのですが、彼が手紙を取りもどすのに、わずか五分間しかなかったんですよ――わたしたちが現場に着く直前の五分間です。
watashi wa hakken shita no desu ga, kare ga tegami o torimodosu no ni, wazuka gofunkan shika nakattan desu yo. watashitachi ga genba ni tsuku chokuzen no go funkan desu.
I have discovered that there were only five short minutes in which he could have taken it–the five minutes immediately before our own arrival on the scene.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 即将…之前 (jíjiāng…zhīqián)
Korean: 직전 (jigjeon)
In French: juste avant
In Spanish: justo antes
In German: kurz bevor
In Italian: appena prima
