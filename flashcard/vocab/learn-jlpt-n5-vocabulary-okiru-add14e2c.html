Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 起きる (okiru)

November 16, 2015   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 起きる (okiru)]

Kana: おきる
Kanji: 起きる
Romaji: okiru

Type: Verb

Meaning: to get up; to rise; to wake up; to be awake; to stay awake; to occur (usu. of unfavourable incidents); to happen; to take place

Example sentences:


起きなさい。もう１１時だよ。
Wake up. It’s already 11 o’clock.
okinasai. mou juuichi ji da yo.

I didn’t hear you get up.

Something interesting might happen.

You can’t wake her up once she’s gone to sleep.

It looks like no one in the household has woken up yet.

さっそく話してもらいたい。何が起きたのか、すべてを。
Now you must tell me exactly what happened.
sassoku hanashite moraitai. nani ga okita no ka, subete o.

It will be a comfort to me to know that you are with her if anything happens to me.
kono saki, jibun no mi ni nani ga okiyou to, kimi ga kanojo no soba ni iru to omoeba nagusamerareru.

Never before have I been able to get up whenever I want or eat whatever I like.
suki na toki ni okite, tabetai mono o taberu nante, konna koto wa ima made ni nai.
