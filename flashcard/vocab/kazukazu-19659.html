Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 数々 (kazukazu)

December 16, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 数々 (kazukazu)]

Kana: かずかず
Kanji: 数々
Romaji: kazukazu

Type: Noun, のadjective
Meaning: many, numerous, various, large number of
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

今夜は数々の思い出が鮮明によみがえってきた。
The memories had been very clear tonight.
Kon’ya wa kazukazu no omoide ga senmei ni yomigaette kita.

BIGBANGは数々のヒット曲を生み出した。
BIGBANG has produced numerous hit songs.
Bigguban wa kazukazu no hitto kyoku o umidashita.

わたしがこれまでに行なった数々の破壊的な行為、それについては謝罪します。
I apologize for the destructive things I have done.
Watashi ga kore made ni okonatta kazukazu no hakai-teki na koui, sore ni tsuite wa shazai shimasu.

１８世紀の日本をとらえた希少な写真の数々を見ていきましょう。
Let’s take a look at a number of rare photographs that capture Japan in the 18th century.
juuhachi seiki no Nihon o toraeta kishou na shashin no kazukazu o mite ikimashou.

そんなわけで人々は、これほど勇敢な人であれば、過去の数々の過ちは大目に見て忘れてしまおうと考えたわけだ。
sonna wake de hitobito wa, kore hodo yuukan na hito de areba, kako no kazukazu no ayamachi wa oome ni mite wasurete shimaou to kangaeta wake da.
Yes, people felt they could forgive and forget a great many things for such a brave man.

私は、一人の首相が職を去り、その後継者が任に就く時に起こった数々のばつの悪いエピソードに思いをとらわれた。
watashi wa, hitori no shushou ga shoku o sari, sono koukeisha ga nin ni tsuku toki ni okotta kazukazu no batsu no warui episoodo ni omoi o torawareta.
I was haunted by tales of embarrassing episodes as one prime minister left and his successor entered office.

[Ảnh: 数々 (kazukazu)]

生涯にわたる裏切り、投獄、政治亡命、破約の数々が彼を変え、非情にした。
shougai ni wataru uragiri, tougoku, seiji boumei, hayaku no kazukazu ga kare o kae, hijou ni shita.
A lifetime of betrayal, prison sentences, political exile, and broken promises had changed and hardened him.

就任式の数ヵ月後、一冊の本が出版された。就任記念週間の熱気と意味合いをとらえた美しい写真の数々を収めた本だ。
shuuninshiki no suukagetsu go, issatsu no hon ga shuppan sareta. shuunin kinen shuukan no nekki to imiai o toraeta utsukushii shashin no kazukazu o osameta hon da.
A few months after the inauguration, a book was published filled with beautiful photographs that capture the excitement and meaning of the inaugural week.
