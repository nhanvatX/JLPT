Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 合わせる (awaseru)

November 22, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 合わせる (awaseru)]

Kana: あわせる
Kanji: 合わせる
Romaji: awaseru

Type: Verb
Meaning: to match (rhythm, speed, etc.), to join together, to unite, to combine, to add up, to face, to be opposite (someone), to compare, to check with, to cause to meet (e.g. an unpleasant fate), to place together, to connect, to overlap, to mix, to combine, to put blade to blade, to fight
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

とても合わせる顔がないわ。逃げ出してしまう。
totemo awaseru kao ga nai wa. nigedashite shimaou.
I can’t face him. I will run away.

よくもわたしに合わせる顔があるわね！
yokumo watashi ni awaseru kao ga aru wa ne.
I don’t see how you have the gall to face me!

二人の男が顔を合わせるのは今度が初めてではない。
The two men have faced each other before.
Futari no otoko ga kao o awaseru no wa kondo ga hajimetede wanai.

彼女は夫の新しい政治活動に合わせて転向した。
She shifted to accommodate her husband’s new political activities.
Kanojo wa otto no atarashī seiji katsudō ni awa sete tenkō shita.

フェイスブックは直接顔を合わせて話すコミュニケーションの代わりではない。
Facebook is not a substitute for face-to-face communication.
Feisu bukku wa chokusetsu kao o awasete hanasu komyunikēshon no kawaride wanai.

力を合わせれば、祖先が何千年もかかってやってきたことを数ヶ月でやることができる。
If we work together, we’ll be able to accomplish in a matter of months what our forefathers had done in thousands of years.
Chikara o awasereba, sosen ga nanzen’nen mo kakatte yatte kita koto o sū-kagetsu de yaru koto ga dekiru.

現在、先進国の銀行資産を合わせると、それらの国々のＧＤＰの１５０パーセントほどにもなる。
genzai, senshinkoku no ginkou shisan o awaseru to, sorera no kuniguni no jiidiipii no hyaku gojuu paasento hodo ni mo naru.
Today, banking assets in the world’s major economies are equivalent to around 150 per cent of those countries’ combined GDP.

それにアジアの豊かな文化と伝統という遺産が合わせると、いわゆるコンテンツ貿易の中核になる滞在的な力にはものすごいものがあります。
sore ni ajia no yutaka na bunka to dentou to iu isan ga awaseru to, iwayuru kontentsu boueki no chuukaku ni naru taizaiteki na chikara ni wa monosugoi mono ga arimasu.
This, together with the rich culture and heritage of Asia, presents the region with tremendous potential to be a content-trading hub.
