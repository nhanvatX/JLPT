Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 妨害 (bougai)

December 29, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 妨害 (bougai)]

Kana: ぼうがい
Kanji: 妨害
Romaji: bougai

Type: Noun, のadjective, するverb
Meaning: disturbance, obstruction, hindrance, jamming, interference
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

僕が戦争を妨害したのは事実です。
It is true that I interfered with the war.
Boku ga sensou o bougai shita no wa jijitsu desu.

このストライキが国の戦争努力を妨害している。
The strike is sabotaging the country’s war effort.
Kono sutoraiki ga kuni no sensou doryoku o bougai shite iru.

それが交渉の妨害に効力を発揮したのだ。
This proved effective in hindering negotiations.
Sore ga koushou no bougai ni kouryoku o hakki shita no da.

連中は、できるかぎり外部との連絡を妨害するはずだ。
I’m sure they are trying to block our contact with the outside world as much as possible.
Renchuu wa, dekiru kagiri gaibu to no renraku o bougai suru hazu da.

セルビアやアルカイダを妨害して不安定な状態に陥れることが主眼であれば、きわめて合理的な行動と言えよう。
serubia ya arukaida o bougai shite fuantei na joutai ni otoshireru koto ga shugan de areba, kiwamete gouriteki na koudou to ieyou.
Since the primary goal will more likely be simply to block or destabilize Serbia or al Qaeda, the interventions will be quite rational.

物品の流れが妨害されれば打撃を被り、妨害が長期におよべば壊滅的な被害を受けかねない。
buppin no nagare ga bougai sarereba dageki o kaburi, bougai ga chouki ni oyobeba kaimetsuteki na higai o ukekanenai.
Any interruption in the flow of commodities would be damaging. An extended interruption would be catastrophic.

[Ảnh: 妨害 (bougai)]

限られた量ながら輸入も続いており、組合の妨害をくぐり抜けていた。
kagirareta ryou nagara yunyuu mo tsudzuite ori, kumiai no bougai o kugurinukete ita.
Limited imports were continuing and getting past the obstacles the unions put up against them.

国際的な結束と決意を示せないと、中国の守ろうとする利益、とくに中東の安定維持、石油価格の安定、世界経済の回復が妨害されるだろう、と私は述べた。
kokusaiteki na kessoku to ketsui o shimesenai to, chuugoku no mamorou to suru rieki, toku ni chuutou no antei iji, sekiyu kakaku no antei, sekai keizai no kaifuku ga bougai sareru darou, to watashi wa nobeta.
I said that lack of international unity and resolve would undermine the interests China was seeking to protect, including maintaining stability in the Middle East, keeping oil prices stable, and protecting the recovery of the global economy.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 妨害 (fánghài)
Korean: 방해 (banghae)
In French: obstacle
In Spanish: obstáculo
In German: Hindernis
In Italian: ostacolo
