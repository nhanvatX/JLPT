Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 名 (na)

January 23, 2018   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 名 (na)]

Kana: な
Kanji: 名
Romaji: na

Type: Noun
Meaning: name, given name, title, fame, renown, reputation, pretext, pretense, justification, appearance
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

その名が示すとおり、議会図書館は議会のために設立された。
sono na ga shimesu toori, gikai toshokan wa gikai no tame ni setsuritsu sareta.
As its name implies, the Library of Congress was established to serve Congress.

その取締役は自分の名と社名を伏せるという条件で話をしてくれた。
sono torishimariyaku wa jibun no na to shamei o fuseru to iu jouken de hanashi o shite kureta.
Said the executive, on the condition that he and his company not be quoted by name.

彼の名を口にしただけで一同の上に暗い影がさし、たっぷり五分間というものその影は消えなかった。
kare no na o kuchi ni shita dake de ichidou no ue ni kurai kage ga sashi, tappuri go funkan to iu mono sono kage wa kienakatta.
The mention of his name cast a dark shadow on the party, which was not dispelled for full five minutes.

断れば、あんたは歴史上最悪の大統領として名を残す。
kotowareba, anta wa rekishijou saiaku no daitouryou toshite na o nokosu.
If you reject this offer, you’re going to go down in history as the worst President the world has ever seen.

あの男の名はなんといったっけ？
What was the guy’s name?
Ano otoko no na wa nan to itta kke?

これがあなたの名よ、テルオ。
This is your name, Teruo.
Kore ga anata no na yo, Teruo.

彼女は震える子供じみた声でおっとの名を呼んだ。
She called her husband’s name in a trembling child-like voice.
Kanojo wa furueru kodomo jimita koe de otto no na o yonda.

白いドアには、黒い文字で名が記されている。
On the white door is a name written in black.
Shiroi doa ni wa, kuroi moji de na ga shirusarete iru.
