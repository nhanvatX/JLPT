Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 念 (nen)

December 6, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 念 (nen)]

Kana: ねん
Kanji: 念
Romaji: nen

Type: Noun
Meaning: sense, idea, thought, feeling, desire, concern, attention, care
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

わたしの胸に感謝の念がこみあげた。
Gratitude welled up in my heart.
Watashi no mune ni kansha no nen ga komiageta.

その青年の率直さにぼくは称賛の念を禁じ得なかった。
I couldn’t help but admire the young man’s frankness.
Sono seinen no socchoku-sa ni boku wa shousan no nen o kinji enakatta.

唐突に、濃密な疑心暗鬼の念が全身を包み込んだ。
A sudden intense paranoia swept over me.
Toutotsu ni, noumitsu na gishin angi no nen ga zenshin o tsutsumikonda.

田中先生があらわれて、同情の念をあらわした。
Dr. Tanaka came along and expressed his sympathy.
Tanaka sensei ga arawarete, doujou no nen o arawashita.

不信の念が強く、お互いに相手が西欧列強と協定を結ぶのを妨げようとしているにすぎないのではないかと疑っていた。
fushin no nen ga tsuyoku, otagai ni aite ga seiou rekkyou to kyoutei o musubu no o samatageyou to shite iru ni suginai no de wa nai ka to utagatte ita.
Mutual distrust was profound, and each side suspected that the other might be merely trying to hinder it reaching an agreement with the Western Powers.

それに続く数週間、彼らが、悲しみと歴史の苛酷なスポットライトに耐えながら品位と威厳を保っていたことに、私は畏敬の念を抱いた。
sore ni tsuzuku suu shuukan, karera ga, kanashimi to rekishi no kakoku na supottoraito ni taenagara hin’i to igen o tamotte ita koto ni, watashi wa ikei no nen o idaita.
In the weeks that followed I was awed by the grace and dignity with which the they coped with their grief and the harsh spotlight of history.

[Ảnh: 念 (nen)]

念のために申し添えておくが、私は自分がどう考えてなにを選んだのかをお話ししているだけで、私の前任や後任を批判しているわけではない。
nen no tame ni moushisoete oku ga, watashi wa jibun ga dou kangaete nani o eranda no ka o ohanashi shite iru dake de, watashi no zennin ya kounin o hihan shite iru wake de wa nai.
I want to make it clear that my choices and the reasons I made them are not judgments on either my successors or predecessors.
***念のため (nen no tame): just to be sure

予約日の一週間前に、念を押すために歯科医の予定表が電子メールで自動的に送られてくる。
yoyaku hi no isshuukan mae ni, nen o osu tame ni shikai no yotei hyou ga denshi meeru de jidouteki ni okurarete kuru.
The week before your appointment, your dentist’s calendar will automatically send you an e-mail reminding you of the appointment.
***念を押す (nen o osu): to make sure of

Chinese: 念头 (niàntou)
Korean: 염원 (yeom-won)
In French: sentiment
In Spanish: sentimiento
In German: Gefühl
In Italian: sensazione
