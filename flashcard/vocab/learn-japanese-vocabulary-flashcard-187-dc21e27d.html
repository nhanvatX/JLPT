Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 貸す (kasu)

September 23, 2014   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 貸す (kasu)]
Kana: かす
Kanji: 貸す
Romaji: kasu

Type: verb

Meaning: to lend; to loan; to rent out; to hire out

Example sentences:


お金を貸してくれる？
Can you lend me some money?
okane o kashite kureru?

Please help me out if something else comes up.
mata nanika attara chikara o kashite kudasai yo.

金ならどっさりある。貸してやるぜ。
I got plenty of money. I’ll lend you some.
kane nara dossari aru. kashite yaru ze.

もしあなたがその問題に関心をお持ちなら、いい本をお貸ししましょう。
I can lend you rather a good book if you are interested in that subject.
moshi anata ga sono mondai ni kanshin o omochi nara, ii hon o okashi shimashou.

By the way, you couldn’t loan me a million or two till payday, could you?
tokoro de, kyuuryoubi made, hyakuman ka nihyaku man, kashite kurenai kashira.

Help me out, and maybe I’ll tell you something you want to know.
boku ni te o kase. sou shitara kimi ga shiritagatte iru koto o oshiete yatte mo ii.

わたしはＣＩＡのラットという者だ。ＣＩＡの権限において、あなたの携帯を至急一分貸してもらえるか？
I’m CIA agent Ratt. I need to requisition your cellphone for a minute to make an official call.
watashi wa shiiaiei no ratto to iu mono da. shiiaiei no kengen ni oite, anata no keitai o shikyuu ippun kashite moraeru ka.

彼のような若者に慣習と恐怖というなの川を渡って正しいと信じる行動をとろうとする努力ができるなら、わたしはかならず川の反対側で彼を迎え、岸へ上がるのに手を貸したいと思う。
If a young man like him can make the effort to cross the currents of habit and fear in order to do what he knows is right, then I want to be sure that I’m there to meet him on the other side and help him onto shore.
kare no you na wakamono ni kanshuu to kyoufu to iu na no kawa o watatte tadashii to shinjiru koudou o torou to suru doryoku ga dekiru nara, watashi wa kanarazu kawa no hantaisoku de kare o mukae, kishi e agaru noni te o kashitai to omou.
