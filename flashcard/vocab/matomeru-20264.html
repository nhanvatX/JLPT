Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 纏める (matomeru)

December 21, 2017   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 纏める (matomeru)]

Kana: まとめる
Kanji: 纏める
Romaji: matomeru

Type: Verb
Meaning: to collect; to put (it all) together; to integrate; to consolidate; to unify; to summarize; to aggregate; to bring to a conclusion; to finalize; to settle; to put in order; to establish; to decide
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

会議終了後、決断に必要な書類を我々がまとめた。
kaigi shuuryougo, ketsudan ni hitsuyou na shorui o wareware ga matometa.
After it was over we prepared a decision paper.

二週間後、彼は閣議提出の文書に提案をまとめた。
nishuukango, kare wa kakugi teishutsu no bunsho ni teian o matometa.
Two weeks later he set out his proposals in a Cabinet paper.

彼らは荷物をまとめ、帰ることにした。
They packed their things and started back.
Karera wa nimotsu o matome, kaeru koto ni shita.

死体をこんなにたくさんまとめてみたのは初めてだ。
I’ve never seen so many bodies in one place.
Shitai o konna ni takusan matomete mita no wa hajimete da.

頭痛のせいか、考えをまとめることがやけに難しい。
It’s hard to think because of the headache.
Zutsuu no sei ka, kangae o matomeru koto ga yake ni muzukashii.

というところで、今夜の状況をまとめると、どうなる？
So to sum it up, where are we tonight?
To iu tokoro de, kon’ya no joukyou o matomeru to, dou naru?

わたし、荷物をまとめるためにもどってきただけなんだ。
I just came back to gather my stuff.
Watashi, nimotsu o matomeru tame ni modotte kita dake nan da.

[Ảnh: 纏める (matomeru)]

もしイランが受け入れれば、我々はイランの核計画に関するすべての懸念に対処する、より包括的な取引をまとめる時間を稼ぐことができる。
moshi iran ga ukeirereba, wareware wa iran no kaku keikaku ni kansuru subete no ke’nen ni taisho suru, yori houkatsuteki na torihiki o matomeru jikan o kasegu koto ga dekiru.
If the Iranians accepted, we’d have time to work toward a more comprehensive deal that would answer all our concerns about the nuclear program.

２０１０年の春を通じて、我々は票をまとめるために積極的に活動した。
nisenjuu nen no haru o tsuujite, wareware wa hyou o matomeru tame ni sekkyokuteki ni katsudou shita.
Throughout the spring of 2010 we worked aggressively to round up the votes.
