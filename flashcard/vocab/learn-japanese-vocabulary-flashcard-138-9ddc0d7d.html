Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 会う (au)

September 22, 2014   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 会う (au)]
Kana: あう
Kanji: 会う
Romaji: au

Type: verb

Meaning: to meet; to encounter; to see

Idiom:

事故にあう (jiko ni au): to have an accident

Example sentences:


じゃ、また会おう！
Let’s meet again!
ja, mata aou.
