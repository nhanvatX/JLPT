Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 素敵 (suteki)

December 18, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 素敵 (suteki)]

Kana: すてき
Kanji: 素敵
Romaji: suteki

Type: なadjective
Meaning: lovely, dreamy, beautiful, great, fantastic, superb, cool
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

これは、大統領の外遊に伴う重要政策発表の狂騒状態の中で、素敵で静かなひとときだった。
kore wa, daitouryou no gaiyuu ni tomonau juuyou seisaku happyou no kyousou joutai no naka de, suteki de shizuka na hitotoki datta.
It was a lovely, quiet moment together in the middle of all the madness of a Presidential trip and a major policy roll-out.

彼の父親は素敵な老紳士であり、鉄壁の意志をもち、曲がったことの大嫌いな厳格の士でもあるらしく、息子が二十歳のとき無一文で家からたたき出し、一家の聖書の後ろに記された名簿からもその名を削った。
kare no chichioya wa suteki na roushinshi de ari, teppeki no ishi o mochi, magatta koto no daikirai na genkaku no shide mo arurashiku, musuko ga hatachi no toki muichimon de ie kara tatakidashi, ikka no seisho no ushiro ni shirusareta meibo kara mo sono na o kezutta.
His father, a charming old gentleman with an iron will and a ramrod for a backbone, had cast him out without a penny when he was twenty and even stricken his name from the family Bible.

[Ảnh: 素敵 (suteki)]

そこに行ったのは初めてだったが、なかなかすてきな店だと思った。
soko ni itta no wa hajimete datta ga, nakanaka suteki na mise da to omotta.
I had never been to the store, but it seemed nice enough.

ライトがキラ。本当にそうだったなら素敵ね。
raito ga kira. hontou ni sou datta nara suteki ne.
Light is Kira? That would be awesome if it was true.

その素敵な顧客というのは結婚してるのかい？
Is this wonderful client of yours married?
Sono suteki na kokyaku to iu no wa kekkon shiteru no kai?

誰か素敵な人を見つけて、早く幸せになってちょうだい。
I want you to find someone wonderful and be happy.
Dareka suteki na hito o mitsukete, hayaku shiawase ni natte choudai.

あなたはわたしが出会った男性の中で一番素敵な人ですよ。
You’re the most wonderful man I’ve ever known.
Anata wa watashi ga deatta dansei no naka de ichiban suteki na hito desu yo.

あなたのことを素敵なお医者さんだって皆が言っているわよ。
Everybody says you’re a wonderful doctor.
Anata no koto o suteki na oishasan datte mina ga itte iru wa yo.
