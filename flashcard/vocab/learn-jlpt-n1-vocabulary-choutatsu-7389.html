Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 調達 (choutatsu)

January 31, 2016   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary with Flashcard]

Kana: ちょうたつ
Kanji: 調達
Romaji: choutatsu

Type: noun; verb (調達する)

Meaning: supply; provision; raising

Example sentences:


お父さん、長靴を一足、調達願えますか？
otousan, chouka o issoku, choutatsu negaemasu ka.
Father, could you manage to get me a pair of boots?

木村に頼んでお金を調達しよう。
kimura ni tanonde okane o choutatsu shiyou.
I’ll get the money from Kimura.

[Ảnh: 調達 (choutatsu)]

わたしたち、どこかからお金を調達しなくちゃいけないと思わない？
watashitachi, dokoka kara okane o choutatsu shinakucha ikenai to omowanai.
Doesn’t it occur to you that we’ll have to get the money somewhere?

彼が、僕たちに食べ物を調達してくれるんだ。
kare ga, bokutachi ni tabemono o choutatsu shite kurerun da.
He’s been providing us with food.

新たに現金を調達しなければなりませんね。
You have to raise more money.
Arata ni genkin o choutatsu shinakereba narimasen ne.

核兵器の調達は１９６０年代まで続いた。
Procurement of nuclear weapons continued until the 1960s.
Kakuheiki no choutatsu wa sen kyuuhyaku rokujuu-nendai made tsudzuita.

演説や資金調達や同僚議員の選挙運動のために、ほかの州へ出かける。
There are the trips to other states to deliver a speech, raise money, or campaign for your colleagues.
Enzetsu ya shikin choutatsu ya douryou giin no senkyo undou no tame ni, hoka no shuu e dekakeru.

選挙運動の開始から三ヶ月間、わたしは資金調達アシスタントと部屋にこもって、これまで民主党に献金してくれたことがある人々に電話をかけた。楽しい作業ではない。
In the first three months of my campaign, I would shut myself in a room with my fund-raising assistant and call previous Democratic donors. It was not fun.
Senkyo undou no kaishi kara sankagetsu-kan, watashi wa shikin choutatsu ashisutanto to heya ni komotte, kore made minshutou ni kenkin shite kureta koto ga aru hitobito ni denwa o kaketa. Tanoshii sagyou de wa nai.
