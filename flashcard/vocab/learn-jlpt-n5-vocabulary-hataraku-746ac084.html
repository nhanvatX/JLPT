Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 働く (hataraku)

January 13, 2016   Learn Japanese N5 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N5 Vocabulary: 働く (hataraku)]
Kana: はたらく
Kanji: 働く
Romaji: hataraku

Type: verb

Meaning: to work; to labor; to labour

Example sentences:


大学を卒業したら、銀行で働きたいです。
After graduating from college, I want to work in a bank.
daigaku o sotsugyou shitara, ginkou de hatarakitai desu.

I can’t work as a lecturer yet.
