Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 今後 (kongo)

October 24, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 今後 (kongo)]

Kana: こんご
Kanji: 今後
Romaji: kongo

Type: Noun
Meaning: from now on, hereafter
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

今後、山地には山ほど雪が積もるはずだ。
There’s going to be a lot of snow in the mountains.
Kongo, sanchi ni wa yama hodo yuki ga tsumoru hazu da.

それで、今後もそういうやり方を続けるつもりなんだな？
So, do you intend to continue doing that in the future?
Sore de, kongo mo sou iu yarikata o tsudzukeru tsumori nan da na?

今後このムーブメントを広めるのは、女性たちだと思います。
I think it’s the women who will make the movement widespread.
Kongo kono muubumento o hiromeru no wa, josei-tachi da to omoimasu.

今後も医者の助言をよく聞いて、そうすればなにも心配はいりません。
Take your doctor’s advice and you won’t have to worry about anything.
Kongo mo isha no jogen o yoku kiite, sou sureba nanimo shinpai wa irimasen.

これがグローバルのローカル化で、われわれはまだその入り口にいるにすぎない。今後、大量の仕事を創出する可能性を秘めている。
kore ga guroobaru no rookaruka de, wareware wa mada sono iriguchi ni iru ni suginai. kongo, tairyou no shigoto o soushutsu suru kanousei o himete iru.
It’s the localization of the global and we’re just at the beginning of it. It has enormous potential to be very job-creating.

もう一度言いますが、入植活動についての我々の政策は変わってはおらず、今後も変わることはありません。
mou ichido iimasu ga, nyuushoku katsudou ni tsuite no wareware no seisaku wa kawatte wa orazu, kongo mo kawaru koto wa arimasen.
I want to stress to you again, that our policy on settlement activity is and will remain unchanged.

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 今後 (kongo)]

いままで優れた成績を収めてきた人間なら今後もそのような成績である可能性は高い。
Ima made sugureta seiseki o osamete kita ningen nara kongo mo sono you na seiseki de aru kanousei wa takai.
If past performance is outstanding, chances are good that performance in the future will continue at that level.

アキラもタケシも、もっと情報を開示しなければならないという点で意見が一致した。今後の展開に、ふたりは気を引き締めた。
akira mo takeshi mo, motto jouhou o kaiji shinakereba naranai to iu ten de iken ga icchi shita. kongo no tenkai ni, futari wa ki o hikishimeta.
Akira and Takeshi now agreed that more disclosure was required. They mentally braced themselves for what would follow.
***気を引き締める (ki o hikishimeru): to brace oneself, to focus one’s mind

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 以后 (yǐhòu)
Korean: 금후 (geumhu)
In French: à l’avenir
In Spanish: en lo sucesivo
In German: fortan
In Italian: avvenire
