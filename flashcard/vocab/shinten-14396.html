Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 進展 (shinten)

October 31, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 進展 (shinten)]

Kana: しんてん
Kanji: 進展
Romaji: shinten

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: progress, development
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

ソ連国内で意外な事態の進展があった。
There was an unexpected development in the Soviet Union.
Soren kokunai de igai na jitai no shinten ga atta.

今のところ、満足すべき進展が得られている。
Satisfactory progress is being made.
Ima no tokoro, manzoku subeki shinten ga erarete iru.

調査の進展具合を毎日わたしに報告してもらいたい。
I want you to report your progress to me every day.
Chousa no shinten guai o mainichi watashi ni houkoku shite moraitai.

進展状況についてはわたしのほうから委員会に報告しておく。
I will alert the committee as to the progress.
Shinten joukyou ni tsuite wa watashi no hou kara iinkai ni houkoku shite oku.

あまり具体的な進展を示せない平和プロセスに幻滅した人々のあいだに、パレスチナ国家は暴力と不屈の抵抗によって達成しうるという間違った期待をハマスは広めていた。
amari gutaiteki na shinten o shimesenai heiwa purosesu ni genmetsu shita hitobito no aida ni, paresuchina kokka wa bouryoku to fukutsu no teikou ni yotte tassei shiuru to iu machigatta kitai o hamasu wa hiromete ita.
To those disillusioned by a peace process that had failed to deliver much concrete progress, Hamas peddled the false hope that a Palestinian state could somehow be achieved through violence and uncompromising resistance.

いまこそ、信頼を構築し、時間をかけて、具体的な進展を図る時です。
ima koso, shinrai o kouchiku shi, jikan o kakete, gutaiteki na shinten o hakaru toki desu.
Now is the time to build the trust, and provide the time for substantial progress to be made.

[Ảnh: 進展 (shinten)]

その翌日、カイロで起きた進展が情勢を変えはじめた。
sono yokujitsu, kairo de okita shinten ga jousei o kaehajimeta.
The very next day a development in Cairo began to change the calculus.

私生活では心に傷を負っていた時期だったが、仕事のほうでは進展があった。
shiseikatsu de wa kokoro ni kizu o otte ita jiki datta ga, shigoto no hou de wa shinten ga atta.
Even during this traumatic period in my personal life, my career had progressed.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 进展 (jìnzhǎn)
Korean: 진전 (jinjeon)
In French: progrès
In Spanish: progreso
In German: Fortschritt
In Italian: progresso
