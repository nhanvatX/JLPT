Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 預金 (yokin)

November 30, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 預金 (yokin)]

Kana: よきん
Kanji: 預金
Romaji: yokin

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: deposit, bank account
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

あの人は、社会という銀行に大きな預金をしましたが、引き出したものはすくなかった。
ano hito wa, shakai to iu ginkou ni ooki na yokin o shimashita ga, hikidashita mono wa sukunakatta.
That person made major deposits in society’s bank, but his withdrawals have been few.

私たちが老後に備えた銀行預金の大部分が、債券市場につぎ込まれている。
watashitachi ga rougo ni sonaeta ginkou yokin no daibubun ga, saiken ichiba ni tsugikomarete iru.
A large part of the money we put aside for our old age ends up being invested in the bond market.

[Ảnh: 預金 (yokin)]

日本の銀行は政府規制の下で、預金に対して極端に低い利息しか支払わなかった。
nihon no ginkou wa seifu kisei no moto de, yokin ni tai shite kyokutan ni hikui risoku shika shiharawanakatta.
Japanese banks, under government regulation, paid extremely low interest rates on money deposited by ordinary Japanese.

この危機感を抱いた預金者たちは、極端に低い金利で預金する以外に道はなかった。
kono kikikan o idaita yokinshatachi wa, kyokutan ni hikui kinri de yokin suru igai ni michi wa nakatta.
This pool of desperate depositors had no alternative but to make deposits at very low interest rates.

各銀行には預金を下ろす客が列を作った。
Customers lined up at each bank to make deposits.
Kaku ginkou ni wa yokin o orosu kyaku ga retsu o tsukutta.

銀行で外貨預金しても金利は高いですか？
Is the interest rate high even if I deposit in a foreign currency at a bank?
Ginkou de gaika yokin shite mo kinri wa takai desu ka?

祖父はぼくに不動産や預金など、全財産を譲った。
My grandfather gave me his entire fortune, including his real estate and deposits.
Sofu wa boku ni fudousan ya yokin nado, zensaizan o yuzutta.

会社から支給されるぼくの給料は直接妻の預金口座に入金される。
My salary is sent straight to my wife’s bank account.
Kaisha kara shikyuu sareru boku no kyuuryou wa chokusetsu tsuma no yokin kouza ni nyuukin sareru.

Meaning in other languages:

Chinese: 存款 (cúnkuǎn)
Korean: 예금 (yegeum)
In French: dépôt
In Spanish: depósito
In German: Anzahlung
In Italian: deposito
