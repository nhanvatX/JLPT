Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 判明 (hanmei)

November 26, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 判明 (hanmei)]

Kana: はんめい
Kanji: 判明
Romaji: hanmei

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: establishing, proving, ascertaining, identifying, confirming
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

まったく見知らぬ人物に会って、実は数日前からの知り合いだと判明するのは妙な気分だ。
mattaku mishiranu jinbutsu ni atte, jitsuwa suujitsu mae kara no shiriai da to hanmei suru no wa myou na kibun da.
It’s a strange sensation to meet a total stranger and realize you had actually known him for several days.

ときおり、私と方向性がずれていると判明し、部下を更迭しなければならないことがある。
tokiori, watashi to houkousei ga zurete iru to hanmei shi, buka o koutetsu shinakereba naranai koto ga aru.
Now and again it turns out that a subordinate is not in harmony with me, and I have to relieve him.

[Ảnh: 判明 (hanmei)]

攻撃を受けて判明したのは、警護が最も必要な時に、リビア人同胞に対して治安維持活動を遂行する意思と能力が、彼らには致命的に欠けているということだった。
kougeki o ukete hanmei shita no wa, keigo ga motto mo hitsuyou na toki ni, ribiajin douhou ni tai shite chian iji katsudou o suikou suru ishi to nouryoku ga, karera ni wa chimeiteki ni kakete iru to iu koto datta.
As became evident during the attacks, there were fatal weaknesses in their abilities and willingness to fulfill their security duties against fellow Libyans when they were most needed.

最初の報告では一隻のはずだったドイツ巡洋艦が、その後の飛行機の報告で二隻あることが判明し、海軍本部は大事をとってベルゲン攻撃を中止した。
saisho no houkoku de wa isseki no hazu datta doitsu jun’youkan ga, sono ato no hikouki no houkoku de niseki aru koto ga hanmei shi, kaigun honbu wa daiji o totte berugen kougeki o chuushi shita.
When aircraft reported that two German cruises were there instead of one as earlier reported, the Admiralty was overcome with caution and cancelled the attack on Bergen.
