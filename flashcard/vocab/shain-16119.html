Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 社員 (shain)

November 15, 2017   Learn Japanese N2 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 社員 (shain)]

Kana: しゃいん
Kanji: 社員
Romaji: shain

Type: Noun
Meaning: company employee, company stockholders (esp. in legal contexts), members of a corporation
JLPT level: N3

Example sentences:

彼は社員にはつねに献身を期待した。
He expected devotion from his employees.
Kare wa shain ni wa tsune ni kenshin o kitai shita.

社員たちはこもごも集まって、会合のことを話し合った。
The employees gathered to discuss the meeting.
Shain-tachi wa komogomo atsumatte, kaigou no koto o hanashiatta.

ここにいる社員とはすでに長い付き合いだ。
These employees have been with me for years.
Koko ni iru shain to wa sude ni nagai tsukiaida.

この仕組みは、残業する社員をほぼゼロにした。
This system has reduced the number of overtime employees to almost zero.
Kono shikumi wa, zangyou suru shain o hobo zero ni shita.

刑法では、社員の行為について企業の立場を弁明するのは難しい。
keihou de wa, shain no koui ni tsuite kigyou no tachiba o benmei suru no wa muzukashii.
The criminal law makes it very difficult to defend corporations against the acts of their employees.

長時間労働をこなす社員に雇い主は金をじゃんじゃん浴びせるかわりに、働いているときは片時も休むことを許さない。
choujikan roudou o konasu shain ni yatoinushi wa kane o janjan abiseru kawari ni, hataraite iru toki wa katatoki mo yasumu koto o yurusanai.
The employers shower the employees with money for working long hours that but in return want every waking second of their time.

[Ảnh: 社員 (shain)]

アヤトは、大企業についてはあまりにも世間知らずだった。取締役を広報担当の社員として雇うのが異常だとは思わなかった。
ayato wa, daikigyou ni tsuite wa amari ni mo seken shirazu datta. torishimariyaku o kouhou tantou no shain toshite yatou no ga ijou da to wa omowanakatta.
Ayato was naive about large corporations, and he saw nothing remarkable in a major company hiring a member of its board of directors to work as a public-affairs spokesperson.

菊井にかかると、このプロセスはいたって簡単なように見えるが、じつはたいへんな技術と修練を必要とするので、彼と社員たちの仕事量は膨大だった。
kikui ni kakaru to, kono purosesu wa itatte kantan na you ni mieru ga, jitsu wa taihen na gijutsu to shuuren o hitsuyou to suru node, kare to shaintachi no shigoto ryou wa boudai datta.
Even though Kikui made the process look effortless, the technique and discipline underlying it actually did involve an enormous amount of work for him and his employees.
