Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 紙幣 (shihei)

December 29, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Vocabulary: 紙幣 (shihei)]

Kana: しへい
Kanji: 紙幣
Romaji: shihei

Type: Noun
Meaning: paper money, note, bill
JLPT level: N2

Example sentences:

このような制度の基本概念になじみがあるように感じるとすれば、現在の紙幣にも似たような性格があるためだ。
kono you na seido no kihon gainen ni najimi ga aru you ni kanjiru to sureba, genzai no shihei ni mo nita you na seikaku ga aru tame da.
If the basic concept seems familiar to us, it is partly because a modern banknote does similar things.

そうでないと、各国が不況と失業を回避するために、紙幣を増発して石油価格の上昇に対処しようとする危険があった。
sou de nai to, kakkoku ga fukyou to shitsugyou o kaihi suru tame ni, shihei o zouhatsu shite sekiyu kakaku no joushou ni taisho shiyou to suru kiken ga atta.
Otherwise, the danger was that countries would seek to accommodate higher oil prices by printing money.

[Ảnh: 紙幣 (shihei)]

国民に債券を売り出したため、イギリス政府には多額の現金が流れ込んだが、離れた戦地では紙幣など役に立たなかった。
kokumin ni saiken o uridashita tame, igirisu seifu ni wa tagaku no genkin ga nagarekonda ga, hanareta senchi de wa shihei nado yaku ni tatanakatta.
Selling bonds to the public had certainly raised plenty of cash for the British government, but banknotes were of little use on distant battlefields.

南部、北部の双方とも、紙幣を増刷する必要があったことは事実だ。
nanbu, hokubu no souhou tomo, shihei o zousatsu suru hitsuyou ga atta koto wa jijitsu da.
Both sides in the Civil War had to print more money, it is true.

この銀行は、２０年の満期で正貨（金貨または銀貨）で払い戻される紙幣を発行する権限を与えられていた。
The bank was licensed to issue notes payable in specie (gold or silver) for a twenty-year period.
Kono ginkou wa, nijuu nen no manki de seika (kinka matawa ginka) de haraimodosareru shihei o hakkou suru kengen o ataerarete ita.

銀行に紙幣を預けた商人たちに発行された領収証を別にすれば、カネはほとんど存在しないも当然だった。
Apart from receipts issued to merchants who deposited coin with the bank, the money had no physical existence.
Ginkou ni shihei o azuketa shounin-tachi ni hakkou sareta ryoushuushou o betsu ni sureba, kane wa hotondo sonzai shinai mo touzen datta.

南部諸州は戦争などの出費をまかなうために、裏づけのないドル紙幣を１７奥ドルほど乱発した。
The Confederate government was forced to print unbacked paper dollars to pay for the war and its other expenses, 1.7 billion dollars’ worth in all.
Nanbu shoshuu wa sensou nado no shuppi o makanau tame ni, uradzuke no nai doru shihei o juunana oku doru hodo ranpatsu shita.

南部の各州や自治体が、独自に紙幣を印刷できたこと、グレイバック紙幣は印刷が雑で、簡単に偽造できたため、ニセ札が横行したことも、状況を悪化させた。
The situation was worsened by the ability of Southern states and municipalities to print paper money of their own; and by rampant forgery, since Confederate notes were crudely made and easy to copy.
Nanbu no kakushuu ya jichitai ga, dokuji ni shihei o insatsu dekita koto, gurei bakku shihei wa insatsu ga zatsu de, kantan ni gizou dekita tame, nise-satsu ga oukou shita koto mo, joukyou o akka saseta.

In German: Banknote
