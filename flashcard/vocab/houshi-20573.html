Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 奉仕 (houshi)

December 23, 2017   Learn Japanese N1 Vocabulary

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N1 Vocabulary: 奉仕 (houshi)]

Kana: ほうし
Kanji: 奉仕
Romaji: houshi

Type: Noun, するverb
Meaning: service, ministry, attendance, church work, offering goods at a reduced price, providing a service for free
JLPT level: N1

Example sentences:

私は時折、国に奉仕し、殉職した人たちの遺族のことを考える。
I often think about the families who lost loved ones serving our country.
Watashi wa tokiori, kuni ni houshi shi, junshoku shita hito-tachi no izoku no koto o kangaeru.

兄は、自分を必要とする人たちに奉仕することに喜びを見いだす男だった。
My brother found joy in devoting his life to help those in need.
Ani wa, jibun o hitsuyou to suru hito-tachi ni houshi suru koto ni yorokobi o miidasu otoko datta.

それで、金をもうける仕事から国家を奉仕する仕事に移られたというわけですね。
So you moved from making money to serving your country.
Sore de, kane o moukeru shigoto kara kokka o houshi suru shigoto ni utsurareta to iu wake desu ne.

国家に奉仕する人々を危険な場所に送ることは、我々の国家と指導者が余儀なくされる最も困難な選択の一つである。
kokka ni houshi suru hitobito o kiken na basho ni okuru koto wa, wareware no kokka to shidousha ga yoginaku sareru motto mo konnan na sentaku no hitotsu de aru.
Sending those who serve our nation into harm’s way is one of the hardest choices our country and leaders ever have to make.

大海に守られ大陸の恩恵に恵まれたアメリカは、自国の発展に神経をそそぐことで自由という理想に最大の奉仕をし、世界の国々と人々の希望の光になることができた。
taikai ni mamorare tairiku no onkei ni megumareta amerika wa, jikoku no hatten ni shinkei o sosogu koto de jiyuu to iu risou ni saidai no houshi o shi, sekai no kuniguni to hitobito no kibou no hikari ni naru koto ga dekita.
Protected by an ocean and with the bounty of a continent, America could best serve the cause of freedom by concentrating on its own development, becoming a beacon of hope for other nations and people around the globe.

[Ảnh: 奉仕 (houshi)]

わたしは永遠の炎の前にひざまずき、短い祈りを捧げた。彼らの人生と奉仕を神に感謝し、大冒険に乗り出すにあたって知恵と力を賜るよう願った。
ưatashi wa eien no honô no mae ni hizamazuki, mijikai inori o sasageta. karera no jinsei to houshi o kami ni kansha shi, dai bouken ni noridasu ni atatte chie to chikara o tamawaru you negatta.
I knelt at the eternal flame and said a short prayer, thanking God for their lives and service and asking for wisdom and strength in the great adventures just ahead.

この悲劇を繰り返し政治的な道具として利用した人々は、わが国に奉仕した人々の犠牲を矮小化している。
kono higeki o kurikaeshi seijiteki na dougu toshite riyou shita hitobito wa, wagakuni ni houshi shita hitobito no gisei o waishouka shite iru.
Those who exploit this tragedy over and over as a political tool minimize the sacrifice of those who served our country.
