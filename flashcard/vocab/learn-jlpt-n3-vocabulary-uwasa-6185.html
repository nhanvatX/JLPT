Learn JLPT N3 Vocabulary: 噂 (uwasa)

December 4, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Vocabulary

[Hình ảnh đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]

Kana: うわさ
Kanji: 噂
Romaji: uwasa

Type: Noun; Verb (噂する); adj-no

Meaning: rumour; rumor; hearsay; gossip; talk; common talk

Example sentences:


ネットの噂って全部本当？
Are the rumors on the internet true?
netto no uwasa tte zenbu hontou.

なぜ村人の噂なんか気になさるんです？
Why should you mind what the villagers say?
naze murabito no uwasa nanka ki ni nasarun desu.

リンが、あなた様の事をいつもお噂しています！
Rin talks of you all the time, sir!
rin ga, anatasama no koto o itsumo ouwasa shite imasu.

Speak of the devil, look! Satoshi’s here!

One morning a rumor circulated through the town.
aru asa hitotsu no uwasa ga machijuu ni hiromatta.

The absurd reports have died away.

There’s a rumor she has a brother who’s bad business.

There are rumors that he has come to Nagoya in secret.

Is the rumour that you’re dating actress Jennifer true?

I’ve certainly never heard any rumor of such feeling.

You know how fast gossip gets around in Washington.

No one knows for certain, but there are a lot of strange rumors.

Once people start spreading rumors, you can’t deny it.
