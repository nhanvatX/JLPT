Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 切る (kiru)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 切る (kiru)]

Meaning: to do something completely to the end

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + 切る/切れる
