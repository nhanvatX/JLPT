Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ように (you ni)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ように (you ni)]

Meaning: in order to (e.g. meet goal); so that; hoping or wishing for something

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ように
Verb-ないform + ように
