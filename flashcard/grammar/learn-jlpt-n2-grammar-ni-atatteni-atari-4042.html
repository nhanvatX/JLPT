Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: にあたって/にあたり (ni atatte/ni atari)

August 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Hình ảnh đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]

Meaning: at the time; on the occasion of

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + にあたって(は)/にあたり
Noun + にあたって(は)/にあたり

I had two objectives when I came here today.
