Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: にかけて (ni kakete)

July 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: にかけて (ni kakete)]

Meaning: over (a period of time), through, concerning (an area of expertise)

Formation:

Noun + にかけて（は）

Download JTest4You’s N3 grammar ebook (567 pages)

Example sentences:

明日は昼から夕方にかけて雨でしょう。
Tomorrow it will rain from noon through late afternoon.
ashita wa hiru kara yuugata ni kakete ame deshou.
