Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: という (to iu)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: という (to iu)]

Meaning: called; named; that

Formation:

Noun + という + Noun
Phrase + という + Noun

Download JTest4You’s N3 grammar ebook (567 pages)

Example sentences:

これは、日本の楽器で「尺八」というものです。
This is a Japanese musical instrument called “Shakuhachi”.
kore wa, nihon no gakki de shakuhachi to iu mono desu.
