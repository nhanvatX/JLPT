Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: というと/といえば/といったら (to iuto / to ieba / to ittara)

July 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: というと/といえば/といったら (toiuto / toieba / toittara)]

Meaning: if it were the case that…, then certainly; if one were to speak of… then certainly

Formation:

Noun + というと/といえば/といったら
