Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ものだ (mono da)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ものだ (mono da) 1]

Meaning:

1. used to do something
2. of course (states an obvious fact)
3. exclamation (expresses deep emotion)

You usually use ものだ to state an obvious fact, or to say that you used to do something in the past.

Download JTest4You’s N3 grammar ebook (567 pages)

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + ものだ/もんだ: used to do something
Verb-casual, non-past + ものだ/もんだ: social norm/an obvious fact
Adjective + ものだ/もんだ: exclamation
