Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: あまり (amari)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: あまり (amari)]

Meaning: so much… that

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + あまり
Verb-casual, past + あまり
Noun + のあまり
