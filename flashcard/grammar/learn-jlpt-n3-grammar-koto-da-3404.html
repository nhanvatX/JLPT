Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ことだ (koto da)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ことだ (koto da)]

Meaning: should (used when giving suggestions or advice)

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ことだ
Verb-ないform + ことだ
