Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: いまだに (ima da ni)

August 31, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: still; even now; until this very day

Formation:

いまだに + action

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

事故であったのか、自殺であったのか、いまだに明らかになっておりません。
There wasn’t anything to show whether it was accident or suicide.
jiko de atta no ka, jisatsu de atta no ka, ima da ni akiraka ni natte orimasen.
