Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: いよいよ (iyoiyo)

August 15, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese Grammar JLPT N2 Flashcard]

Meaning: at last; finally

Formation:

いよいよ + phrase

Example sentences:

いよいよ探していたものを見つけた。
I finally found what I’ve been looking for.
iyoiyo sagashite ita mono o mitsuketa.
