Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: つつ (tsutsu)

August 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: つつ (tsutsu)]

Meaning: while; although

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + つつ (も)
