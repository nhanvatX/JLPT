Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: なり (nari)

September 1, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: なり (nari)]

Meaning: as soon as

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + なり

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

今度は横になるなりほぼ即座に寝ついていた。
That time I fell asleep almost immediately.
