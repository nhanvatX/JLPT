Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: がたい (gatai)

December 2, 2014   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: がたい (gatai)]

Meaning: hard to, difficult to

Formation:

Verb-ます stem + がたい
