Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ほど~ない (hodo~nai)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ほど~ない (hodo~nai)]

Meaning: is not as… as

Formation:

Noun + ほど + Verb-ないform
Noun + ほど + Adjective-ないform

There is nothing more cruel than talk.
