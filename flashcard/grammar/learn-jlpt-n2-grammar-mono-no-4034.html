Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ものの (mono no)

August 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ものの (mono no)]

Meaning: but; although; even though

Formation:

Verb-casual + ものの
Noun + であるものの
いadj + ものの
なadj + なものの
