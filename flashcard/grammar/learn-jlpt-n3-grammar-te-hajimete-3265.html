Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: てはじめて (te hajimete)

July 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: てはじめて (te hajimete)]

Meaning: not until; only after… did I

Formation:

Verb-てform + はじめて
