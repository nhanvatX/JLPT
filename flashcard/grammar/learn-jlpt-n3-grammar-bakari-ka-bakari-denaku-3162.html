Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ばかりか/ばかりでなく (bakari ka / bakari denaku)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ばかりか/ばかりでなく (bakari ka / bakari denaku)]

Meaning: not only… but also, as well as

Formation:

Verb-casual + ばかりか/ばかりでなく
Noun + ばかりか/ばかりでなく
いadj + ばかりか/ばかりでなく
なadj + なばかりか/なばかりでなく
