Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: っぽい (ppoi)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: っぽい (ppoi)]

Meaning: -ish, -like

Formation:

Verb-stem + っぽい
Noun + っぽい
いadj (-い) + っぽい
なadj + っぽい
