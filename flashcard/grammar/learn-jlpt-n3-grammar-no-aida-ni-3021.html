Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 間 (aida)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar
[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: の間に (no aida ni)]

Meaning: while, during (the time when)

の間 also means the “relationship” between two parties or two persons.

Formation:

Verb-casual, non-past + 間(に)
Noun + の間(に)
いadj + 間(に)
なadj + な間(に)

Example sentences:

ふたりのあいだに冷たい沈黙が流れた。
A cold silence hung between them.
futari no aida ni tsumetai chinmoku ga nagareta.
