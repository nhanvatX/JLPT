Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ろくに～ない (roku ni~nai)

August 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ろくに～ない (roku ni~nai)]

Meaning: not doing something properly

Formation:

ろくに + Verb-ないform
