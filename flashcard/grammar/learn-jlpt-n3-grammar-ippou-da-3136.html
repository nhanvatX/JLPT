Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 一方だ (ippou da)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 一方だ (ippou da)]

Meaning: more and more, continue to

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + 一方だ
