Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ばよかった (ba yokatta)

September 24, 2014   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ばよかった (ba yokatta)]

Meaning: should have; it would have been better if…, I wish…

Formation:

Verb-ば conditional + よかった（のに）

I shouldn’t have told you.
