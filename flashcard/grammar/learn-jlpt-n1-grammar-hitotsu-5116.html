Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: ひとつ (hitotsu)

October 4, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: to give something a try

Formation:

ひとつ + Verb

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

ひとつ、これをごらんになってください。
Please look here.
hitotsu, kore o goran ni natte kudasai.

Let’s see if anybody has been impertinent with you or me this time.

Try and do something about it!

We ought to rehearse tonight.
