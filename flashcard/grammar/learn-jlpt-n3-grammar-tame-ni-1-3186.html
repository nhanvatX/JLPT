Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ために (tame ni) – 1

July 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ために (tame ni) – 1]

Meaning: for, for the sake of, in order to, for the benefit of

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ため（に）
Noun + のため（に）
