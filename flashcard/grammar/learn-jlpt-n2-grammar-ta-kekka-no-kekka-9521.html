Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: た結果 / の結果 (ta kekka / no kekka)

May 26, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: た結果 / の結果 (ta kekka / no kekka)]

Meaning: as a result of; after

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + 結果
Noun + の結果
