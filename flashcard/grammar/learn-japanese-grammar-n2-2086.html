Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: くせに (kuse ni)

December 2, 2014   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: くせに (kuse ni)]

Meaning: even though, and yet, in spite of the fact that

Formation:

Verb-Casual + くせに
Noun + のくせに
なadjective + なくせに
いadjective + くせに
