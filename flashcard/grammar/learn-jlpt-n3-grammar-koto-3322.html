Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: こと (koto) – 2

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: こと (koto) – 2]

Meaning: (must) do

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + こと
Verb-ないform + こと
