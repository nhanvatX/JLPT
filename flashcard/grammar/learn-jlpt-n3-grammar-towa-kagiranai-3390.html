Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: とは限らない (to wa kagiranai)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: とは限らない (to wa kagiranai)]

Meaning: not necessarily so; is not always true

Formation:

Sentence + とは限らない
