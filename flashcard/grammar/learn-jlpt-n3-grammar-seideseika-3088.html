Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: せいで (sei de)

July 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: せいで (seide)]

Meaning: because of

Formation:

Verb-casual + せいで
Noun + のせいで
