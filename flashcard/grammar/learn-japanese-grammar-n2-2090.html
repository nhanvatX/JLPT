Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: かねない (kanenai)

December 2, 2014   Learn Japanese Grammar, Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: かねない (kanenai)]

Meaning: (someone) might do something; something might happen

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + かねない

Telling you could be dangerous.
