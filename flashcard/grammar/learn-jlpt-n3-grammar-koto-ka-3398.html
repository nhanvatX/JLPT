Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ことか (koto ka)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ことか (koto ka)]

Meaning: how…!; what…!

Formation:

Phrase + ことか

I only wish I were joking.
