Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: たとたん (ta totan)

July 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: たとたん (ta totan)]

Meaning: the moment; the instant; just (now, at the moment); as soon as

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + とたん（に）
