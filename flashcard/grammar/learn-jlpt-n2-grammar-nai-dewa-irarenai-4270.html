Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ないではいられない (nai dewa irarenai)

August 15, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ないではいられない (nai dewa irarenai)]

Meaning: can’t help but feel; can’t help but do

Formation:

Verb-ないform + ではいられない

I had to do it.
