Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ごとに (goto ni)

August 4, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ごとに (goto ni)]

Meaning: each; every; at intervals of

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ごとに
Noun + ごとに
