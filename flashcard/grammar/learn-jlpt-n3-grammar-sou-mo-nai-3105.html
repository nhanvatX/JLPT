Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: そうもない (sou mo nai)

July 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: そうもない (sou mo nai)]

Meaning: very unlikely to

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + そうもない
