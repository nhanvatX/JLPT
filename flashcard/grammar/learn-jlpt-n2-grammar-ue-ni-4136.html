Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: 上に (ue ni)

August 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 上に (ue ni)]

Meaning: as well; besides; in addition to; not only… but also

Formation:

Verb-casual + 上(に)
Noun + の + 上(に)
いadj + 上(に)
なadj + な + 上(に)
