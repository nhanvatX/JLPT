Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: なにも～ない (nanimo~nai)

August 3, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: なにも～ない (nani mo~nai)]

Meaning:

1. there’s no need to…
2. nothing; (not) ~ at all

Formation:

なにも + negative form of Verb/Adj/Noun
