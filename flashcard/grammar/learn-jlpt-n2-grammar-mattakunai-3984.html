Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: 全く～ない (mattaku~nai)

August 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: 全く～ない (mattaku~nai)]

Meaning: not… at all

Formation:

まったく + Verb-ないform
