Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: だらけ (darake)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: だらけ (darake)]

Meaning: covered all over in, full of

Formation:

Noun + だらけ
