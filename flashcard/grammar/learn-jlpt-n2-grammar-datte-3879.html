Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: だって (datte)

August 6, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: だって (datte)]

Meaning: because; but; after all; even; too

Formation:

だって + phrase: because, but
Noun + だって: even, too
