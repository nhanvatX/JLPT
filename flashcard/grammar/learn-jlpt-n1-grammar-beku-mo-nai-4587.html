Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: べくもない (beku mo nai)

September 3, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: can’t; cannot possibly be

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + べくもない
Exception: する -> すべくもない

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

それは、否定すべくもない。
No point denying it.
sore wa, hitei subeku mo nai.

Big Bang is quite famous in Japan although they don’t really compare with Arashi.

The primitive Balkan armies had no power to withstand Germany’s air and tank forces, while Britain could send them very little help.

Similar grammar:

べく (beku): for the purpose of
はずがない (hazu ga nai): cannot be
わけがない (wake ga nai): there is no way that
わけではない (wake dewa nai): it is not the case that
