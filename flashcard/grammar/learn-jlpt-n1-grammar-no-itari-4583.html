Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: の至り (no itari)

September 3, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: utmost; extremely

の至り is usually used with 光栄, 恐縮 and 感激.

Formation:

Noun + の至り

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

大統領にお目にかかれて、実に光栄の至りです。
It’s my utmost honor to be able to meet Mr. President.
daitouryou ni ome ni kakarete, jitsu ni kouei no itari desu.

I came to make some friends. But I am rather bothered at having disturbed you.
