Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ほど (hodo)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ほど (hodo)]

Meaning: to the extent that, so much… that; the more

Formation:

Verb-casual, non-past + ほど
なadj + なほど/であるほど
Noun + ほど
いadj + ほど
