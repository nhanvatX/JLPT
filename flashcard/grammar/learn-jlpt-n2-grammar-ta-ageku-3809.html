Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: たあげく (ta ageku)

August 4, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: たあげく (ta ageku)]

Meaning: to end up; in the end; finally; after all

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + あげく（に）
Noun + のあげく（に）
