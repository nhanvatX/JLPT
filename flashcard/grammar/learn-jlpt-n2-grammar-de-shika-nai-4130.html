Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: でしかない (de shika nai)

August 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: でしかない (de shika nai)]

Meaning: merely; nothing but; no more than

Formation:

Noun + でしかない

I’m just a little guy.
