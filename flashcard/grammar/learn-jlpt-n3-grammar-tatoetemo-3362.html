Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: たとえ～ても (tatoe~temo)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: たとえ～ても (tatoe~temo)]

Meaning: even if… is the case

Formation:

たとえ + Noun + でも
たとえ + Verb-てform + も
たとえ + なadj + でも
たとえ + いadj (remove い) + くても

If they had asked any question, I wouldn’t have replied.
