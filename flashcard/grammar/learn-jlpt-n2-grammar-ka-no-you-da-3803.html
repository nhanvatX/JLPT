Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: かのように (ka no you ni)

August 4, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: かのように (ka no you ni)]

Meaning: as if; just like

Formation:

Noun/なadj + であるかのように
Verb-casual + かのように + Verb
Verb-casual + かのような + Noun
Verb-casual + かのようだ
