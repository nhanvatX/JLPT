Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: よりも (yorimo)

May 13, 2017   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: よりも (yorimo)]

Meaning: in comparison to; rather than

Formation:

Verb-casual + よりも
Noun + よりも

よりも is usually used with 方が (hou ga).

Example sentences:

彼女は私よりも精神的に強くて、そんなところがうらやましいですね。
She’s much more stronger me mentally, and I envy her for that.
kanojo wa watashi yorimo seishinteki ni tsuyokute, sonna tokoro ga urayamashii desu ne.
