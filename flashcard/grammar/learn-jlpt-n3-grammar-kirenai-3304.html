Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 切れない (kirenai)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 切れない (kirenai)]

Meaning: being too many or too much to finish or complete

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + 切れない

Download JTest4You’s N3 grammar ebook (567 pages)

Example sentences:

ご飯の量が多くて、食べきれない。
I can’t finish the rice because there is too much.
gohan no ryou ga ookute, tabekirenai.
