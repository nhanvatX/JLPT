Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: える/うる (eru/uru)

August 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: える/うる (eru/uru)]

Meaning: can; is possible

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + 得る (える/うる)
