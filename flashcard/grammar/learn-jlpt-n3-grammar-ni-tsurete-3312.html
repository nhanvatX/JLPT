Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: につれ / につれて (ni tsure / ni tsurete)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: につれ / につれて (ni tsure / ni tsurete)]

Meaning: as, in proportion to, with, as… then…

You use につれ/につれて to indicate that a change occurs in accordance with another change.

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + につれ/につれて
Noun + につれ/につれて
