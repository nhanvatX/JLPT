Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: ながらに/ながらの (nagara ni/nagara no)

September 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: without change; while; during

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + ながらに/ながらの
Noun / adjective + ながらに/ながらの

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

人間は生まれながらにして平等である。
All men are created equal.
ningen wa umare nagara ni shite byoudou de aru.

This is my favorite kind of evening, whiled away to the sound of shared laughter and familiar banter.
