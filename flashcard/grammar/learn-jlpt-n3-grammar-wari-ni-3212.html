Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: わりに (wari ni)

July 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: わりに (wari ni)]

Meaning: for, despite, although, unexpectedly

You use わりに to talk about an unexpected feeling.

Formation:

Verb-casual + わりに
いadj + わりに
なadj + なわりに
Noun + のわりに
