Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: がち (gachi)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: がち (gachi)]

Meaning: apt to do, tend to do

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + がちだ
Noun + がちだ
