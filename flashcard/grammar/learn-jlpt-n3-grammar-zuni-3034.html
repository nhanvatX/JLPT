Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ずに (zuni)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ずに (zuni)]

Meaning: without doing

Formation:

Verb-ないform (remove ない) + ず(に)

Download JTest4You’s N3 grammar ebook (567 pages)

Example sentences:

左右を見ずに道を渡ると危ないです。
It’s dangerous to cross the street without looking both ways.
sayuu o mizu ni michi o wataru to abunai desu.
