Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: まるで (marude)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: まるで (marude)]

Meaning: as if; as though; just like

Formation:

まるで + Verb-casual + よう
まるで + Noun + のよう

まるで can also be used with かのよう.
