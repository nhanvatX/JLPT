Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: 折に (ori ni)

September 30, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: when; at the time; on the occasion

Formation:

Verb-casual + 折に（は）
Noun + 折に（は）

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

彼が二年前に日本へきた折に会いました。
I met him two years ago when he came to Japan.
kare ga ni’nen mae ni nihon e kita ori ni aimashita.
