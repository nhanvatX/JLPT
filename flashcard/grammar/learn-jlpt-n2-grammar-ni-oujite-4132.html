Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: に応じて (ni oujite)

August 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese Grammar JLPT N2 Flashcard]

Meaning: depending on; in accordance with

Formation:

Noun + に応じて/に応じた

Download JTest4You’s N2 grammar ebook (1,617 pages)

Example sentences:

予算に応じて、旅行のコースを決める。
We’ll plan our trip according to our funds.
yosan ni oujite, ryokou no koosu o kimeru.
