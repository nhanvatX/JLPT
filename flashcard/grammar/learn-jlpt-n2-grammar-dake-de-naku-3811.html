Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: だけでなく (dake de naku)

August 4, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese grammar: だけでなく (dake de naku)]

Meaning: not only… but also

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + だけで(は)なく
Noun + だけで(は)なく
なadj + なだけで(は)なく
いadj + だけで(は)なく
