Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: なんて (nante)

July 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: なんて (nante)]

Meaning: such as, things like

Formation:

Noun + なんて/なんか
Sentence + なんて
