Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: とか (toka)

August 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N2 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: I heard that

Formation:

Verb-casual + とか
Noun + とか
いadj + とか
なadj + とか

Download JTest4You’s N2 grammar ebook (1,617 pages)

Example sentences:

また公共料金が上がるとか。
I heard that the public utilities charge will increase again.
mata koukyou ryoukin ga agaru toka.

Similar grammar:

とか～とか (toka~toka): among other things
そうだ (sou da) – 1: people say that
ようだ (you da): it seems that
と聞いた (to kiita): I heard…
