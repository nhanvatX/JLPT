Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: しかない (shika nai)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: しかない (shika nai)]

Meaning: have no choice but

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + しかない
