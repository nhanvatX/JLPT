Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: かと思ったら/かと思うと (ka to omottara/ka to omou to)

August 6, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: かと思ったら/かと思うと (ka to omottara/ka to omou to)]

Meaning: just when; no sooner than

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + かと思ったら/かと思うと
