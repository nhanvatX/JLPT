Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: と共に (to tomo ni)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: と共に (to tomo ni)]

Meaning: together with; at the same time as; as well as

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + と共に
Noun + と共に
