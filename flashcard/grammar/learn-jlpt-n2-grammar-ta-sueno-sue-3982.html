Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: た末/の末 (ta sue/no sue)

August 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: た末/の末 (ta sue/no sue)]

Meaning: after…; as a conclusion of

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + 末（に）
Noun + の末（に）
