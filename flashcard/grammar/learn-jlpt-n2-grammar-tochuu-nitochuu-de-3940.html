Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 途中で/途中に (tochuu ni/tochuu de)

August 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 途中で/途中に (tochuu ni/tochuu de)]

Meaning: on the way; en route; in the middle of; midway

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + 途中で/途中に
Noun + の途中で/の途中に
