Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: おかげで (okage de)

July 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: おかげで (okage de)]

Meaning: thanks to A, B happens; because of A, B (something good) happens

Formation:

Verb-casual + おかげで
いadjective + おかげで
なadjective + なおかげで
Noun + のおかげで

You really helped me out. I owe you one.
