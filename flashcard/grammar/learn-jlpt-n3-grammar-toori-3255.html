July 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Hình ảnh đã lược bỏ ở chế độ HTML+CSS offline]

Japanese grammar: とおり (toori). Click on image to view full size.
Grammaire japonaise : とおり (toori). Cliquez pour voir l’image en taille réelle.

Meaning: in the same way as; in the way; as

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + とおり
Verb-casual, past + とおり
Noun + どおり/のとおり
