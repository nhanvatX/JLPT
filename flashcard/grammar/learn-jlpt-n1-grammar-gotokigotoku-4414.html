Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: ごとき/ごとく (gotoki/gotoku)

August 20, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: like; as if; the same as

Formation:

Verb-casual + ごとく
Noun + のごとく/ごとき

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

パトカーはサイレンを鳴らしてナイフのごとく車の群れを切り裂きました。
The police car siren parted the traffic like a knife.
patokaa wa sairen o narashite naifu no gotoku kuruma no mure o kirisakimashita.
