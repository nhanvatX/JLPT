Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: と同時に (to douji ni)

August 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: と同時に (to douji ni)]

Meaning: at the same time as; while

Formation:

Verb-casual, non-past + と同時に
Noun + と同時に
なadj + であると同時に

Download JTest4You’s N2 grammar ebook (1,617 pages)

When と is omitted, 同時に means coincident with, while, simultaneously.

Example sentences:

There are 37 example sentences available for this grammar point.

彼は幸福であると同時に、失望していた。
He was happy but at the same time he was disappointed.
kare wa koufuku de aru to douji ni, shitsubou shite ita.
