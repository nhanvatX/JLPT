Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 代わりに (kawari ni)

July 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 代わりに (kawari ni)]

Meaning: instead of, in exchange for, to make up for

Formation:

Verb-casual + 代わりに
Noun + の代わりに
