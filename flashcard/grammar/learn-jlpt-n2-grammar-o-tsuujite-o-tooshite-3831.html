Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: を通じて/を通して (o tsuujite / o tooshite)

August 5, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: を通じて/を通して (o tsuujite / o tooshite)]

Meaning: through; via; throughout

Formation:

Noun + を通じて/を通して
