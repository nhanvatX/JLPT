Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: 上 (jou)

August 6, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 上 (jou)]

Meaning: for the sake of; from the standpoint of; as a matter of; in terms of

Formation:

Noun + 上
