Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: に沿って (ni sotte)

August 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese Grammar JLPT N2 Flashcard]

Meaning: along with; in accordance with

Formation:

Noun + に沿って/に沿い/に沿う/に沿った

Download JTest4You’s N2 grammar ebook (1,617 pages)

Example sentences:

山道に沿って進んでいくと広い湖に着きますよ。
If you walk along the mountain road, you’ll reach the lake.
yamamichi ni sotte susunde iku to hiroi mizuumi ni tsukimasu yo.
