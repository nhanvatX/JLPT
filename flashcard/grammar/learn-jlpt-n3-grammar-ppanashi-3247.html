Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: っぱなし (ppanashi)

July 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: っぱなし (ppanashi)]

Meaning: leaving (something) on, leaving (something) still in use

Formation:

Verb-stem + っぱなし
