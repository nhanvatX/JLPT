Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: 上で (ue de)

July 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 上で (ue de)]

Meaning 1: after
Meaning 2: when, for, in order to

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + 上で: after, upon
Verb-dictionary form + 上で: when, for, in order to
Noun + 上で: after, when
