Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: げ (ge)

August 15, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: げ (ge)]

Meaning: seeming; giving the appearance of

Formation:

いadj (－い） + げに
なadj + げな
