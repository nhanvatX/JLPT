Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ことはない (koto wa nai)

July 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ことはない (koto wa nai)]

Meaning: there is no need to; (something) never happens; there is no possibility that

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ことはない
