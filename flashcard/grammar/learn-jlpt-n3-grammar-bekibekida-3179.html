Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: べき/べきだ (beki/beki da)

July 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: べき/べきだ (beki/bekida)]

Meaning: must, should, ought to

Exception: する => すべき/するべき

Download JTest4You’s N3 grammar ebook (567 pages)

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + べき/べきだ

Example sentences:

どのようにして対処すべきだろうか？
What should we do about it?
dono you ni shite taisho subeki darou ka.
