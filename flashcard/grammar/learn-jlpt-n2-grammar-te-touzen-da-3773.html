Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: て当然だ (te touzen da)

August 3, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese Grammar JLPT N2 Flashcard]

Meaning: natural; as a matter of course

Formation:

Verb-てform + 当然だ/当たり前だ
いadj （－い） + くて当然だ/て当たり前だ
なadj + で当然だ/で当たり前だ

Example sentences:

ひどいことばかり言ったので、彼女に嫌われて当然だ。
I’m not surprised that she hates me because I said a lot of nasty things.
hidoi koto bakari itta node, kanojo ni kirawarete touzen da.
