Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: たびに (tabi ni)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: たびに (tabi ni)]

Meaning: each time, every time, whenever (something happens)

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + たびに
Noun + のたびに
