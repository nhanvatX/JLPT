Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: てはいられない (tewa irarenai)

August 8, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


Meaning: can’t afford to; unable to

Formation:

Verb-てform + (は)いられない
Noun/なadj + ではいられない

I couldn’t see the children suffer.
