Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: きり (kiri)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: きり (kiri)]

Meaning: only, just, since, after

Formation:

Verb-casual + きり/っきり
Noun + きり/っきり
