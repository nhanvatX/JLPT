Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: にしろ/にせよ (ni shiro/ni seyo)

August 13, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: にしろ/にせよ (ni shiro/ni seyo)]

Meaning: even though; even if; whether… or

Formation:

Verb-casual + にしろ/にせよ
Noun + (である)にしろ/にせよ
いadj + にしろ/にせよ
なadj + (である)にしろ/にせよ
