Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: まま (mama)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: まま (mama)]

Meaning: as it is

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + まま
Verb-ないform + まま
Noun + のまま
