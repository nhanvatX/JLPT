Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: という (to iu) – 2

September 26, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: all; every

Formation:

Noun + という + (same) Noun

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

優勝したサッカーチームの選手たちの顔という顔が喜びに満ち溢れていた。
The team members’ faces beamed with joy over their victory.
yuushou shita sakkaa chiimu no senshutachi no kao to iu kao ga yorokobi ni michi afurete ita.

We can’t afford to set the police buzzing, today of all days.
