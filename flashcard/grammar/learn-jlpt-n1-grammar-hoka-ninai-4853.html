Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: ほかに～ない (hoka ni~nai)

September 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ほかに～ない (hoka ni~nai)]

Meaning: no other; nothing else

Formation:

ほかに + Verb-ないform

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)
