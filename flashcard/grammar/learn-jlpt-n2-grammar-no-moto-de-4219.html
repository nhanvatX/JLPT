Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: のもとで (no moto de)

August 13, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: のもとで (no moto de)]

Meaning: under

Formation:

Noun + のもとで/のもとに
