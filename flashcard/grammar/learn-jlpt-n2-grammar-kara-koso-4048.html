Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: からこそ (kara koso)

August 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese Grammar: からこそ (kara koso)]

Meaning: precisely because

Formation:

Verb-casual + からこそ
Noun + だからこそ
だからこそ + phrase
