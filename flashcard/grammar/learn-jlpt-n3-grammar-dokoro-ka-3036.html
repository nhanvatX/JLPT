Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: どころか (dokoro ka)

June 28, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: どころか (dokoro ka)]

Meaning: far from, anything but, let alone, much less

Formation:

Verb-casual, non-past + どころか
いadjective + どころか
なadjective + (な)どころか
Noun + どころか
