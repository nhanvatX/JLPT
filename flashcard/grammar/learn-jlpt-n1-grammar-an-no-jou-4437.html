Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: 案の定 (an no jou)

August 21, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: just as one thought; sure enough

Formation:

案の定 + phrase

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

案の定、筆記試験は難しかった。
Just as I thought, the written exam was difficult.
an no jou, hikki shiken wa muzukashikatta.
