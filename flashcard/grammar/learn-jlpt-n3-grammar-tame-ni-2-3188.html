Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: ために (tame ni) – 2

July 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ために (tame ni) – 2]

Meaning: because of, as a result of

Formation:

Verb-casual + ため（に）
Noun + のため（に）
