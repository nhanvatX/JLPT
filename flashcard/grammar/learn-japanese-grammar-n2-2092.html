Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: わけだ (wake da)

December 2, 2014   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: わけだ (wake da)]

Meaning: the fact is, for that reason, as you’d expect, as you know

Formation:

Verb-casual + わけだ
いadjective + わけだ
なadjective + なわけだ
Noun + である + わけだ
