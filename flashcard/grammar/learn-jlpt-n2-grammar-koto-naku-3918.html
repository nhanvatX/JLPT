Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ことなく (koto naku)

August 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ことなく (koto naku)]

Meaning: without doing something even once

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ことなく
