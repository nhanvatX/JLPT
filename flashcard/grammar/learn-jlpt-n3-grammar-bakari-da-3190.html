Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ばかりだ (bakari da)

July 9, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ばかりだ (bakari da)]

Meaning: continue to (similar to 一方だ)

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ばかりだ
