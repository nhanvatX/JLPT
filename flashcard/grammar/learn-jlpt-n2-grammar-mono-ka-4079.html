Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: ものか (mono ka)

August 10, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: ものか (mono ka)]

Meaning: as if

You use ものか to emphasize a determination not to do something.

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + ものか/もんか
