Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: に基づいて (ni motozuite)

August 5, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: に基づいて (ni motozuite)]

Meaning: based on; on the basis of

Formation:

Noun + に基づいて/に基づき
