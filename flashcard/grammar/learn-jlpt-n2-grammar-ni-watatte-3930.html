Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: にわたって (ni watatte)

August 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: にわたって (ni watatte)]

Meaning: throughout; over a period of

Formation:

Noun + にわたって/にわたり/にわたる/にわたった
