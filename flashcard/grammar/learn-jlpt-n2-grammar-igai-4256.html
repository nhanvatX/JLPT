Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: 以外 (igai)

August 15, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 以外 (igai)]

Meaning: with the exception of; excepting

Formation:

Noun + 以外
