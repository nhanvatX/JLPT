Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: 際に (sai ni)

August 13, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: 際に (sai ni)]

Meaning: at the time; in case of

Formation:

Verb-dictionary form + 際に/際は/際には
Verb-casual, past + 際に/際は/際には
Noun + の + 際に/際は/際には
