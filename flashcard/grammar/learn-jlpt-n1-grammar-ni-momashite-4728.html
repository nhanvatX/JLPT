Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: にもまして (ni momashite)

September 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: more than…; above…

Formation:

Noun + にもまして

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

彼女は前にもまして元気なくうなずいた。
She nodded more dejectedly than ever.
kanojo wa mae ni momashite genki naku unazuita.
