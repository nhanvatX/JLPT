Learn JLPT N1 Grammar: んばかりに (n bakari ni)

September 1, 2015   Learn Japanese N1 Grammar

[Ảnh: Learn Japanese N1 Grammar with Flashcard]

Meaning: as if; as though

Formation:

Verb-ないform (－ない) + んばかり + に/の/だ

Exception: する -> せん + ばかりに

JLPT level: N1 (一級の文法)

Download JTest4You’s N1 grammar ebook (1,640 pages)

Example sentences:

彼女は胸もはり裂けんばかりにわっと泣きくずれた。
kanojo wa mune mo harisaken bakari ni watto nakikuzureta.
She cried as though her heart would break.
