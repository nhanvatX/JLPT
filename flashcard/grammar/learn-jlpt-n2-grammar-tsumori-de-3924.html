Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: つもりで (tsumori de)

August 7, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar


Meaning: with the intention of doing

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + つもりで
Verb-dictionary form + つもりで
なadjective + なつもりで
Noun + つもりで
