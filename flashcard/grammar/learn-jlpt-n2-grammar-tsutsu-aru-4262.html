Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: つつある (tsutsu aru)

August 15, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: つつある (tsutsu aru)]

Meaning: to be doing; to be in the process of doing

Formation:

Verb-ますstem + つつある
