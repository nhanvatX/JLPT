Learn JLPT N3 Grammar: っけ (kke)

July 11, 2015   Learn Japanese N3 Grammar


[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: っけ (kke)]

Meaning: what is… again?

We use particle っけ to indicate that the speaker is trying to recall some information.

Formation:

Verb-casual, past + っけ
Noun + だっけ/だったっけ
いadj + (-い) + かったっけ
なadj + だっけ/だったっけ
