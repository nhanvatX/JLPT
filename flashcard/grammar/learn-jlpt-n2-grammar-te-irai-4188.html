Learn JLPT N2 Grammar: て以来 (te irai)

August 12, 2015   Learn Japanese N2 Grammar

[Ảnh: Japanese Grammar: て以来 (te irai)]

Meaning: since

Formation:

Verb-てform + 以来
Noun + 以来
