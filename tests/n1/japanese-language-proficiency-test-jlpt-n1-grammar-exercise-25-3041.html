New words:

商売 (shoubai): trade; business; commerce; transaction

ニーズ: needs; demand; requests

卒業試験 (sotsugyou shiken): graduation examination; graduation test

意外 (igai): unexpected; surprising

転勤 (tenkin): job transfer; job relocation

辞令 (jirei): notice of personnel change (appoinment; dismissal, etc.)

背く (somuku): to run counter to; to go against; to disobey

出来上がる (dekiagaru): to be completed; to be finished; to be ready

検討 (kentou): consideration; examination; scrutiny

優秀 (yuushuu): superiority; excellence

大雨 (ooame): heavy rain

続き (tsudzuki): sequel; continuation

最終日 (saishuubi): last day; final day

無断で (mudan de): without permission; without notice

立場 (tachiba): standpoint; position

尊重 (sonchou): respect; regard

正常 (seijou): normalcy; normality; normal

強盗 (goutou): robber; mugger; robbery; burglary
