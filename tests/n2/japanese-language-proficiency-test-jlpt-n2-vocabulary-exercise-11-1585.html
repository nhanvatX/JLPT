New words:

訂正 (teisei): correction; revision; amendment

変換 (henkan): change; conversion; transformation

整備 (seibi): servicing; putting in place; provision

改善 (kaizen): betterment; improvement

日記 (nikki): diary; journal

初歩 (shoho): basics; elements

素人 (shirouto): amateur; layman; novice

初級 (shokyuu): elementary level

純情 (junjou): pure heart; innocence; naivete

疑わしい (utagawashii): doubtful; questionable; uncertain

目つき (metsuki): look; expression of the eyes; eyes

事前 (jizen): prior; beforehand; in advance

開始 (kaishi): start; commencement; beginning

最終 (saishuu): last; final; closing

打ち合わせ (uchiawase): advance arrangements; preparatory meeting; briefing session

お問い合わせ (otoiawase): enquiry; inquiry

ご遠慮ください (goenryo kudasai): please refrain from ~

対応 (taiou): interaction; correspondence; coping with; dealing with

批判 (hihan): criticism; judgement; comment

付き合い (tsukiai): association; socializing

交流 (kouryuu): (cultural) exchange; interchange; interaction

月日 (tsukihi): time; years; days

交際 (kousai): company; friendship; association; acquaintance

みずから: oneself; for one’s self; personally

立ち上げる (tachiageru): to start (something); to start up; to launch (a business)

しばらくして: after a short time; presently

回復 (kaifuku): restoration; rehabilitation; recovery

実施 (jisshi): enforcement; implementation; putting into practice

交通事故 (koutsuu jiko): traffic accident

減らす (herasu): to abate; to decrease; to diminish

有効 (yuukou): valid; effective

制限 (seigen): restriction; restraint; limitation; limit
